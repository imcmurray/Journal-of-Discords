# TOPIC: Racial Teachings
## Teachings on Race, Curse of Cain, and Interracial Marriage

---

## Overview

Early LDS prophets taught that Black people bore the "curse of Cain," were less valiant in the pre-existence, and were denied priesthood and temple blessings as a result. This topic documents the systematic racial theology preached from LDS pulpits and published in official records for over a century.

**What This Topic Covers:**
- The theological framework identifying Black people as "seed of Cain" cursed with specific physical features
- The claim that Black people exist to serve as "the devil's representation upon the earth"
- Death penalty declarations for interracial marriage presented as "the law of God"
- Priesthood and temple restrictions lasting 130+ years (1849-1978)
- Complete institutional disavowal in 2013, without apology or full accountability

**Why This Matters:**
- Taught by Church Presidents (Brigham Young, John Taylor) as divine revelation
- Sustained as institutional policy for 130+ years
- Affected millions of Black Latter-day Saints and their families
- Used specific theological justifications (curse of Cain, pre-mortal unworthiness, satanic representation)
- Declared as eternal divine law ("This will always be so")
- Completely disavowed by 2013 church essay
- Raises fundamental questions about prophetic authority and institutional accountability
- Affects living people: Black members, former members, and interracial families

**Institutional Pattern:**
This was not one prophet's opinion. Multiple prophets and apostles taught these doctrines from the 1850s through the 1970s. The teachings were published in official records, implemented as institutional policy, and defended as God's will. The pattern demonstrates institutional racism taught as divine revelation.

## Cross-Reference Connections

### How These Three Findings Connect

These three sermons (1859, 1863, 1881) form a coherent system of racist theology spanning 22 years across two Church Presidents. They are not isolated incidents but evidence of institutional doctrine.

#### 1. Confirmation Pattern: Multiple Prophets, Same Doctrine

**What this means:** When multiple Church Presidents teach the same doctrine across decades, it cannot be dismissed as "one man's opinion."

**The pattern:**
- **1859 (Brigham Young)**: Physical features (flat nose, black skin) are divine curse for Cain's murder
- **1863 (Brigham Young)**: "Death on the spot" for interracial marriage as "the law of God"
- **1881 (John Taylor)**: God deliberately preserved Cain's curse through Ham's wife to provide "the devil's representation upon the earth"

**Why it matters:**
- Two separate Church Presidents (BY + JT)
- Consistent over 22+ years
- Both published in official Journal of Discourses
- Both taught from the pulpit with full prophetic authority
- Forms the theological justification for 130+ years of priesthood/temple ban

**This destroys the apologetic:** "He was speaking as a man, not a prophet." When two prophets teach the same doctrine across decades and it's implemented as institutional policy, that's institutional revelation - not personal opinion.

#### 2. Escalation Pattern: Rhetoric Intensifying Over Time

**What this means:** The rhetoric didn't soften over time; it hardened and became more explicit.

**The timeline:**
- **1859**: Physical features as curse + "uncouth, uncomely, disagreeable"
- **1863**: Death penalty declared as divine law + "This will always be so"
- **1881**: Explicit identification as "the devil's representation upon the earth"

**Notice:**
- 1859: Dehumanizing language about appearance and intelligence
- 1863: Violence language (execution for interracial marriage)
- 1881: Satanic identification (cosmic role as devil's agents)

Each sermon builds on previous teachings, adding theological weight and specificity. By 1881, John Taylor provides systematic theodicy: God deliberately designed Black people to oppose His work.

**Why this matters:**
- Shows institutional commitment, not casual remarks
- Demonstrates deepening theological investment in racism
- Makes later disavowal more devastating (the church wasn't drifting away from racism; it was building elaborate theology to justify it)

#### 3. Authority-Error Connection: "Law of God" Now Disavowed

**What this means:** These weren't presented as theories or speculation - they were declared as divine law with absolute certainty.

**The divine authority claims:**

**Production notes:**
- Style: Black gospel/spiritual

**Song status:** HIGH PRIORITY for development

- Specified sequential order: "all the other descendants of Adam" must receive it first

- Demonstrates prophetic failure on racial doctrine

- Both were defended as eternal principles then reversed/disavowed
- Both created generational harm through institutional policy

- Used divine authority language: "decree," "cannot alter," "God's will"
- 2013 "Race and the Priesthood" essay disavows but doesn't explain how prophets could be so wrong
