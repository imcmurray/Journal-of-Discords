# TOPIC: Authority Claims
## Prophetic Authority and Reliability Claims

---

## Overview

Early LDS prophets made strong claims about their own authority, the reliability of their teachings, and the binding nature of their sermons. These claims become significant when the teachings themselves are falsified or disavowed.

**Why This Matters:**
- Sermons declared "as good as Scripture"
- "Thus saith the Lord" claims attached to false teachings
- "Follow the prophet" doctrine creates accountability
- Authority claims + errors = authority problem

---

## Key Findings

### Sermons as Scripture

#### Finding 1: "As Good Scripture as Is Couched in This Bible"
**Source:** JoD 13:95 (January 2, 1870)

> "I say now, when they [the sermons] are copied and approved by me they are as good Scripture as is couched in this Bible, and if you want to read revelation read the sayings of him who knows the mind of God."
> — Brigham Young

**Category:** SELF-CONTRA (when paired with errors)

**Context:** Brigham was endorsing the Journal of Discourses as authoritative.

**Why It Matters:**
- JoD sermons are claimed to be Scripture
- Scripture contains sun inhabitants, Adam-God, blood atonement, etc.
- Either JoD is Scripture (including errors) or this claim is false
- Either way, prophetic reliability is undermined

**Song Status:** 📎 **Supporting Material** (strengthens other songs)

### Infallibility Adjacent Claims

#### Finding 3: "Never Led Astray"
**Source:** Wilford Woodruff (1890) and subsequent teachings

> "The Lord will never permit me or any other man who stands as President of this Church to lead you astray."
> — Wilford Woodruff

**Category:** SELF-CONTRA

**Context:** Often quoted in modern church. Creates problem when past presidents clearly did teach things now rejected.

**Connection:** If Brigham taught Adam-God and it was wrong, was that "leading astray"?

## The Authority Problem

### The Structure of the Problem:

1. **Strong Authority Claim:** "As good Scripture as is couched in this Bible"
2. **Specific Teaching:** Sun is inhabited, Adam is God, blood atonement, etc.
3. **Teaching Falsified/Disavowed:** Scientific evidence, modern church rejection
4. **Therefore:** Either the authority claim is false, or Scripture contains errors

### The Trilemma:

When a prophet says "Thus saith the Lord" about something that's wrong:

**Option A:** The prophet was speaking as a man, not as prophet
- *Problem:* How do we distinguish? No mechanism provided.

**Option B:** The prophet was wrong even when speaking as prophet
- *Problem:* Undermines all prophetic teaching.

**Option C:** The teaching isn't actually wrong
- *Problem:* Evidence clearly shows it is (sun inhabitants, etc.)

## Apologetic Responses

### Defense 1: "Prophets Are Human"

**What They Say:** Prophets make mistakes. They're not infallible.

**Why It Fails:**
1. This defense contradicts "never led astray"
2. If prophets err, how do we know when to trust them?
3. No mechanism provided for distinguishing revelation from error
4. Members are told to "follow the prophet" without qualification

---

### Defense 2: "Speaking as a Man vs. Prophet"

**What They Say:** Not everything a prophet says is doctrine.

**Why It Fails:**
1. General Conference = official teaching setting
2. "Thus saith the Lord" = explicit doctrine claim
3. Temple teaching = highest authority level
4. If even these can be error, nothing is reliable
5. No contemporaneous way to distinguish was ever provided

---

### Defense 3: "Line Upon Line"

**What They Say:** Understanding develops over time; earlier prophets had incomplete understanding.

**Why It Fails:**
1. "No question of it" isn't tentative
2. "This will always be so" isn't developmental
3. Wrong is different from incomplete
4. Adam-God isn't an incomplete version of a true doctrine

---

### Defense 4: "The Church Is True, Not the People"

**What They Say:** The institution is divinely led even if individuals err.

**Why It Fails:**
1. The institution taught these doctrines officially
2. Temple ceremonies aren't individual opinions
3. General Conference isn't personal speculation
4. Policy lasted 130+ years (racial ban)
