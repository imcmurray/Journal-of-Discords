# TOPIC: Adam-God Doctrine
## The Teaching That Adam Is God the Father

---

## Overview

The Adam-God doctrine is arguably the single strongest example of prophetic error in LDS history. Brigham Young taught clearly and repeatedly that Adam (Michael the Archangel) is actually God the Father—the father of our spirits and the literal father of Jesus Christ in the flesh.

**Why This Matters:**
- Taught in General Conference (most authoritative setting)
- Taught in the temple endowment (most sacred setting)
- Taught consistently over decades
- Now completely rejected by the modern church
- Creates an impossible trilemma for prophetic authority

---

## Key Findings

### The General Conference Declaration

#### Finding 1: "Adam Is Our Father and Our God"
**Source:** JoD 1:50-51 (April 9, 1852) — General Conference

> "Now hear it, O inhabitants of the earth, Jew and Gentile, Saint and sinner! When our father Adam came into the garden of Eden, he came into it with a celestial body, and brought Eve, one of his wives, with him. He helped to make and organize this world. He is MICHAEL, the Archangel, the ANCIENT OF DAYS! about whom holy men have written and spoken—He is our FATHER and our GOD, and the only God with whom WE have to do."
> — Brigham Young, JoD 1:50 (April 9, 1852)

**Category:** DISAVOWED

**Context:** 
- General Conference address (most authoritative setting)
- "Now hear it, O inhabitants of the earth" — prophetic proclamation formula
- Caps on "MICHAEL," "ANCIENT OF DAYS," "FATHER," "GOD" in original
- Eve described as "one of his wives" (plural)

**Why It Matters:**
- Cannot be dismissed as "speculation" — this is prophetic declaration
- General Conference is binding on the church
- Modern church explicitly rejects this teaching
- If the prophet was wrong here, how do we know when he's right?

## The Doctrinal Problem

### What Brigham Taught:
1. Adam = Michael the Archangel = God the Father
2. Adam came to Eden with a celestial (resurrected) body
3. Adam brought Eve as one of his celestial wives
4. Adam is "the only God with whom WE have to do"
5. Adam is the literal father of Jesus Christ in the flesh

### What the Church Now Teaches:
1. God the Father (Heavenly Father) and Adam are separate beings
2. Adam was created, not pre-existent as God
3. Eve was created from Adam's rib, not brought as a celestial wife
4. Heavenly Father is the God with whom we have to do
5. Heavenly Father is the father of Jesus Christ

### The Contradiction:
These positions are mutually exclusive. Either:
- Brigham was right and the modern church is apostate
- Brigham was wrong and prophets can teach fundamental falsehoods as doctrine
- Both are somehow true (logically impossible)

## Apologetic Responses

### Defense 1: "We Don't Fully Understand What He Meant"

**What They Say:** Brigham's teaching is complex and we may be misinterpreting it.

**Why It Fails:**
1. "Now hear it, O inhabitants of the earth" is unambiguous proclamation
2. "He is our FATHER and our GOD" is definitional statement
3. The teaching was clear enough to include in the temple
4. If it was unclear, why teach it in General Conference?

**Source Rebuttal:**
> "He is our FATHER and our GOD, and the only God with whom WE have to do."

This is not ambiguous.

---

### Defense 2: "He Was Speaking as a Man"

**What They Say:** Not everything a prophet says is doctrine. Brigham was speculating.

**Why It Fails:**
1. General Conference is the setting for official doctrine
2. "Now hear it, O inhabitants of the earth" is prophetic declaration
3. It was taught in the temple — the most sacred setting
4. If this was "speaking as a man," how do we identify "speaking as prophet"?

---

### Defense 3: "It's a Mystery We Don't Understand"

**What They Say:** Some doctrines are beyond our current understanding.

**Why It Fails:**
1. The modern church explicitly rejects it, not suspends judgment
2. "Heavenly Father and Adam are separate beings" is current doctrine
3. You can't have it both ways — either Adam is God or he isn't
4. This defense admits the teaching but avoids the implications

---

### Defense 4: "Later Prophets Clarified"

**What They Say:** Subsequent revelation superseded this teaching.

**Why It Fails:**
1. No revelation superseding it has been published
2. The church simply stopped teaching it
3. If prophets can be superseded, current prophets can be too
4. This admits Brigham was wrong but avoids saying so
