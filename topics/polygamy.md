# TOPIC: Polygamy
## Plural Marriage as Eternal Requirement and the Reversal

---

## Overview

Early LDS prophets taught that plural marriage (polygamy) was an eternal principle required for exaltation in the highest degree of the celestial kingdom. They declared the church would never abandon it. In 1890, the church officially ended the practice.

**Why This Matters:**
- Taught as essential for exaltation (highest salvation)
- Leaders vowed it would never be abandoned
- 1890 Manifesto reversed the practice
- D&C 132 remains in canon
- Creates clear self-contradiction

**Source:** JoD 25:309 (September 1884)

### D&C 132 Connection

#### Finding 5: Revelation Still Canon
**Source:** Doctrine and Covenants 132

D&C 132 (the revelation on plural marriage) remains in the LDS scriptural canon despite the practice being abandoned.

**Key Verses:**
- v.4: "if ye abide not that covenant, then are ye damned"
- v.6: "as pertaining to the new and everlasting covenant, it was instituted for the fulness of my glory"
- v.52: Emma commanded to "receive all those that have been given unto my servant Joseph"

**Why It Matters:**
- The revelation commanding polygamy is still Scripture
- The practice is forbidden but the scripture remains
- Creates tension between canon and practice

## The Doctrinal Problem

### What Was Taught:
1. Polygamy required for exaltation ("become Gods")
2. Abandoning polygamy = abandoning the Gospel
3. The church will never give it up
4. D&C 132 is eternal revelation

### What Happened:
1. 1890 Manifesto ended the practice
2. Post-Manifesto polygamists were excommunicated
3. Modern church requires monogamy for temple marriage
4. D&C 132 still in canon but reinterpreted

### The Contradiction:
- "Only men who enter into polygamy" become Gods → Now can become Gods without it
- "Never give it up" → Gave it up
- "Do away with that = do away with religion" → Did away with that, kept religion

## Apologetic Responses

### Defense 1: "Continuing Revelation"

**What They Say:** The 1890 Manifesto was revelation superseding previous teaching.

**Why It Fails:**
1. The Manifesto doesn't claim to be revelation
2. It says the church is "not teaching" polygamy — not that it's doctrinally wrong
3. D&C 132 is still canon
4. If prophets can be superseded, trust is undermined

---

### Defense 2: "Government Pressure"

**What They Say:** The church had to stop due to federal pressure (Edmunds-Tucker Act).

**Why It Fails:**
1. This admits the change was pragmatic, not revelatory
2. Prophets said they'd never yield to pressure
3. God's eternal law changed due to government?
4. Undermines the "continuing revelation" defense

---

### Defense 3: "Celestial Polygamy Continues"

**What They Say:** Polygamy in eternity is still doctrine (men sealed to multiple wives).

**Why It Fails:**
1. Earthly practice was what was required for exaltation
2. "Enter into polygamy" was not about celestial sealing only
3. Modern members don't practice polygamy
4. This defense actually highlights the inconsistency

---

### Defense 4: "We Don't Fully Understand"

**What They Say:** The doctrine is complex and eternal principles may differ from temporal practice.

**Why It Fails:**
1. "Only men who enter into polygamy become Gods" is clear
2. The prophets understood it clearly enough to require it
3. "Never give it up" is not ambiguous
4. This defense admits the problem without solving it
