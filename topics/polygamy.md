# TOPIC: Polygamy
## Plural Marriage as Eternal Requirement and the Reversal

---

## Overview

Early LDS prophets taught that plural marriage (polygamy) was an eternal principle required for exaltation in the highest degree of the celestial kingdom. They declared the church would never abandon it. In 1890, the church officially ended the practice.

**Why This Matters:**
- Taught as essential for exaltation (highest salvation)
- Leaders vowed it would never be abandoned
- 1890 Manifesto reversed the practice
- D&C 132 remains in canon
- Creates clear self-contradiction

## Key Findings

### Required for Exaltation

#### Finding 1: No Exaltation Without Plural Marriage
**Source:** JoD 11:269 (August 19, 1866)
**Analysis:** `volumes/vol-11/findings/1866-08-19-only-men-become-gods.md`

> "The only men who become Gods, even the Sons of God, are those who enter into polygamy. Others attain unto a glory and may even be permitted to come into the presence of the Father and the Son; but they cannot reign as kings in glory, because they had blessings offered unto them, and they refused to accept them."
> — Brigham Young, JoD 11:269

**Additional Key Quotes:**
- "You will be polygamists at least in your faith, or you will come short of enjoying the salvation and the glory which Abraham has obtained. This is as true as that God lives." (JoD 11:269)
- "I know it is from God—I know that he revealed it from heaven; I know that it is true, and understand the bearings of it and why it is." (JoD 11:270)
- "If we are not admitted [to statehood] until then [denying polygamy], we shall never be admitted." (JoD 11:270)

**Category:** PRIMARY: CONTRA-MODERN / SECONDARY: DISAVOWED, SELF-CONTRA, FAILED-PROPH

**Context:** August 19, 1866 sermon in the Bowery, Salt Lake City. Six years after polygamy called "twin relic of barbarism" with slavery. Post-Civil War, pre-enforcement of Morrill Anti-Bigamy Act. Brigham defending polygamy against federal pressure while teaching it as absolute requirement for exaltation.

**Why It Matters:**
- **Categorical absolute:** "ONLY" men who enter polygamy become Gods—no exceptions
- **Ultimate authority:** "As true as that God lives"—strongest possible prophetic claim
- **Belief requirement:** Must be "polygamists at least in your faith"—even believing it's not required is condemned
- **Failed prophecy:** "We shall never be admitted [without denying it]"—they denied it and were admitted (1896)
- **Modern contradiction:** Monogamous temple couples receive same exaltation promises Brigham said require polygamy
- **D&C 132 still canon:** The revelation commanding polygamy remains scripture despite practice abandonment

**Song Status:** ⭐ **HIGHEST PRIORITY—FLAGSHIP CANDIDATE** (★★★★★ rating)

**Song Development Notes:**
- Perfect hook: "The only men who become Gods are those who enter into polygamy"
- Self-condemning structure: Brigham's standard → Modern practice fails it
- Multiple song-worthy elements (categorical claim, authority claim, failed prophecy, reversal)
- Visual irony: Modern monogamous temple couples vs. Brigham's "ONLY polygamists"
- No reasonable apologetic defense ("only" is categorical, "as true as God lives" is absolute)

#### Finding 2: Monogamy Is a System Established by Robbers
**Source:** JoD 9:322-323 (July 6, 1862)
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Analysis:** `volumes/vol-09/findings/1862-07-06-monogamy-robbery.md`

**Primary Quote:**
> "Thus this monogamic order of marriage, so esteemed by modern Christians as a holy sacrament and divine institution, is nothing but a system established by a set of robbers."
> — Brigham Young, JoD 9:322-323

**Additional Key Quotes:**
- "Monogamy, or restrictions by law to one wife, is no part of the economy of Heaven among men." (JoD 9:322)
- "[Polygamy] is the only popular religion there [in heaven], for this is the religion of Abraham, and, unless we do the works of Abraham, we are not Abraham's seed and heirs according to promise." (JoD 9:323)
- "The Congress of the United States have lately passed a law to punish polygamy... In doing this, they have undertaken to dictate the Almighty in his revelations to his people, and those who hand out insult to the Great I Am, in the end, are apt to get more than they have spoken for." (JoD 9:323)

**Category:** PRIMARY: CONTRA-MODERN / SECONDARY: IRONIC, FAILED-PROPH

**Context:** Delivered in **July 1862**, the exact same month Congress passed the Morrill Anti-Bigamy Act (signed July 8, 1862). This sermon was Brigham's direct theological counterpunch to the first federal law criminalizing polygamy in U.S. territories. Brigham argued monogamy originated with Roman "wandering brigands" who stole Sabine women.

**Why It Matters:**
- **Absolute condemnation:** Monogamy is "nothing but" a robbers' system (categorical rejection)
- **Theological claim:** "No part of the economy of Heaven among men" (eternal divine order)
- **Salvation requirement:** "Unless we do the works of Abraham" (polygamy tied to exaltation)
- **"Only popular religion" in heaven:** Polygamy exclusively accepted in celestial kingdom
- **Divine punishment threatened:** Congress "insulting the Almighty" will "get more than they spoke for"
- **28 years later:** Church capitulated (1890 Manifesto), didn't receive divine protection
- **Modern reversal:** Church now requires monogamous temple marriage for exaltation
- **Total contradiction:** Every modern LDS couple practices what Brigham called "robbers' system"

**Song Status:** ★★★★★ **PREMIERE MATERIAL** — One of the most quotable, devastating statements in entire JoD corpus

### Church Will Never Abandon

#### Finding 3: "Never Given to Another People"
**Source:** JoD 25:271 (August 31, 1884)
**Speaker:** George Q. Cannon (First Presidency)
**Analysis:** `volumes/vol-25/findings/1884-08-31-never-another-people.md`

**Primary Quote:**
> "God has given unto us the assurance that this Church, this work that He has established, shall never be given into the hands of another people, but that it shall stand forever, and it shall go forward accomplishing His designs, until it shall fill the whole earth."
> — George Q. Cannon, JoD 25:271

**Additional Key Quotes:**
- "Men may be slain, as they have been; people may be driven, as they have been; efforts of the most herculean character may be made to extirpate this work from the earth, but we have the promise of our God that it shall stand and that it shall not be overthrown." (JoD 25:271)
- "Looking at affairs naturally, however, it would seem as though it was presumptuous in a people like us to entertain such hopes. How often have we been told that in a very little while the opposition to this work would be of such a character that it would completely overwhelm it." (JoD 25:271)
- "For the day will come, and it is not far distant, when in our own nation, there will be civil strife, there will be domestic broils, there will be a withdrawal of peace... God revealed this and predicted it, upwards of fifty years ago, and it will, just as sure as He predicted it, be fulfilled to the letter." (JoD 25:276)

**Category:** PRIMARY: FAILED-PROPH / SECONDARY: IRONIC, CONTRA-MODERN

**Context:** Delivered at Tabernacle, Salt Lake City, **six years before the 1890 Manifesto**. Cannon was First Counselor in First Presidency during intense federal persecution (Edmunds Act 1882, pending Edmunds-Tucker Act 1887). "The work" being defended was centrally polygamy — the persecution he references was polygamy raids.

**Why It Matters:**
- **Absolute promise:** "Shall NEVER be given" to another people (no conditions, no exceptions)
- **Divine assurance:** "We have the promise of our God"
- **Stand forever:** Suggests no fundamental changes to church structure/doctrine
- **"The work" in 1884:** Polygamy was the defining practice under attack
- **Six years later:** Manifesto fundamentally altered "this work"
- **The great irony:** Which is the "other people"?
  - **Fundamentalists (FLDS, AUB, etc.):** Practice 1884 doctrine, excommunicated as "another people"
  - **Modern mainstream LDS:** Changed fundamental doctrines, became different from 1884 church
- **Failed prophecy:** Opposition DID overwhelm them (from 1884 perspective); federal pressure forced change
- **Constitutional prophecy failed:** "Not far distant" civil strife predicting Saints would save Constitution — 140+ years later, still waiting

**Song Status:** ★★★★★ **CRITICAL TIER** — Perfect built-in irony: "Who are the 'other people'?"

#### Finding 4: "We Cannot Tone It Down"
**Source:** JoD 25:309 (October 6-7, 1884)
**Speaker:** John Taylor (Church President)
**Analysis:** `volumes/vol-25/findings/1884-10-06-cannot-tone-down.md`

**Primary Quote:**
> "God has given us a revelation in regard to celestial marriage. I did not make it. He has told us certain things pertaining to this matter, and they would like us to tone that principle down and change it and make it applicable to the views of the day. This we cannot do; nor can we interfere with any of the commands of God to meet the persuasions or behests of men. I cannot do it, and will not do it."
> — John Taylor, JoD 25:309

**Additional Key Quotes:**
- "We will stand by the principles of eternal truth; living we will proclaim them, and dying we will be true to them, and after death will live again in their enjoyment in the eternal worlds. That is my feeling; so I don't feel very trembly in the knees, and I do not think you do, generally... and all who sanction it say Amen—[the vast congregation responded with a loud 'Amen.']" (JoD 25:309)
- "We have also been told that 'it is not mete that men who will not abide my law shall preside over my Priesthood,' and yet some people would like very much to do it. Well, they cannot do it." (JoD 25:309, referencing D&C 132)
- "Some folks think that we polygamists are very much indebted to our brethren who are monogamists to help to steady the ark... but if God is with us, we ask no odds of the world, for He governs the destinies of the human family." (JoD 25:310)
- "People want to slip round a corner, or creep out in some way. There is something very creepy about it. There was a man in former times we are told, came to Jesus by night. His name was Nicodemus... Well, there is not much to such folks." (JoD 25:309-310)

**Category:** PRIMARY: FAILED-PROPH / SECONDARY: IRONIC, SELF-CONTRA, DISAVOWED, MORAL-PROBLEM

**Context:** Delivered at **General Conference, October 6-7, 1884** — exactly **5 years, 11 months, 18 days** before the 1890 Manifesto. Church President speaking from Tabernacle pulpit with formal congregational ratification ("Amen" vote). Taylor went underground 4 months later (February 1885) and died in hiding defending polygamy (July 1887). His successor Wilford Woodruff issued the Manifesto three years after Taylor's death.

**Why It Matters:**
- **Absolute language:** "We CANNOT do" (theological impossibility, not mere preference)
- **Divine commands:** "Commands of God" presented as unchangeable by humans
- **Presidential authority:** Church President declaring institutional position at General Conference
- **Congregational ratification:** "Vast congregation" formally sustained with "Amen" the commitment to never compromise
- **Personal commitment:** "I cannot do it, and will not do it" — Taylor kept this promise (died in hiding 1887)
- **Institutional betrayal:** Church DID tone it down six years later (Manifesto 1890)
- **Self-contradiction:** Taylor mocked those who "slip round a corner... like Nicodemus," then went underground 4 months later
- **Priesthood requirement:** "Men who will not abide my law shall not preside" — modern leaders don't practice plural marriage
- **Modern reversal:** The monogamists Taylor mocked became the model for modern church
- **Theological crisis:** If 1884 "Amen" was inspired, 1890 surrender contradicts God; if 1890 was inspired, 1884 was rebellion

**The Timeline:**
- **October 6-7, 1884:** "We cannot tone it down"
- **February 1, 1885:** Taylor goes underground (4 months later)
- **July 25, 1887:** Taylor dies in hiding, still faithful (2 years, 9 months later)
- **September 24, 1890:** Manifesto issued (5 years, 11 months, 18 days after sermon)

**Song Status:** ★★★★★ **PLATINUM TIER** — Perfect hook, highest authority, congregational ratification, Taylor's personal tragedy (died true, church didn't), six-year timeline to complete reversal

### The Impossible Contradiction

**The Logic Problem:**

1. **If Brigham was right** (polygamy required for exaltation):
   - Modern monogamous couples CANNOT become Gods
   - Modern church teaches false doctrine
   - Fundamentalists (FLDS) are correct

2. **If modern church is right** (monogamy leads to exaltation):
   - Brigham was wrong about salvation requirement
   - "I KNOW it is from God" was false
   - "As true as God lives" was false
   - Prophetic authority fails

3. **If "continuing revelation" changed it:**
   - Taylor was wrong: "We CANNOT interfere with commands of God" (but they did)
   - God changed "eternal truth" based on government pressure
   - Undermines meaning of "eternal principle"
   - What else might change? (Current doctrines on LGBTQ+, women's roles?)

4. **If Manifesto was surrender, not revelation:**
   - Church capitulated to "persuasions and behests of men" (exactly what Taylor said they couldn't do)
   - Divine promises failed (Cannon: "God will fight their battles")
   - Prophecies failed (Brigham: "never admitted" without denying it)

**No Matter Which Position:**
- **Prophetic certainty was misplaced** ("I KNOW," "as true as God lives," "we CANNOT")
- **Divine promises failed** ("never given to another people," "shall stand forever")
- **Congregational ratification was wrong** (1884 "Amen" to never change → 1890 "Amen" to change)
- **D&C 132 remains canonical** (revelation commanding polygamy still scripture)

## Apologetic Responses

### Defense 1: "Continuing Revelation"

**What They Say:** The 1890 Manifesto was revelation superseding previous teaching.

**Why It Fails:**
1. The Manifesto doesn't claim to be revelation
2. It says the church is "not teaching" polygamy — not that it's doctrinally wrong
3. D&C 132 is still canon
4. If prophets can be superseded, trust is undermined

---

### Defense 2: "Government Pressure"

**What They Say:** The church had to stop due to federal pressure (Edmunds-Tucker Act).

**Why It Fails:**
1. This admits the change was pragmatic, not revelatory
2. Prophets said they'd never yield to pressure
3. God's eternal law changed due to government?
4. Undermines the "continuing revelation" defense

---

### Defense 3: "Celestial Polygamy Continues"

**What They Say:** Polygamy in eternity is still doctrine (men sealed to multiple wives).

**Why It Fails:**
1. Earthly practice was what was required for exaltation
2. "Enter into polygamy" was not about celestial sealing only
3. Modern members don't practice polygamy
4. This defense actually highlights the inconsistency

---

### Defense 4: "We Don't Fully Understand"

**What They Say:** The doctrine is complex and eternal principles may differ from temporal practice.

**Why It Fails:**
1. "Only men who enter into polygamy become Gods" is clear
2. The prophets understood it clearly enough to require it
3. "Never give it up" is not ambiguous
4. This defense admits the problem without solving it
