## Core Concept

Wilford Woodruff, future President of the Church, taught that any scientific knowledge should be abandoned if the Prophet contradicts it. This isn't about faith informing reason — it's about authority overriding evidence. The song connects this doctrine to the prophetic claims that have failed the test of science: inhabited suns, young earth, anti-evolution. When prophets can override facts, what happens when the facts prove the prophet wrong?

---

## Key Quotes

### The Hook

> "If the Prophet of God should tell me that a certain principle or theory which I might have learned was not true, I do not care what my ideas might have been, I should consider it my duty, at the suggestion of my file leader, to abandon that principle or theory."
> — JoD 5:83

### Supporting Quote 1

> "Whatever I might have obtained in the shape of learning, by searching and study respecting the arts and sciences of men—whatever principles I may have imbibed during my scientific researches..."
> — JoD 5:83

### Supporting Quote 2

> "I have seen men in the days of Joseph bring up principles, and read, and teach, and advocate theories, when the Prophet would say, 'It is not right to do so: they are not true.' Those men would still argue, maintain their position..."
> — JoD 5:83

### Supporting Quote 3

> "The very moment a man does that, he crosses the path of the servant of God who is set to lead the way to life and salvation."
> — JoD 5:83

### Supporting Quote 4

> "We cannot close up our minds and say that we will go so far and no farther. This we cannot do without jeopardising our standing before God."
> — JoD 5:84

## Structural Ideas

### Option A: The Test of Time
- Verse 1: Woodruff's declaration — abandon science for the prophet
- Verse 2: The prophets spoke on science (sun inhabitants, no evolution)
- Chorus: "The prophet over science"
- Verse 3: What happened when science proved them wrong?
- Bridge: Woodruff himself reversed course on polygamy
- Final Chorus: The doctrine that destroys itself

### Option B: Obedience vs. Evidence
- Verse 1: A believer learns science
- Verse 2: The prophet says otherwise
- Chorus: What do you abandon?
- Verse 3: The abandoned theories proved true
- Bridge: Where does authority end?
- Final Chorus: Question everything, or question nothing

## Apologetic Responses to Address

1. **"Spiritual matters, not science"** — No, he explicitly says "arts and sciences of men"
2. **"Faith over reason"** — This isn't dialogue, it's override: "I do not care"
3. **"Prophets can err"** — Then why should we abandon science when they speak?
4. **"Members don't believe this now"** — When did it stop being doctrine?
