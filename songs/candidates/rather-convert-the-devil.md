## Core Concept

Brigham Young declared he would rather try to convert the devil himself than convert a Jew. This stunning statement reveals the depth of anti-Semitic theology in early Mormonism. The song lets Young's own words condemn him, exposing how prophetic authority was used to dress up common prejudice as divine revelation.

---

## Key Quotes

### The Hook

> "Yea, I would rather undertake to convert the devil himself, if it were possible."
> — JoD 2:142

### Supporting Quote 1

> "I would rather undertake to convert five thousand Lamanites, than to convert one of those poor miserable creatures, whose fathers killed the Savior, and who say, 'Amen to the deed,' to this day."
> — JoD 2:142

### Supporting Quote 2

> "Can you make a Christian of a Jew? I tell you, nay. If a Jew comes into this Church, and honestly professes to be a Saint, a follower of Christ, and if the blood of Judah is in his veins, he will apostatize."
> — JoD 2:142

### Supporting Quote 3

> "Do I believe there is one particle of the blood of Judah in his veins? No, not so much as could be seen on the point of the finest cambric needle, through a microscope with a magnifying power of two millions."
> — JoD 2:142 (about a Jewish convert)

### Supporting Quote 4

> "Jerusalem is not to be redeemed by the soft still voice of the preacher of the Gospel of peace... they will be the last of all the seed of Abraham to have the privilege of receiving the New and Everlasting Covenant."
> — JoD 2:143

## Structural Ideas

### Option A: Hierarchy of Souls
- Verse 1: Who can be saved? Everyone has a chance...
- Verse 2: But some are harder than others (Lamanites)
- Chorus: "Rather convert the devil"
- Verse 3: Jews at the very bottom, cursed forever
- Bridge: The blood test — if you convert, you weren't really Jewish
- Final Chorus: What kind of God thinks this way?

### Option B: The Prophet Speaks
- Verse 1: Young establishes his authority
- Verse 2: He ranks the convertibility of peoples
- Chorus: "Rather convert the devil himself"
- Verse 3: The circular logic — true Jews can't convert
- Bridge: Speaking as prophet or speaking as man?
- Final Chorus: Ironic echo

## Apologetic Responses to Address

1. **"Product of his time"** — Exactly the point: prophets reflect culture, not transcend it
2. **"Speaking as a man"** — This is doctrinal teaching from the pulpit about salvation history
3. **"The Church doesn't teach this now"** — But a prophet did, with authority
4. **"Context"** — Full context (the whole sermon) makes it worse, not better
