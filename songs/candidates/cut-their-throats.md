## Core Concept

Brigham Young proposed cutting the throats of "miserable scoundrels" as a form of mob justice, and the entire congregation said "Amen." This call-and-response endorsement of violence was recorded and published in the official Journal of Discourses. The "Amen" from the congregation transforms this from one man's rhetoric into a community endorsement of religious violence.

---

## Key Quotes

### The Hook

> "If any miserable scoundrels come here, cut their throats. (All the people said, Amen.)"
> — JoD 2:311

### Supporting Quote 1

> "I intend to meet them on their own grounds."
> — JoD 2:311

### Supporting Quote 2

> "It was asked this morning how we could obtain redress for our wrongs; I will tell you how it could be done, we could take the same law they have taken, viz., mobocracy."
> — JoD 2:311

### Supporting Quote 3

> "If any man here is a coward, there are fine mountain retreats for those who feel their hearts beating, at every little hue and cry of the wicked."
> — JoD 2:311

### Supporting Quote 4

> "Are we going to fight? No, unless they come upon us and compel us either to fight or be slain."
> — JoD 2:311
