## Core Concept

Brigham Young declared in General Conference that Adam is literally God the Father—"the only God with whom we have to do." He spoke with absolute prophetic authority ("Now hear it, O inhabitants of the earth... every man... must hear it and will know it sooner or later"). The modern LDS Church completely rejects this doctrine as false. The irony: a prophet's solemn declaration that "every man will know it sooner or later" turned out to be something the church hopes everyone will forget.

> "Now hear it, O inhabitants of the earth, Jew and Gentile, Saint and sinner!"
> — JoD 1:50

### Supporting Quote 2

> "Every man upon the earth, professing Christians or non-professing, must hear it, and will know it sooner or later."
> — JoD 1:50

### Supporting Quote 3

> "When our father Adam came into the garden of Eden, he came into it with a celestial body, and brought Eve, one of his wives, with him. He helped to make and organize this world."
> — JoD 1:50

### Supporting Quote 4

> "Now, let all who may hear these doctrines, pause before they make light of them, or treat them with indifference, for they will prove their salvation or damnation."
> — JoD 1:50

## Structural Ideas

### Option A: Prophetic Declaration Structure
- Verse 1: The setting and the buildup ("Now hear it, O inhabitants...")
- Verse 2: The doctrine explained (Adam with celestial body, Eve his wife)
- Chorus: The declaration ("He is our Father and our God")
- Verse 3: The certainty ("Every man will know it sooner or later")
- Bridge: The modern disavowal
- Final Chorus: Ironic echo—what every man "will know" is now false doctrine

### Option B: Question-Answer Structure
- Verse 1: "Who is God?" — The question
- Verse 2: Brigham's answer ("Adam is our Father and our God")
- Chorus: The solemn declaration
- Verse 3: "But today they say..." — The disavowal
- Bridge: What does it mean when prophets speak falsely?
- Final Chorus: Haunting repetition

4. "Context changes the meaning" — Context makes it stronger
