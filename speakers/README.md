### 3. Joseph Smith (1 Crown Jewel)
**File:** `joseph-smith.md`
**Position:** Founder and First President (1830-1844)
**Significance:** Most theologically radical sermon, foundation of LDS cosmology

| Finding | Date | JoD | Category | Rating |
|---------|------|-----|----------|--------|
| King Follett Discourse | Apr 7, 1844 | 6:1-11 | Cosmology | ★★★★★ |

**Key Claims:**
- God was once a mortal man
- God the Father died and was resurrected
- Multiple Gods in council
- God could not create the spirit of man
- Humans can become gods

**Key Patterns:**
- Grandiose authority claims ("I am learned, know more than all the world")
- Staked prophetic calling on this teaching
- Delivered two months before assassination

**Song Priority:** FLAGSHIP for cosmology category
- Modern embarrassment (Hinckley: "I don't know that we teach it")
- Foundation of entire LDS theology

### 4. George Q. Cannon (1 Crown Jewel)
**File:** `george-q-cannon.md`
**Position:** First Counselor in First Presidency (1880-1901)
**Significance:** Perfect failed prophecy with unresolvable irony

| Finding | Date | JoD | Category | Rating |
|---------|------|-----|----------|--------|
| Never Another People | Aug 31, 1884 | 25:271 | Failed Prophecy | ★★★★★ |

**The Prophecy:**
"This Church, this work that He has established, shall never be given into the hands of another people."

**The Failure:**
Six years later (1890 Manifesto), "the work" was fundamentally changed.

**The Unresolvable Irony:**
- Fundamentalists: "We kept the work; mainstream gave it to another people"
- Mainstream: "We're the work; fundamentalists are another people"
- Either way, prophecy failed

**Song Priority:** TIER-1 for failed prophecies
- Perfect six-year timeline
- Cannon participated in his own prophecy's failure
- Pairs with John Taylor's "Cannot Tone Down" (same conference season)

## Speaker Patterns

### Common Themes Across Speakers

1. **Absolute Authority Claims**
   - Joseph: "I am learned, know more than all the world"
   - Brigham: "As true as God lives"
   - Taylor: "I tell you that in the name of the Lord"
   - Cannon: "We have the promise of our God"

2. **"Never" Statements That Failed**
   - Brigham: "We shall never be admitted [without denying polygamy]"
   - Taylor: "We cannot tone it down"
   - Cannon: "Shall never be given to another people"

3. **Divine Sanction for Problematic Teachings**
   - Joseph: God was once mortal = "the great secret"
   - Brigham: Interracial marriage = "the law of God"
   - Taylor: Black people as devil's representation = divine design
   - Cannon: Polygamy preservation = "promise of our God"

4. **Modern Church's Response: Silence or Disavowal**
   - Joseph: De-emphasized (Hinckley: "I don't know that we teach it")
   - Brigham: Disavowed (2013 Race essay)
   - Taylor: Disavowed but never named in essay
   - Cannon: Changed doctrine without addressing prophecy

## Self-Contradictions

### Within Individual Speakers

**Brigham Young:**
- Claimed neutrality on slavery, declared death for interracial marriage
- Said "negroes should be used like human beings" then commanded death penalty
- Promised statehood would "never" come without polygamy; came 6 years after denying it

**John Taylor:**
- Condemned Christian ministers as "murderers," taught Black people as devil's representation
- Said "we cannot tone it down" then went underground (compromise)
- Claimed to speak for God while teaching now-disavowed racism

**Joseph Smith:**
- "I never did harm any man" → ordered Expositor destruction, died in shootout
- Book of Mormon monotheism → King Follett polytheism
- "Meddle not with any man for his religion" → founded missionary religion

**George Q. Cannon:**
- "Never be given to another people" → supported Manifesto changing "the work"
- "God will fight their battles" → surrendered six years later
- "Fast approaching" constitutional collapse → 140+ years later, still waiting

### Across Speakers (Institutional Contradictions)

**On Polygamy:**
- Brigham: "The ONLY men who become Gods"
- Taylor: "We cannot tone it down"
- Modern: Forbidden, monogamous couples achieve full exaltation

**On God's Nature:**
- Joseph: "God was once a mortal man"
- Modern: De-emphasized, "I don't know that we teach it"

**On Race:**
- Brigham: "Death on the spot" for interracial marriage
- Taylor: Black people as "devil's representation"
- Modern: "Unequivocally condemn all racism" (but won't name these teachings)

**On Church Permanence:**
- Cannon: "Shall never be given to another people"
- Reality: Fundamentalists practice 1884 doctrine, mainstream changed

## Notes

**Why These Four Speakers?**
These represent the core leadership of early Mormonism:
- Joseph Smith: Founder
- Brigham Young: Second President (longest tenure)
- John Taylor: Third President (died defending polygamy)
- George Q. Cannon: First Presidency across three presidents

All four held ultimate authority positions when delivering these sermons. All four claimed divine sanction. All four are now contradicted or disavowed by modern church.

**Strategic Importance:**
These aren't fringe apostles or regional leaders—these are the prophets, seers, and revelators at the very top of the hierarchy. When they fail this badly, it undermines the entire prophetic model.
