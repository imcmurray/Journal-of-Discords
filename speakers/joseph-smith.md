## Overview

Joseph Smith was the founder of the Latter-day Saint movement and first president of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. He claimed to have received divine revelations beginning in 1820, translated the Book of Mormon, and established the church in 1830. He was killed by a mob while imprisoned in Carthage Jail, Illinois, on June 27, 1844.

**Historical Significance:**
- Founded the LDS church (1830)
- Translated the Book of Mormon (published 1830)
- Received revelations compiled in Doctrine & Covenants
- Introduced temple endowment ceremonies
- Introduced polygamy (practiced secretly from early 1840s)
- Established Nauvoo as theocratic city-state
- Ran for U.S. President (1844)
- Killed by mob two months after King Follett Discourse

**Speaking Style:**
- Prophetic authority claims ("Thus saith the Lord")
- Mixing biblical interpretation with new revelation
- Redefining traditional Christian concepts
- Grandiose claims about his knowledge and authority
- Apocalyptic and millennial themes

**JoD Context:**
Joseph Smith's sermons in the Journal of Discourses were not delivered during the JoD publication period (1854-1886) but were reconstructed from contemporary notes and published retrospectively. The King Follett Discourse is his most significant JoD entry.

## Key Findings

### Cosmology and Nature of God

#### Finding 1: The King Follett Discourse
**Source:** JoD 6:1-11 (April 7, 1844)
**Full Analysis:** `volumes/vol-06/findings/1844-04-07-king-follett.md`

**The Teaching:**
> "God himself was once as we are now, and is an exalted Man, and sits enthroned in yonder heavens... I am going to tell you how God came to be God. We have imagined and supposed that God was God from all eternity, I will refute that idea, and will take away and do away the vail, so that you may see."

**Context:**
- Delivered April 7, 1844 (two months before Joseph's assassination)
- Funeral sermon for King Follett
- General Conference, 8,000-20,000 people present
- Recorded by four scribes (Willard Richards, Wilford Woodruff, Thomas Bullock, William Clayton)
- Joseph's most mature theological exposition

**The Core Claims:**
1. **God was once a mortal man** who progressed to godhood
2. **God the Father died and was resurrected** on another world
3. **Humans can become gods** through the same progression
4. **Multiple Gods exist** ("the head of the Gods called a council")
5. **God did not create ex nihilo** (organized eternal matter instead)
6. **Human intelligence is co-eternal with God** (God never created it)
7. **God could not create the spirit of man** (limitation on divine power)

**Why It Matters:**
- **Radical departure from Christianity:** No traditional Christian theology teaches God was once mortal
- **Polytheism:** "Head of the Gods," "council of the Gods," "all Gods have done before you"
- **Limited God:** God as organizer, not omnipotent Creator
- **Modern embarrassment:** Church has systematically de-emphasized these teachings
- **Hinckley's denial (1997):** "I don't know that we teach it. I don't know that we emphasize it"
- **Foundational to LDS theology:** Eternal progression, exaltation, temple ceremonies all depend on this

**Specific Shocking Quotes:**

**On God's Mortality:**
> "God himself the Father of us all, dwelt on an earth, the same as Jesus Christ himself did... Jesus said, 'As the Father hath power in himself, even so hath the Son power'—to do what? Why, what the Father did. The answer is obvious—in a manner, to lay down his body and take it up again. Jesus, what are you going to do? To lay down my life, as my Father did, and take it up again."

**On Becoming Gods:**
> "Here, then, is eternal life—to know the only wise and true God; and you have got to learn how to be Gods yourselves, and to be kings and priests to God, the same as all Gods have done before you—namely, by going from one small degree to another, and from a small capacity to a great one—from grace to grace, from exaltation to exaltation."

**On God's Power Limitations:**
> "I might with boldness proclaim from the housetops that God never had the power to create the spirit of man at all. God himself could not create himself."

**On Council of Gods:**
> "In the beginning, the head of the Gods called a council of the Gods; and they came together and concocted a plan to create the world and people it."

**Modern Church Position:**
- Still teaches humans can become "like God" (exaltation)
- Still teaches God has a body of flesh and bone
- De-emphasizes or avoids teaching God was once mortal
- Never explicitly repudiated King Follett Discourse
- Strategic silence in manuals and missionary materials
- President Hinckley (1997): "I don't know that we teach it... I don't know a lot about it"

**The Problem:**
- Joseph staked his prophetic calling on this teaching: "If I fail to do it, it becomes my duty to renounce all further pretensions to revelations, inspirations, or to be a Prophet"
- Later prophets (Brigham Young, Lorenzo Snow) taught it explicitly
- Modern church can't formally repudiate without undermining Joseph's authority
- Can't fully embrace without appearing bizarre to mainstream Christianity

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ (Foundational doctrine with modern disavowal)

**Song Angles:**
1. **"How God Came to Be God"** — Joseph's promise vs. modern silence
2. **"God Himself Was Once as We Are Now"** — The shocking claim
3. **"I Am Learned"** — Joseph's grandiosity vs. reality
4. **Hinckley's "I Don't Know"** — Modern retreat from Joseph's certainty

### Authority Claims

#### Pattern: Ultimate Knowledge Claims

Throughout King Follett Discourse, Joseph makes grandiose claims:

> "I am learned, and know more than all the world put together. The Holy Ghost does, anyhow; and he is within me, and comprehends more than all the world" (JoD 6:5)

> "I never did harm any man since I was born into the world" (JoD 6:10)

> "Meddle not with any man for his religion" (JoD 6:3)

**The Irony:**
- Minimal formal education, yet claims to "know more than all the world"
- Made numerous provably false claims about Hebrew, Egyptian, etc.
- "Never did harm" — two months later killed in shootout after ordering destruction of Nauvoo Expositor
- "Meddle not" — founded religion that sends missionaries worldwide

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ (Grandiosity vs. reality)

### Apostasy and Sons of Perdition

#### The Unpardonable Sin

**The Teaching:**
> "What must a man do to commit the unpardonable sin? He must receive the Holy Ghost, have the heavens opened unto him, and know God, and then sin against him... This is the case with many apostates of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints... When a man begins to be an enemy to this work, he hunts me; he seeks to kill me, and never ceases to thirst for my blood."

**Why It Matters:**
- Creates category of unforgivable sinners (apostates with spiritual experiences)
- Demonizes those who leave the church
- Claims apostates are bloodthirsty
- Two months later, killed by mob including former members
- Self-fulfilling prophecy and persecution narrative

**Song Potential:** ★★★ (Creates framework for demonizing ex-members)

## Self-Contradictions

**Within King Follett Discourse:**
1. Claims to teach only from Bible while arguing all Christian doctors are wrong about the Bible
2. Says "God himself could not create himself" but also teaches God progressed to godhood (infinite regress problem)
3. Claims to want peace while demonizing apostates as bloodthirsty
4. "I never did harm any man" while commanding militia and ordering Expositor destruction

**Across Institution:**
- Book of Mormon teaches traditional monotheism ("there is but one God")
- King Follett teaches polytheism ("council of the Gods")
- Earlier revelations more orthodox, later teachings increasingly radical
- Modern church teaches deification but not God's mortality

## Apologetic Challenges

### Challenge 1: "Speaking Metaphorically"
**Why it fails:** Joseph explicitly says he's revealing "the great secret" and telling people "how God came to be God" — language is unambiguously literal.

### Challenge 2: "Just Speculation, Not Doctrine"
**Why it fails:** Delivered at General Conference, recorded by official scribes, Joseph staked his prophetic calling on it, shaped LDS theology for over a century.

### Challenge 3: "Modern Revelation Has Superseded"
**Why it fails:** No official revelation has retracted it, it remains published, core concepts (exaltation) still taught, church just quietly distances.

### Challenge 4: "This Is Christian Deification Doctrine"
**Why it fails:** No Christian theology teaches God was once mortal, died, or that there are multiple Gods in council — this goes far beyond Eastern Orthodox theosis.
