## Major Discoveries

### 1. THE "NEVER ABANDON" PROPHECY ★★★★★

**Primary Source: JoD 25:303-317** (Design_of_God sermon)
- **Speaker:** John Taylor (Church President)
- **Date:** October 6-7, 1884 (General Conference)
- **Context:** Height of federal prosecution under Edmunds Act

**Key Quote:**
> "God has given us a revelation in regard to celestial marriage. I did not make it. He has told us certain things pertaining to this matter, and they would like us to tone that principle down and change it and make it applicable to the views of the day. **This we cannot do; nor can we interfere with any of the commands of God** to meet the persuasions or behests of men. I cannot do it, and will not do it."

**More from same sermon:**
> "If God has introduced something for our glory and exaltation, we are not going to have that kicked over by any improper influence, either inside or outside of the Church of the living God. **We will stand by the principles of eternal truth; living we will proclaim them, and dying we will be true to them**, and after death will live again in their enjoyment in the eternal worlds."

**Congregation response:** "The vast congregation responded with a loud 'Amen.'"

**Timeline to failure:** 6 years (Manifesto issued 1890)

---

### 2. "NEVER GIVEN TO ANOTHER PEOPLE" ★★★★★

**Primary Source: JoD 25:268-275** (A Peculiar Work)
- **Speaker:** George Q. Cannon (First Presidency)
- **Date:** August 31, 1884
- **Context:** Defending the work against persecution

**Key Quote:**
> "There is this consolation, however connected with the work with which we are identified—that **God has made promises unto us that it shall never be given into the hands of another people**. The Apostles looked forward to the time when there would be a great falling away, and the man of sin be revealed, and they warned the church in their day of that falling away. But **God has given unto us the assurance that this Church, this work that He has established, shall never be given into the hands of another people, but that it shall stand forever**, and it shall go forward accomplishing His designs, until it shall fill the whole earth."

**Context for failure:**
- Spoken about "this work" in context of persecution for polygamy
- Within 6 years, the work WAS given to another people (monogamists)
- Polygamists excommunicated and formed fundamentalist groups
- Modern LDS church explicitly rejects polygamy practice
