#### 1. JoD 3:264-268 - Polygamy Damnation Threat
**Sermon:** "Plurality of Wives—The Free Agency of Man" by Brigham Young
**Date:** July 14, 1855 (Provo)
**Categories:** STRIKING, SPECIFIC, TONE
**Quote:** "Now if any of you will deny the plurality of wives, and continue to do so, I promise that you will be damned"
**Analysis:**
- Direct threat of damnation for rejecting polygamy doctrine
- BY admits he "desired the grave" when first learning the doctrine
- "I could hardly get over it for a long time"
- "When I saw a funeral, I felt to envy the corpse its situation"
- Claims spirits will be born in "brothels" if necessary to get bodies
- IRONIC: BY initially hated the doctrine he now threatens damnation for rejecting

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ (Strong - direct threat + BY's own confession of revulsion)

#### 2. JoD 3:43-51 - Blood Atonement / Severed Heads
**Sermon:** "Faith, etc.—Practical Religion—Chastisement—Necessity of Devils" by Brigham Young
**Date:** October 6, 1855
**Categories:** STRIKING, BIZARRE, SPECIFIC
**Quote:** "Just let the Lord Almighty say, 'Lay judgment to the line and righteousness to the plummet,' and the time of thieves is short in this community... their heads will have to be severed from their bodies"
**Analysis:**
- Clear blood atonement language
- Specific punishment: beheading for theft
- BY mocks Massachusetts/Connecticut reactions
- Talks about exposing members' faults publicly
- Threatens to "expose you, from time to time, as we please"

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ (Strong - blood atonement + specific violence)

#### 3. JoD 3:28-37 - Racial Curse / Thwarting God
**Sermon:** "The History of Mahomedanism" by George A. Smith
**Date:** September 23, 1855
**Categories:** STRIKING, SPECIFIC
**Quote:** "When the curse of the Almighty comes upon a people, it certainly is the work of generations to remove it. When Cain brought a curse upon his own head... The curse that came upon Canaan, the son of Ham, has extended to a great portion of the human race, and has continued to the present day. For the last hundred years, philanthropists... have exerted themselves vigorously to thwart the purposes of the Almighty... but their endeavors are vain and useless"
**Analysis:**
- Says abolitionists are "thwarting the purposes of the Almighty"
- Curse on Ham's descendants "extended to a great portion of the human race"
- Continues "to the present day"
- Explicitly condemns anti-slavery efforts
- Positioned as "thwarting God"

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ (Strong - direct defense of slavery as God's will)

#### 4. JoD 3:80-96 - "The Vision" + Universal Salvation
