## METHODOLOGY

Read each sermon systematically, flagging:
- **BIZARRE** — Strange claims that don't fit other categories
- **STRIKING** — Memorable quotes with song potential
- **IRONIC** — Self-contradictions, unaware hypocrisy
- **SPECIFIC** — Unusual specificity (visions, dates, numbers)
- **TONE** — Arrogance, dismissiveness, hostility
- **VIOLENCE** — Advocacy of violence or bloodshed

Rating system: ★ (notable) to ★★★★★ (exceptional song material)

## TOP-TIER FINDINGS (5-STAR SONG CANDIDATES)

### 1. "The Bowie Knife Dream" — JoD 1:81-84
**Speaker:** Brigham Young, March 27, 1853
**Rating:** ★★★★★
**Why:** Brigham recounts dream of slitting throats "from ear to ear," congregation erupts in approval, all vote to support violence against apostates. Shocking violence with democratic endorsement.

**Key Quotes:**
- "I took my large bowie knife...and cut one of their throats from ear to ear, saying, 'Go to hell across lots'"
- "I say, rather than that apostates should flourish here, I will unsheath my bowie knife, and conquer or die"
- "[Great commotion in the congregation, and a simultaneous burst of feeling, assenting to the declaration.]"
- "If you say it is right, raise your hands. [All hands up.]"

**Apologetic Response:** "It was just a dream! He didn't actually do it!"
**Counter:** He told the dream publicly to threaten apostates, and the entire congregation voted to support the violence.

---

### 2. "Lay Down Your Religion" — JoD 1:209-220
**Speaker:** Brigham Young, Oct 9, 1852
**Rating:** ★★★★★
**Why:** Perfect metaphor for compartmentalized Mormonism — Saints literally leaving religion at canyon entrance, swearing and fighting, then picking it up on the way home.

**Key Quotes:**
- "They lay down their religion at the mouth of the kanyon, saying, 'Thou lie there, until I go for my load of wood'"
- "You Elders of Israel will go into the kanyons, and curse and swear—damn, and curse your oxen, and swear by Him who created you!"
- "You will rip, and curse, and swear, as bad as any pirates ever did"

**Song Hook:** "Religion, thou lie there / Until I get my load / I'll pick you up on my way home / Back down the canyon road"

---

### 3. "Blood Atonement Preview" — JoD 1:71-73
**Speaker:** Orson Hyde, April 9, 1853
**Rating:** ★★★★★
**Why:** Early, explicit advocacy for public execution of thieves. Direct precursor to later blood atonement doctrine.

**Key Quotes:**
- "The best way to sanctify ourselves...is to rid ourselves of every thief...it is the law and practice of our neighbouring state...it would have a tendency to place a terror on those who leave these parts...when they see the heads of thieves taken off, or shot down before the public"
- "I believe it to be pleasing in the sight of heaven to sanctify ourselves and put these things away from our midst"

---

### 4. "By the Voice of the People" — JoD 1:131-137
**Speaker:** Brigham Young, April 6, 1853
**Rating:** ★★★★★
**Why:** Brigham explicitly says Joseph was President "by the voice of the people," not by revelation. Undermines prophetic authority at its foundation.

**Key Quotes:**
- "Does a man's being a Prophet in this Church prove that he shall be the President of it? I answer, no!"
- "Can you find any revelation appointing him the President of the Church? The keys of the Priesthood were committed to Joseph...but when he was called to preside over the Church, it was by the voice of the people"

**Apologetic Response:** "Keys and Presidency are different things"
**Counter:** Then why do modern prophets claim both divine keys AND divine appointment as President?

---

### 5. "The Prophet's Store" — JoD 1:209-220
**Speaker:** Brigham Young, Oct 9, 1852
**Rating:** ★★★★★
**Why:** Extended parable showing Joseph couldn't run a business because people would call him a false prophet if he demanded payment. Perfect illustration of impossible expectations.

**Key Quotes:**
- "Brother Joseph, I want a shawl.' 'Well,' says Joseph, 'these goods will make the people apostatize; so over they go, they are of less value than the people'"
- "Joseph was a first-rate fellow with them all the time, provided he never would ask them to pay him"
- "In this way it is easy for us to trade away a first-rate store of goods, and be in debt for them"

**Apologetic Response:** "This shows the Saints' faith problems, not Joseph's"
**Counter:** It shows Joseph was trapped by impossible expectations — prophet if generous, fraud if businesslike.

## THEMATIC PATTERNS

### Violence & Blood Atonement
- Orson Hyde advocating public execution (1:71-73)
- Brigham's bowie knife dream with congregation approval (1:81-84)
- Howard Egan acquittal despite clear murder (1:100-103)

### Authoritarian Control
- Brigham cannot be removed as Governor (1:185-191)
- "Do what you want, IF YOU CAN" followed by restrictions (1:74-79)
- Death preferable to children's disobedience (1:66-71)

### Impossible Expectations
- Joseph's store parable — damned if he charged, damned if he didn't (1:209-220)
- Saints paying Gentiles, stiffing the Church (1:209-220)
- Persecution makes Mormonism spread faster (1:88-94)

### Bizarre Cosmology
- Deity within us causing growth (1:66-71, 1:88-94)
- Earth undergoing baptism and resurrection (1:328-334)
- Gold and silver growing like wheat (1:209-220)
- Adobe better than stone for temples (1:209-220)

### Democratic vs. Theocratic Tensions
- Joseph appointed by popular vote, not revelation (1:131-137)
- But Brigham claims divine mandate to remain Governor (1:185-191)

## CONCLUSION
