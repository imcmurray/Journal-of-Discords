#### 1. God's Ancient People Polygamists
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** October 7, 1874
- **Pages:** JoD 17:214-229
- **Topics:** Polygamy, Authority, Prophecy
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★★
- **Flag Reason:** Extensive defense of polygamy with extraordinary claims
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "God has told us Latter-day Saints that we shall be condemned if we do not enter into that principle [polygamy]"
  - "I want to prophecy... all men and women who oppose the revelation which God has given in relation to polygamy will find themselves in darkness; the Spirit of God will withdraw from them... until they will finally go down to hell and be damned"
  - Description of New Jerusalem with 12 gates named after polygamous sons of Jacob, with monogamists relegated to "dogs, sorcerers, whoremongers... whosoever loveth and maketh a lie"
  - Claims polygamy required for Israel's restoration: "when they do absolutely return and hearken to all the words of the book of this law God has promised to gather them again"
  - Mathematical argument for ancient polygamy based on census data
- **Apologetic Concerns:** This is a failed prophecy (polygamy abandoned 1890), harsh condemnation of those who oppose it, extreme rhetoric
- **Cross-References:** Topic: Polygamy; Failed prophecies

#### 3. Nothing Strange or New to Live and Die (Cosmology)
- **Speaker:** Brigham Young
- **Date:** July 19, 1874
- **Pages:** JoD 17:139-145
- **Topics:** Cosmology, Creation, Fall
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★
- **Flag Reason:** Scientific claims about Earth's fall from heaven
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "This earth is our home, it was framed expressly for the habitation of those who are faithful to God... When the earth was framed and brought into existence and man was placed upon it, it was near the throne of our Father in heaven"
  - "When man fell—though that was designed in the economy... when man fell, the earth fell into space, and took up its abode in this planetary system, and the sun became our light"
  - "Ask the astronomer how far we are from the nearest of those heavenly bodies that are called the fixed stars. Can he count the miles? It would be a task for him to tell us the distance"
  - "When the Lord said—'Let there be light,' there was light, for the earth was brought near the sun that it might reflect upon it"
  - "When it [the earth] is glorified it will return again unto the presence of the Father, and it will dwell there"
- **Apologetic Concerns:** Contradicts established astronomy and physics; literal interpretation of Fall affecting Earth's orbit
- **Cross-References:** Topic: Cosmology; Speaker: Brigham Young

#### 4. Man is the Offspring of God (Science/Cosmology)
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** March 14, 1875
- **Pages:** JoD 17:322-333
- **Topics:** Cosmology, Science, Other worlds
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★
- **Flag Reason:** Claims about other inhabited worlds, light as intelligence, scientific speculation
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "The light that proceeds forth from all these heavenly luminaries... is the same light that quickens the understanding"
  - "The elements, composing our spirits were eternal; they were never created, neither indeed can be"
  - "Inquires one—'Do you mean to say that other worlds have fallen as well as ours?' Yes, man is an agent... when God sends inhabitants on various creations he sends them on the great and grand principle of giving them an opportunity to exercise that agency; and they have exercised it, and have fallen"

- **Cross-References:** Topic: Cosmology; Speaker: Orson Pratt

#### 5. Cease to Bring in and Build up Babylon (Book of Mormon "Proof")
- **Speaker:** Brigham Young
- **Date:** April 18, 1874
- **Pages:** JoD 17:36-47
- **Topics:** Book of Mormon, Science, Authority
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★
- **Flag Reason:** Claims fossil horse discovery proves Book of Mormon
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "There is one result [of Professor Marsh's research], so far, that particularly pleases me. There are some here who know a man by the name of John Hyde... his great argument against the Book of Mormon was, that it stated that the old Jaredites and, perhaps, the Nephites... had horses, while it is well known that horses were unknown to the aboriginal inhabitants of America when it was discovered by Columbus"
  - "Now, since Professor Marsh and his class began their investigations, they have found among the fossil remains of the extinct animals of America no less than fourteen different kinds of horses, varying in height from three to nine feet"
  - "This discovery of Professor Marsh is particularly pleasing to us 'Mormons,' because he has so far scientifically demonstrated the Book of Mormon to be true"
- **Apologetic Concerns:** Fossil horses were extinct millions of years before humans; doesn't prove Book of Mormon horses; misuses science
- **Cross-References:** Topic: Book of Mormon claims; Speaker: Brigham Young
