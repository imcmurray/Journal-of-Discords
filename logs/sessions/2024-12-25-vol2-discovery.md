## Sermon 1: The Gospel—Growing in Knowledge, etc.
**Source:** JoD 2:1-10
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** October 23, 1853

### Discoveries

#### 1. "You Poor Devils!" — Dismissal of Fellow Saints
- **Quote:** "I say, there was not a man who would say that Joseph Smith was a Prophet, could stay there; they had all to leave the State; and you will now show yourselves at this late day, and try to have us believe you are first-rate Latter-day Saints. My thoughts are, 'YOU POOR DEVILS!'"
- **Category:** TONE
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Brigham shows contempt for Saints who stayed in Missouri rather than fleeing. Dismissive, judgmental tone toward fellow believers who made different choices during persecution.

#### 2. Adam Not Made from Dust — Rejection of Biblical Literalism
- **Quote:** "Supposing that Adam was formed actually out of clay, out of the same kind of material from which bricks are formed... he would have been an adobie to this day. He would not have known anything... You believe Adam was made of the dust of this earth. This I do not believe, though it is supposed that it is so written in the Bible; but it is not, to my understanding."
- **Category:** BIZARRE / STRIKING
- **Rating:** ★★★★☆
- **Notes:** Brigham explicitly rejects Genesis account of Adam's creation from dust. Says "You can write that information to the States, if you please—that I have publicly declared that I do not believe that portion of the Bible." Creates space for Adam-God doctrine. This is pre-Adam-God (JoD 1:50-51), showing theological evolution.

#### 3. Persecution as Divine Gift — Extreme View
- **Quote:** "Do I acknowledge the hand of the Lord in persecution? Yes, I do. It is one of the greatest blessings that could be conferred upon the people of God. I acknowledge the hand of the Lord in levelling His people to the dust of the earth, and reducing them to a state of abject poverty."
- **Category:** IRONIC / STRIKING
- **Rating:** ★★★★☆
- **Notes:** Celebrates persecution and poverty as God's blessings. If taken seriously, undermines any complaint about historical persecution. Modern church might find this embarrassing when claiming victim status.

#### 4. God Authors All Evil
- **Quote:** "'Is there evil in the city and I have not done it, saith the Lord.' There is no evil that is not known to the Lord. He has been perfectly acquainted with all the persecutions the Saints have passed through. His hand was there..."
- **Category:** BIZARRE
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Claims God actively causes/authors evil. Problematic theodicy. Brigham attributes all persecution directly to God's hand.

#### 5. "Begotten in a Vault of Liquor"
- **Quote:** "There are scores in our midst who were begotten in a vault of liquor, and were enveloped in it till the day of their birth. They have come forth from it, and have a longing desire to still swim in it unto the day of their death."
- **Category:** BIZARRE / SPECIFIC
- **Rating:** ★★☆☆☆
- **Notes:** Unusual medical/hereditary claim about alcoholism being transmitted through conception conditions. Pre-scientific understanding of addiction.

## Sermon 2: The Power of God and the Power of Satan
**Source:** JoD 2:10-16
**Speaker:** Jedediah M. Grant
**Date:** February 19, 1854

### Discoveries

#### 6. "If Joseph Said He Wanted Your Wife..."
- **Quote:** "When the family organization was revealed from heaven—the patriarchal order of God, and Joseph began, on the right and on the left, to add to his family, what a quaking there was in Israel. Says one brother to another, 'Joseph says all covenants are done away, and none are binding but the new covenants; now suppose Joseph should come and say he wanted your wife, what would you say to that?' 'I would tell him to go to hell.'"
- **Category:** STRIKING / IRONIC
- **Rating:** ★★★★★
- **Notes:** Explicit acknowledgment of polygamy controversy in Nauvoo. Shows resistance to doctrine. Grant presents this as a test of faith, but modern readers see coercion. Incredible candor about the internal reaction.

#### 7. The "Right" Answer to Giving Up Your Wife
- **Quote:** "What would a man of God say, who felt aright, when Joseph asked him for his money? He would say, 'Yes, and I wish I had more to help to build up the kingdom of God.' Or if he came and said, I want your wife?' 'O yes,' he would say, 'here she is; there are plenty more.'"
- **Category:** STRIKING / BIZARRE
- **Rating:** ★★★★★
- **Notes:** Presents total surrender of property AND WIVES as the ideal faithful response. "There are plenty more" is shockingly casual about women as property. This is the test Grant says proves faith. Absolutely damning for polygamy apologetics.

#### 8. Abraham Test — But Not Really
- **Quote:** "Did the Lord actually want Abraham to kill Isaac? Did the Prophet Joseph want every man's wife he asked for? He did not, but in that thing was the grand thread of the Priesthood developed. The grand object in view was to try the people of God, to see what was in them."
- **Category:** IRONIC
- **Rating:** ★★★★☆
- **Notes:** Claims Joseph didn't actually want the wives he asked for — it was just a test. But historical evidence shows he did marry them. This is either dishonest or shows Grant didn't know the full picture. The "Abraham test" defense is common in polygamy apologetics, but here it's stated that some requests were never meant to be fulfilled.

#### 9. Priesthood Has Total Jurisdiction
- **Quote:** "If Joseph had a right to dictate me in relation to salvation, in relation to a hereafter, he had a right to dictate me in relation to all my earthly affairs, in relation to the treasures of the earth, and in relation to the earth itself... That is what he had a right to do, if he had any right at all."
- **Category:** TONE / SPECIFIC
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Totalitarian view of priesthood authority. No separation between spiritual and temporal. Sets stage for United Order, blood atonement, and other controversial practices. Grant argues for absolute prophetic authority over every aspect of life.

## Sermon 18: The Marriage Relations
**Source:** JoD 2:75-87
**Speaker:** Orson Hyde
**Date:** October 6, 1854

**NOTE:** This sermon is PACKED with high-potential discoveries. Most controversial sermon so far.

### Discoveries

#### 12. Jesus Was Married — At Cana
- **Quote:** "We will turn over to the account of the marriage in Cana of Galilee... 'the governor of the feast called the bridegroom, and saith unto him'—that is, the ruler of the feast saith unto the bridegroom... Gentlemen, that is as plain as the translators, or different councils over this Scripture, dare allow it to go to the world, but the thing is there; it is told; Jesus was the bridegroom at the marriage of Cana of Galilee, and he told them what to do."
- **Category:** BIZARRE / STRIKING
- **Rating:** ★★★★★
- **Notes:** Hyde explicitly claims Jesus Christ was the bridegroom at Cana. This is NOT modern LDS doctrine. Modern church avoids/denies this. Incredible claim with zero scriptural support beyond misreading the text. Pure speculation presented as fact.

#### 13. Jesus Had Multiple Wives — Mary, Martha, and Others
- **Quote:** "I do not despise to be called a son of Abraham, if he had a dozen wives; or to be called a brother, a son, a child of the Savior, if he had Mary, and Martha, and several others, as wives; and though he did cast seven devils out of one of them, it is all the same to me."
- **Category:** BIZARRE / STRIKING
- **Rating:** ★★★★★
- **Notes:** Claims Jesus was polygamous, married to Mary Magdalene, Mary, Martha, and others. The "seven devils" reference identifies Mary Magdalene as one of the wives. Absolutely not modern LDS doctrine. Speculative theology presented as truth.

#### 14. Jesus Had Children Before Crucifixion
- **Quote:** "When they make his soul an offering for sin, he shall see his seed... I shall say here, that before the Savior died, he looked upon his own natural children, as we look upon ours; he saw his seed, and immediately afterwards he was cut off from the earth; but who shall declare his generation? They had no father to hold them in honorable remembrance; they passed into the shades of obscurity, never to be exposed to mortal eye as the seed of the blessed one."
- **Category:** BIZARRE / STRIKING
- **Rating:** ★★★★★
- **Notes:** Claims Jesus literally had biological children who were hidden to protect them from assassination. Uses Isaiah 53 to support this. Zero evidence. Pure speculation. Modern church would be horrified by this.

#### 15. Mary Calling Jesus "Lord" = Wife Calling Husband
- **Quote:** "In old times, and it is common in this day, the women, even as Sarah, called their husbands Lord; the word Lord is tantamount to husband in some languages, master, lord, husband, are about synonymous... When Mary of old came to the sepulchre on the first day of the week, instead of finding Jesus she saw two angels in white... 'Because they have taken away my Lord,' or husband, 'and I know not where they have laid him.'"
- **Category:** BIZARRE
- **Rating:** ★★★★☆
- **Notes:** Linguistic gymnastics to argue Mary Magdalene was Jesus' wife. Completely speculative. Inserts "or husband" into scripture. Modern BYU scholars would never do this.

#### 16. World Leaders Keep Mistresses, Murder Embryos
- **Quote:** "A man, for instance, who has the most riches... they not only have their wives and families with whom they publicly live and associate, but they also have in secret places their mistresses, whom they maintain not honorably, but under a cloak as it were. When by their illegitimate connexions they are likely to increase their race, what means do they resort to, to save their credit... A skilful practitioner is employed to destroy the embryo offspring. This is murder."
- **Category:** STRIKING / TONE
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Hyde condemns abortion and mistresses to defend polygamy. Uses worst behavior of elites to justify polygamy as "honest" alternative. But the comparison itself reveals that leaders were aware of how polygamy looked.

#### 17. Gentile Blood Literally Cleansed Out
- **Quote:** (From Brigham in sermon 42, but referenced theme) Joseph said Gentile blood was "actually cleansed out of their veins, and the blood of Jacob made to circulate in them; and the revolution and change in the system were so great that it caused the beholder to think they were going into fits."
- **Category:** BIZARRE (cross-reference to later sermon)
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Will document fully when I reach that sermon.

#### 18. Curse on Prostitution in Zion — Flesh Rotting
- **Quote:** "I will tell you what shall happen to those men and women who commit lewdness, and go and boast of it, and laugh in the face of heaven. The day shall come when their flesh shall rot upon their bones, and as they are walking it shall drop, and become a nauseous stink upon the highway... And when you step, chunks of your flesh shall drop off your bones, and; stink enough to sicken a dog."
- **Category:** TONE / BIZARRE
- **Rating:** ★★★★☆
- **Notes:** Orson Hyde pronounces a literal curse on adulterers/prostitutes in Salt Lake City. Claims their flesh will rot and fall off while they walk. The congregation shouted "AMEN!" to this. Absolutely bizarre. Failed prophecy if taken literally. Blood atonement adjacent.

#### 19. Replenish = Refill — Pre-Adamites
- **Quote:** "The earth, you remember, was void and empty, until our first parents began at the garden of Eden. What does the term replenish mean? This word is derived from the Latin; 're' and 'plenus;' 're' denotes repetition, iteration; and 'plenus' signifies full, complete; then the meaning of the word replenish is, to refill, recomplete... The world was peopled before the days of Adam, as much so as it was before the days of Noah."
- **Category:** BIZARRE
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Pre-Adamite doctrine. Claims earth was populated before Adam. Uses etymology to argue Genesis means "re-populate" not "populate." Contradicts modern LDS doctrine and science.

**Source:** JoD 2:163-169
**Speaker:** Joseph Smith
**Date:** June 30, 1843

### Discoveries

#### 20. "I Will Spill the Last Drop of Blood"
- **Quote:** "But before I will bear this unhallowed persecution any longer—before I will be dragged away again, among my enemies for trial, I will spill the last drop of blood in my veins, and will see all my enemies IN HELL!"
- **Category:** TONE / IRONIC
- **Rating:** ★★★★☆
- **Notes:** Joseph swears he'll fight to the death rather than submit. One year later, he went to Carthage and was killed. Either this shows his resolve failed, or this was bravado. Modern church emphasizes his martyrdom as willing sacrifice — this contradicts that narrative.

#### 21. "Forbearance Is No Longer a Virtue"
- **Quote:** "I swear I will not deal so mildly with them again; for the time has come when forbearance is no longer a virtue; and if you or I are again taken unlawfully, you are at liberty to give loose to blood and thunder."
- **Category:** TONE
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Joseph authorizes violence in "self-defense." "Blood and thunder" license. Sets stage for Nauvoo period violence and possibly Carthage shootout.

#### 22. "I Know More Than They All" (Lawyers)
- **Quote:** "I wish the lawyer who says we have no powers in Nauvoo may be choked to death with his own words. Don't employ lawyers, or pay them money for their knowledge, for I have learnt they don't know anything. I know more than they all."

- **Rating:** ★★☆☆☆

#### 23. "I Am a Lawyer Too" — Taught by God

#### 24. Joseph Preached on Marriage at Pawpaw Grove

## Sermon 50: The Holy Spirit and the Godhead
**Source:** JoD 2:334-347
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Date:** February 18, 1855

### Discoveries

*Note: This is a theological treatise on the nature of God, the Holy Spirit, and material theology. Mostly doctrinal speculation rather than "striking" quotes, but several unusual elements:*

#### 29. Holy Spirit Is Material Substance Diffused Through Space
- **Quote:** "When I speak of the Holy Spirit, I speak of it as being a substance that is precisely the same in its attributes as those of the Father and Son; I speak of it as a substance that is diffused throughout space, the same as oxygen is in pure water or air, and as being cognizant of every day's events."
- **Category:** BIZARRE
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Orson Pratt's material theology. Holy Ghost is literal substance like oxygen, not a person necessarily. Highly speculative. Not modern LDS doctrine. Pratt was later somewhat repudiated for this theology.

#### 30. God = Ahman, Son Ahman, Sons Ahman, Anglo-man
- **Quote:** "There is one revelation that this people are not generally acquainted with... 'What is the name of God in the pure language?' The answer says, 'Ahman.' 'What is the name of the Son of God?' Answer, 'Son Ahman—the greatest of all the parts of God excepting Ahman.' 'What is the name of men?' 'Sons Ahman,' is the answer. 'What is the name of angels in the pure language?' 'Anglo-man.'"
- **Category:** BIZARRE / SPECIFIC
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Pratt reveals otherwise unpublished revelation about God's name in "pure language" (Adamic language). This theology was never fully developed. Shows early speculative doctrine that didn't survive. "Anglo-man" for angels is particularly odd.

#### 31. We Are All "Parts of God"
- **Quote:** "What is the conclusion to be drawn from this? It is, that these intelligent beings are all parts of God, and that those who have the most of the parts of God are the greatest, or next to God, and those who have the next greatest portions of the parts of God, are the next greatest, or nearest to the fulness of God."
- **Category:** BIZARRE
- **Rating:** ★★★☆☆
- **Notes:** Panentheism. Humans are literally portions/parts of God. The more "God substance" you have, the greater you are. Very different from modern LDS anthropology. Pratt's speculative theology.

## Sermon 26: Spiritual Gifts—Hell—The Spirit World—The Lamanites
