## HIGH Priority Sermons

### 1. JoD 5:202-205 — Reformation (Heber C. Kimball)
**Date:** October 12, 1856
**Priority:** ★★★★★ HIGH
**Category:** Blood Atonement / Reformation Doctrine

**Key Quotes:**
> "Where sins have been committed, there must be an atonement made to satisfy the demands of justice: and when justice is satisfied, mercy claims the subject."

> "Supposing you have sinned against your brethren, or in some way offended them, will your sins be remitted, unless you go and make the proper acknowledgments? No, they will not. You have got to pay the debt; and sin cannot be remitted until you confess it and make satisfaction to the party aggrieved."

> "Satisfaction must be made to the one injured, or baptism will be of no benefit: the Holy Ghost will not ratify that act until I have paid the debt."

**Analysis:**
- Classic "satisfaction" language associated with blood atonement period
- Context: Mormon Reformation of 1856-1857
- Theological framework: atonement requires satisfaction to injured parties
- Does NOT explicitly state blood atonement, but uses the doctrinal framework
- Cross-reference with JoD 4:53-54, 219-220 (explicit blood atonement sermons)

**Song Potential:** ★★★☆☆ MEDIUM
- Lacks the shocking explicitness of Jedediah Grant's blood atonement sermons
- More theological than visceral
- Could be used as supporting evidence for blood atonement doctrine

### 2. JoD 5:82-86 — Priesthood Over Science (Wilford Woodruff)
**Date:** April 9, 1857
**Priority:** ★★★★★ HIGH
**Category:** Science/Reason Denial

**Key Quotes:**
> "Now, whatever I might have obtained in the shape of learning, by searching and study respecting the arts and sciences of men—whatever principles I may have imbibed during my scientific researches, yet, if the Prophet of God should tell me that a certain principle or theory which I might have learned was not true, I do not care what my ideas might have been, I should consider it my duty, at the suggestion of my file leader, to abandon that principle or theory."

> "Supposing he were to say the principles by which you are governed are not right—that they were incorrect, what would be my duty? I answer that it would be my duty to lay those principles aside, and to take up those that might be laid down by the servants of God."

> "The very moment a man does that, he crosses the path of the servant of God who is set to lead the way to life and salvation."

**Analysis:**
- Explicit subordination of science, reason, and evidence to prophetic authority
- Falsifiable claim: scientific truth can be overruled by prophetic declaration
- Historical context: Pre-Darwinian, but still relevant to modern science conflicts
- Direct contradiction of "truth is truth" apologetics

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ EXCELLENT
- Clear, quotable, shocking to modern sensibilities
- Directly contradicts modern LDS "all truth" teachings
- Easy to contrast with current church statements on science
- Strong narrative: faith vs. reason

### 3. JoD 5:11-14 — Polygamy Defense (George A. Smith)
**Date:** May 31, 1857
**Priority:** ★★★★☆ HIGH
**Category:** Polygamy

**Key Quotes:**
> "In our country, we are determined that every man shall acknowledge and sanction his own blood. We shall not interfere with Vermont, Massachusetts, or Maryland about their immorality; it is their own business, and they must attend to it themselves; but we do not wish to submit to such immoral regulations in Utah."

> "I was talking with a member of Congress, who was very pious, (he was a minister, by the bye,) and he intimated that the doctrine of plurality of wives was so at variance—so grossly at variance with all the civilized world, that it was intolerable to all Christians. I told him that I was surprised at that; 'for,' said I, 'all our Christian friends expect to sit down in the kingdom of God with father Abraham; and he practised Polygamy.' 'Father Abraham,' said he, 'was guilty of a great many eccentric tricks.' I replied, 'Eccentric as he might be, it is in his bosom that all Christians expect to rest.'"

**Analysis:**
- Frames monogamy as "immoral" and causes prostitution
- Claims polygamy prevents illegitimacy and immorality
- Biblical justification via Abraham
- Reversal of moral framework

**Song Potential:** ★★★☆☆ MEDIUM
- Interesting rhetorical reversal
- Not as shocking as other polygamy defenses
- Good for establishing historical context

### 4. JoD 5:336-343 — Warfare Against United States (Brigham Young)
**Date:** October 18, 1857
**Priority:** ★★★★★ HIGH
**Category:** Failed Prophecy / Violent Rhetoric

**Key Quotes:**
> "As the Lord God lives, we will waste our enemies by millions, if they send them here to destroy us, and not a man of us be hurt. That is the method I intend to pursue."

> "Men shall be secreted here and there and shall waste away our enemies, in the name of Israel's God."

> "Should our enemies venture upon violent measures, I design to so manage affairs that none of our boys will be killed."

> "If the Lord sees that we need their substance, he will turn things to that end; and if he designs them to be wiped out, he will either cause them to undertake to come here or will overrule some other plan to accomplish that end."

> "Another year I am going to prepare for the worst, and I want you to prepare to cache our grain and lay waste this Territory; for I am determined, if driven to that extremity, that our enemies shall find nothing but heaps of ashes and ruins."

> "We have sought for peace all the day long; and I have sought for peace with the army now on our borders, and have warned them that we all most firmly believe that they are sent here solely with a view to destroy this people."

**Analysis:**
- Violent prophetic rhetoric during Utah War
- Promised divine protection: "waste enemies by millions...not a man of us be hurt"
- Scorched earth policy threatened
- Historical outcome: Peaceful resolution, no divine intervention, Mormon surrender
- Clear failed prophecy

**Song Potential:** ★★★★☆ HIGH
- Dramatic, violent language
- Clear historical falsification
- Contrasts sharply with peaceful resolution
- Shows prophetic fallibility

### 5. JoD 5:285-292 — Family Government & Women's Subjugation (Erastus Snow)
**Date:** October 4, 1857
**Priority:** ★★★★☆ HIGH
**Category:** Gender Doctrine / Patriarchy

**Key Quotes:**
> "Will she [woman] ever have another [lord]? No, never; and if she ever expects to have another, she has not learned 'Mormonism' aright. She may tear herself loose from him and attach another, but she may have a worse one: she ought to have a worse one."

> "Do you uphold your husband before God as your lord? 'What!—my husband to be my lord?' I ask, Can you get into the celestial kingdom without him? Have any of you been there? You will remember that you never got into the celestial kingdom without the aid of your husband. If you did, it was because your husband was away, and some one had to act proxy for him. No woman will get into the celestial kingdom, except her husband receives her, if she is worthy to have a husband; and if not, somebody will receive her as a servant."

> "If there is no sieve fine enough yet to separate the dross from the wheat of the female portion of this community, I tell you, in the name of Israel's God, there is a fine one preparing, and it will separate the chaff from the wheat from every family in Israel."

> "Where there are disobedient and rebellious children in the midst of Israel, tell me who their father and mother are, and I will point out to you disobedient, rebellious, disaffected parents; and if there is a woman in any family whose children dishonour their father, I will show you a woman that dishonours her husband and shows him disrespect, from which the children take their example."

**Analysis:**
- Explicit patriarchal theology: husband as "lord"
- Women cannot enter heaven without husband's permission
- Women blamed for children's disobedience
- Threatens "sieving" of disobedient women
- Extremely harsh toward women's autonomy

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ EXCELLENT
- Shocking to modern sensibilities
- Clear gender inequality doctrine
- Contradicts current "equal partnership" teachings
- Highly quotable, visceral impact
