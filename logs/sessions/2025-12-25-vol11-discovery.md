## Discovery Log

### CRITICAL Finding #1: JoD 11:266-272 - Delegate Hooper Sermon
**File:** `Delegate_Hooper_Beneficial_Effects_of_Polygamy,_etc.html`
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** August 19, 1866
**Location:** Bowery, Great Salt Lake City
**Reporter:** G. D. Watt

#### Context
- Post-Civil War period (slavery "twin relic" just abolished)
- Federal pressure mounting on polygamy as remaining "twin relic"
- Delegate Hooper just returned from Washington D.C.
- Defensive sermon responding to anti-polygamy legislation

#### Key Findings

##### 1. POLYGAMY REQUIRED FOR EXALTATION (★★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "The only men who become Gods, even the Sons of God, are those who enter into polygamy. Others attain unto a glory and may even be permitted to come into the presence of the Father and the Son; but they cannot reign as kings in glory, because they had blessings offered unto them, and they refused to accept them."

**Citation:** JoD 11:269
**Category:** Polygamy doctrine / Exaltation requirements
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ CRITICAL

**Analysis:**
- CATEGORICAL statement: "The ONLY men who become Gods"
- Creates three-tier salvation system:
  1. Gods/Sons of God = polygamists who "reign as kings in glory"
  2. Lesser glory = non-polygamists who "come into presence" but don't reign
  3. Lower kingdoms = others
- Explicitly ties polygamy to celestial exaltation
- Makes polygamy a REQUIREMENT, not optional
- Impossible to defend as "misunderstood" or "out of context"

**Modern LDS Position:**
- D&C 132 still canonical but no longer practiced
- Modern church: polygamy NOT required for exaltation
- Monogamous couples can achieve celestial glory
- Direct contradiction to Brigham Young's explicit statement

**Apologetic Challenges:**
- Statement is crystal clear and categorical
- No wiggle room for "speaking as a man"
- If prophet can be this wrong about exaltation requirements, what else?
- Creates theological crisis: was Brigham teaching false doctrine?

##### 2. "FAITH IN POLYGAMY" REQUIREMENT (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "I wish to say to you, and all the world, that if you desire with all your hearts to obtain the blessings which Abraham obtained, you will be polygamists at least in your faith, or you will come short of enjoying the salvation and the glory which Abraham has obtained."

**Citation:** JoD 11:269
**Category:** Polygamy doctrine / Faith requirements
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Even if you don't practice polygamy, must believe in it
- Ties to Abraham covenant (still taught today)
- Creates impossible position for modern members
- "At least in your faith" = minimum requirement

##### 3. POLYGAMY FOR RESURRECTION, NOT THIS WORLD (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "What is this order for? It is for the resurrection; it is not for this world. I would not go across this bowery for polygamy, if it only pertained to this world. It is for the resurrection."

**Citation:** JoD 11:269 (later in sermon)
**Category:** Polygamy doctrine / Eternal marriage
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Admits polygamy isn't valuable in mortality
- Entire purpose is resurrection/exaltation
- Reveals motivation: eternal godhood, not earthly benefit
- Contradicts later apologetics about "caring for widows"

##### 4. MARY HAD TWO HUSBANDS (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "This matter was a little changed in the case of the Savior of the world, the Son of the living God. The man Joseph, the husband of Mary, did not, that we know of, have more than one wife, but Mary the wife of Joseph had another husband. On this account infidels have called the Savior a bastard. This is merely a human opinion upon one of the inscrutable doings of the Almighty. That very babe that was cradled in the manger, was begotten, not by Joseph, the husband of Mary, but by another Being. Do you inquire by whom? He was begotten by God our heavenly Father."

**Citation:** JoD 11:268
**Category:** Christology / Polygamy defense
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- God the Father literally physically fathered Jesus
- Mary was "wife" to both Joseph and God
- Uses this to defend polygamy (if God did it...)
- Acknowledges "infidels" call this bastardry
- Modern LDS church avoids this teaching

##### 5. RACIAL CURSE DOCTRINE (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "The Lamanites or Indians are just as much the children of our Father and God as we are. So also are the Africans. But we are also the children of adoption through obedience to the Gospel of his Son. Why are so many of the inhabitants of the earth cursed with a sin of blackness? It comes in consequence of their fathers rejecting the power of the Holy Priesthood, and the law of God. They will go down to death. And when all the rest of the children have received their blessings in the Holy Priesthood, then that curse will be removed from the seed of Cain, and they will then come up and possess the priesthood, and receive all the blessings which we now are entitled to."

**Citation:** JoD 11:272
**Category:** Race / Priesthood ban
**Song Potential:** ★★★

**Analysis:**
- "Cursed with a sin of blackness"
- Attributes to fathers "rejecting the Holy Priesthood"
- Seed of Cain doctrine
- Will receive priesthood LAST (after everyone else)
- Modern church disavowed this in 2013 Gospel Topics essay

##### 6. CHRISTIAN SOCIETY HYPOCRISY (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "Take another view of this subject Let any one of the poor unfortunates, whom those unprincipled scoundrels have, by their hellish arts, seduced from the paths of virtue and honor, make her appearance in a select party where the ladies are fanning the vanity of those wicked men with their unmeaning and insincere adulations, and what would be the consequence? Instead of making the welcome, she would be spurned from their presence; unceremoniously cast out upon the cold world to be crushed down still deeper into the dark depths of crime and degradation, with none to reach forth a saving hand, or shed a tear of sympathy over the dreadful fate of the dishonored and lost one."

**Citation:** JoD 11:267
**Category:** Social criticism / Polygamy defense
**Song Potential:** ★★★

**Analysis:**
- Extensive critique of Victorian sexual hypocrisy
- Men who seduce women welcomed in society
- Women they seduce are cast out
- Uses this to defend polygamy as "more honest"
- Fair point about double standards, but doesn't justify polygamy

##### 7. BACHELOR CLUBS WITH HIRED GIRLS (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "In the large cities of the east—New York, Philadelphia, Washington, Cincinnati, Albany, Boston, etc., clubs are formed, composed of young men of those cities who pass in society as bachelors. Instead of entering into the honorable state of marriage, they hire and support girls. If one of the young men in the club should get honorably married, he is at once rejected, and his name is erased from the roll."

**Citation:** JoD 11:270
**Category:** Social criticism / Polygamy defense
**Song Potential:** ★★

**Analysis:**
- Claims bachelor clubs hire prostitutes/mistresses
- Compares to hiring "horse and chaise at livery stable"
- Likely based on real urban vice, but exaggerated
- Uses to make polygamy look moral by comparison

##### 8. PROPHECY: SEVEN WOMEN TO ONE MAN (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "Two thousand good, God-fearing men should go there, and take to themselves seven wives a-piece. It is written in the Bible, 'and in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, we will eat our own bread, and wear our own apparel; only let us be called by thy name, to take away our reproach.' The Government of the United States do not intend that that prophecy shall be fulfilled, and the Lord Almighty means that it shall. Do you not think that the Lord will conquer? I think he will, and we are helping him."

**Citation:** JoD 11:270
**Category:** Failed prophecy / Polygamy
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Quotes Isaiah 4:1 as prophecy about polygamy
- Claims US Government vs. God on this
- Prophecy did NOT come to pass
- "We are helping him" = Mormons implementing this
- Historical note: Lowell, Massachusetts textile mills employed many single women

##### 9. JOHN WESLEY CAN'T BE A GOD (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "John Wesley, and other great ecclesiastical reformers, could not attain to the same glory, by their own acts, while in the flesh that they would have done had the fullness of the Holy Priesthood been upon the earth in their day, and they had possessed all the glory and power and keys of it, and lived faithful to its requirements all their days. They cannot be crowned as Gods, even the Sons of God."

**Citation:** JoD 11:271
**Category:** Salvation tiers / Protestant reformers
**Song Potential:** ★★

**Analysis:**
- John Wesley cannot become a God
- Even great reformers limited to lesser glory
- Creates hierarchy of kingdoms
- Reveals Mormon exclusivity

### HIGH Finding #2: JoD 11:119-128 - Personality of God
**File:** `Personality_of_God,_etc.html`
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** June 18, 1865
**Location:** Bowery, Great Salt Lake City

#### Key Findings

##### 1. GOD CREATES AS WE CREATE CHILDREN (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "He created man, as we create our children; for there is no other process of creation in heaven, on the earth, in the earth, or under the earth, or in all the eternities, that is, that were, or that ever will be."
**Citation:** JoD 11:122
**Category:** Cosmology / God's nature
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Physical procreation is "the ONLY process of creation"
- Applies to God creating humans
- "In all the eternities" = universal principle
- Implies God has physical body and procreates sexually
- Modern church avoids this explicit teaching

##### 2. ORSON PRATT REBUKED FOR "VAIN PHILOSOPHY" (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "We have persons in this church who have preached and published doctrines on the subject of the Deity which are not true. Elder Orson Pratt has written extensively on the doctrines of this church, and upon this particular doctrine. When he writes and speaks upon subjects with which he is acquainted and understands, he is a very sound reasoner; but when he has written upon matters of which he knows nothing—his own philosophy, which I call vain philosophy—he is wild, uncertain, and contradictory."
**Citation:** JoD 11:121
**Category:** Self-contradiction / Authority
**Song Potential:** ★★★

**Analysis:**
- Prophet publicly rebuking apostle for false doctrine
- Orson Pratt was brilliant theologian, but Brigham says he's "wild, uncertain, and contradictory"
- Shows internal doctrinal conflict among leaders
- If apostles teach false doctrine, how can members trust?

##### 3. MARTIN LUTHER APPROVED POLYGAMY (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "Philip, Landgrave of Hesse, one of the principal lords and princes of Germany, wrote to the great reformer Martin Luther and his associate reformers, anxiously imploring them to grant unto him the privilege of marrying a second wife, while his first wife, the princess, was yet living... [The reformers wrote:] 'So far do we approve it, and in those circumstances only by us specified, for the gospel hath neither recalled nor forbid what was permitted in the law of Moses with respect to the marriage.'"
**Citation:** JoD 11:126
**Category:** Polygamy defense / Historical claim
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Claims Martin Luther, Philip Melancthon, Martin Bucer approved polygamy (1539)
- Cites specific letter and date
- Uses Protestant reformers to legitimize Mormon polygamy
- Historically accurate (letter exists), but selective use
- First wife consented "that the glory of God might be increased"
- Ironic: using Protestant reformers who reject LDS church

##### 4. JOHN WESLEY IN PARADISE, NOT CELESTIAL (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "Where is John Wesley's abode in the other world? He is not where the Father and the Son live, but he is gone into what is called hades, or paradise, or the spirit-world. He did not receive the gospel as preached by Jesus Christ and His apostles; it was not then upon the earth."
**Citation:** JoD 11:124
**Category:** Salvation tiers
**Song Potential:** ★★★

**Analysis:**
- Great reformer excluded from highest heaven
- "Paradise" = intermediate state, not celestial glory
- No priesthood in his era = eternal limitation
- Echoes earlier quote about Wesley not becoming a God

### HIGH Finding #3: JoD 11:198-208 - Marriage: Its Benefits
**File:** `Marriage.html`
**Speaker:** Amasa M. Lyman
**Date:** April 5, 1866
**Location:** Tabernacle, Great Salt Lake City

#### Key Findings

##### 1. HUMAN LIFESPAN: 1000 YEARS TO 25 YEARS (★★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "The Bible tells us that men used to reach a longevity that extended to near a thousand years; this was near six thousand years ago... yet the statistics of to-day relating to the average life of the human race show that it extends to a fraction over a quarter of a century."
**Citation:** JoD 11:203
**Category:** FALSIFIED (scientific claim)
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Claims average lifespan is 25 years in 1866
- Attributes to "physical degeneracy"
- FALSIFIABLE: Actual life expectancy in 1866 US was ~40 years, not 25
- Even with infant mortality, claim is exaggerated
- Modern life expectancy ~78 years = OPPOSITE of predicted trend

##### 2. POLYGAMY PREVENTS HUMAN EXTINCTION (★★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "I have learned to regard it as the world's great necessity—the great necessity of the race to-day, and it is God's greatest necessity in reference to the salvation of the world, and to the development of His universal empire of peace and righteousness over all the earth. Why? Because I have learned that there has been, and that there is still in existence, operating and producing its deadly effects, a system of physical degeneracy that is telling fearfully upon the history of the race... if the race has so degenerated, physically, in five thousand years that the term of a man's life is reduced from near a thousand years to a quarter of a century, the question would be awakened in their minds as to how narrow a margin of time is left for the continuation of our race on the earth before it becomes entirely extinct."
**Citation:** JoD 11:203
**Category:** FALSIFIED + Failed prophecy
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★

**Analysis:**
- "World's great necessity" = polygamy prevents extinction
- "Physical degeneracy" causing human die-off
- Polygamy will "check the physical corruption and decline"
- Goal: humans live "until he attain to the age of a tree"
- FALSIFIED: Human population grew exponentially after 1866, WITHOUT polygamy
- FAILED: Humanity didn't go extinct; lifespan INCREASED dramatically
- Mormon polygamy discontinued 1890, humanity thrived

##### 3. POLYGAMY EXTENDS MARRIAGE TO ALL WOMEN (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "It is simply an extension of pure marriage to all the social elements in the community, man and woman, that is all... If all men and all women in a community were honorably married, you can readily understand one thing, that there would be no prostitution of women in that community, there would be an end of the corruption of man in that community, there would be no illegitimacy there."
**Citation:** JoD 11:205
**Category:** Polygamy doctrine / Social theory
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Polygamy as solution to prostitution and illegitimacy
- Assumes more women than men (demographic claim)
- Goal: every woman married to avoid "wretched existence"
- Paternalistic: women need husbands to avoid degradation
- Ignores: monogamy + celibacy option, women's agency

##### 4. SALVATION ONLY THROUGH MARRIAGE (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "There are certain facts of our existence which we cannot escape from. We are men and women... if we are ever saved in heaven, we shall be saved as men and women... When we count up the men and women that are in the world, we shall find a broad margin more of women than men; and there is a numerical difference in the sexes, as they are developed in our community and every other community. Women must be saved, if the task should devolve on a man to marry two or three of them."
**Citation:** JoD 11:206
**Category:** Polygamy doctrine / Salvation tiers
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- "Saved as men and women" = marriage required for salvation
- Gender imbalance justifies polygamy
- Men's duty to marry multiple women to save them
- Women can't be saved without being married
- Contradicts modern teaching about salvation for unmarried

##### 5. GOD COULDN'T REVEAL IT EARLIER (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "Why did He not introduce it at the very commencement of this work? Because He could not—because our ears were not open to hear it—our prejudices would not allow us to receive it. If I had been talked to about plurality of wives when I was baptized into the Church, the Lord may know, but I do not know what I would have done."
**Citation:** JoD 11:206
**Category:** Revelation / Church history
**Song Potential:** ★★★

**Analysis:**
- Admits polygamy would have destroyed early church
- God "could not" reveal it earlier due to human prejudice
- Implies God's hands tied by human readiness
- Contradicts omnipotence
- Honest admission of strategic timing

##### 6. MONOGAMY FROM ROBBERS AND WOMAN-STEALERS (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "The founders of that ancient empire [Rome] were robbers and women stealers, and made laws favoring monogamy in consequence of the scarcity of women among them, and hence this monogamic system which now prevails throughout all Christendom."
**Citation:** JoD 11:126
**Category:** Historical claim / Polygamy defense
**Song Potential:** ★★★

**Analysis:**
- Romulus/Remus founding myth (Rape of the Sabines)
- Attributes monogamy to woman scarcity, not morality
- Blames monogamy for "prostitution and whoredom"
- Selective history; ignores Greek monogamy, other cultures

### HIGH Finding #4: JoD 11:257-263 - Advice to Lawyers—Royal Polygamy
**File:** `Advice_to_Lawyers,_etc.html`
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** August 12, 1866
**Location:** Bowery, Great Salt Lake City

#### Key Findings

##### 1. PRINCE CHRISTIAN'S MORGANATIC WIFE (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "Prince Christian of Holstein, who has recently married one of the daughters of Victoria, Queen of England, has what is termed a morganatic wife in Germany, by whom he has several children, yet the first lady in Europe, as Queen Victoria is called, with the knowledge of the fact that this Prince, who proposed for her daughter's hand, was the father of several children by a woman, who to all intents and purposes was his wife, accepts him as a suitable match for her youthful daughter."
**Citation:** JoD 11:261
**Category:** Polygamy defense / European hypocrisy
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Real historical event (1866 marriage of Princess Helena)
- "Morganatic wife" = legally recognized but lower status wife
- Uses European royalty to defend Mormon polygamy
- "Tu quoque" argument: They do it too (secretly)
- Actually strengthens case against polygamy (causes scandal)

##### 2. ILLICIT SEX = HELL; CALL HER WIFE = PERSECUTION (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "I would say to Congress that if they will pass a law, making it death for any man to hold illicit intercourse with any woman but his lawful wife, we would meet them half way on that ground... Men may do as they please with women, have numerous children by them, and take as many liberties with them as if they were their wives, and yet not call them wives, and modern society smiles upon them. But whenever a man applies the sacred name of wife to the mother of his children, if he happen to have more than one, then the world professes to be wonderfully shocked at the idea. What inconsistency!"
**Citation:** JoD 11:261
**Category:** Polygamy defense / Social criticism
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Fair point about Victorian hypocrisy
- Mistresses tolerated, plural wives persecuted
- Proposes death penalty for adultery
- Uses double standard to justify polygamy
- Doesn't address: why not monogamy AND fidelity?

##### 3. EVERY GOD RULES OVER HIS OWN PROGENY (★★★★)
**Quote:**
> "The kingdoms he possesses and rules over are his own progeny. Every man who is faithful and gets a salvation and glory, and becomes a king of kings and Lord of Lords, or a father of fathers, it will be by the increase of his own progeny. Our Father and God rules over his own children. Wherever there is a God in all the eternities possessing a kingdom and glory and power it is by means of his own progeny."
**Citation:** JoD 11:262
**Category:** Cosmology / Exaltation doctrine
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Analysis:**
- Gods rule over their literal offspring
- "Father of fathers" through "increase of his own progeny"
- Ties polygamy to godhood (more wives = more children = bigger kingdom)
- Eternal population growth through procreation
- Modern church downplays this explicit teaching

##### 4. TESTATOR SEALED WITH BLOOD (★★★)
**Quote:**
> "It was not necessary for Joseph Smith to be killed, if the people had believed his testimony; but as the testator has sealed it with his blood, his testimony is in force on all the inhabitants of the earth, and wherever it goes those who reject it will be damned."
**Citation:** JoD 11:262
**Category:** Joseph Smith martyrdom / Authority claim
**Song Potential:** ★★★

**Analysis:**
- Joseph's death makes testimony binding
- "All inhabitants of the earth" accountable
- Reject = damned
- Martyrdom as validation (common religious trope)
- Ignores: truth claims need evidence beyond martyrdom

##### 5. APOSTATE HEARTS WILL ACHE (★★)
**Quote:**
> "There is also a man down the street who tried to exhibit the endowments to a party who was here. You will see what becomes of that man. Do not touch him. He has forfeited every right and title to eternal life; but let him alone, and you will see by and by what will become of him. His heart will ache, and so will the heart of every apostate that fights against Zion; they will destroy themselves."
**Citation:** JoD 11:263
**Category:** Apostates / Threats
**Song Potential:** ★★

**Analysis:**
- Veiled threat against man who revealed temple ceremony
- "Do not touch him" (but he'll be cursed)
- "Forfeited every right to eternal life"
- "They will destroy themselves" = hands-off vengeance
- Context: Tension with revealing temple secrets
