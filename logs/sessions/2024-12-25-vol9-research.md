- **Speaker:** Brigham Young
- **Date:** July 6, 1862
- **Pages:** JoD 9:318-323
- **Flag Reason:** Monogamy called evil system invented by Roman brigands, polygamy defense
- **Topics:** Polygamy, Marriage, Monogamy, Roman history
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★★
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "Monogamy, or restrictions by law to one wife, is no part of the economy of Heaven among men. Such a system was commenced by the founders of the Roman empire." (JoD 9:322)
  - "When these robbers founded the city of Rome, it was evident to them that their success in attaining a balance of power with their neighbours, depended upon introducing females into their body politic, so they stole them from the Sabines, who were near neighbours.   - "Thus this monogamic order of marriage, so esteemed by modern Christians as a holy sacrament and divine institution, is nothing but a system established by a set of robbers." (JoD 9:322)
  - "Why do we believe in and practise polygamy? Because the Lord introduced it to his servants in a revelation given to Joseph Smith, and the Lord's servants have always practiced it. 'And is that religion popular in heaven?' It is the only popular religion there" (JoD 9:323)

In doing this, they have undertaken to dictate the Almighty in his revelations to his people" (JoD 9:322-323)
