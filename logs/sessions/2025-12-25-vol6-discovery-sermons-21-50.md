### Sermon #23: "Self-Government" - Orson Hyde (JoD 6:150-158) ★★★★★

**Category:** Joseph Smith Exaltation, Theocracy, Constitution

**KEY FINDING: JOSEPH AS CELESTIAL GATEKEEPER**

1. **"You Cannot Get Past Joseph"** (★★★★★)
   - Quote: "I tell you, Joseph holds the keys, and none of us can get into the celestial kingdom without passing by him. We have not got rid of him, but he stands there as the sentinel, holding the keys of the kingdom of God"
   - Quote: "I tell you, the pinch will be with those that have mingled with us, stood next to us, weighed our spirits, tried us, and proven us: there will be a pinch, in my view, to get past them"
   - Quote: "If brother Joseph is satisfied with you, you may pass. If it is all right with him, it is all right with me"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Joseph Smith as mandatory gatekeeper to celestial kingdom
   - **IRONY:** Modern LDS theology emphasizes Christ, not Joseph, as judge

2. **Joseph Has "Power Over the Nation"** (★★★★★)
   - Quote: "To him that overcometh will I give power over the nations. Did Joseph Smith overcome, even unto death? Yes. What was his glory? One portion of it is—'To him that overcometh will I give power over the nations'"
   - Quote: "When Joseph takes his position, all laws and parts of laws that shall conflict at all with him are hereby repealed, and his word and his law will be the end of strifes"
   - Quote: "They will have to be arraigned under the government and jurisdiction of their murdered victims"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Joseph will rule/judge the nation that killed him
   - **CATEGORY:** Vengeance theology

3. **Joseph's Blood = Nation's Doom** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "Has the nation atoned for that blood? No. Have they offered to do it? They never have...that blood has to be atoned for, and it has to be atoned for upon all those that have said, We are glad of it!"
   - Quote: "They cannot avoid it by going through death. They will have to be arraigned under the government and jurisdiction of their murdered victims"
   - **TONE:** Prophetic vengeance

4. **Constitution Will Be Destroyed** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "The Constitution is well enough...when it has served the end of its creation, the purpose and design of the Almighty are accomplished, and I do not know that he has any more particular use for it"
   - Quote: "The Almighty looks down from heaven and sees it impossible to save the Constitution, to perpetuate it, and cleanse and purify it; for the wickedness of the people is determined to sweep it out of the way"
   - Quote: "Although it was framed by his wisdom and skill, and his power and goodness, yet with as much cheerfulness will it be overthrown as it was ever erected or framed"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Constitution divinely framed but destined for destruction
   - **IRONY:** Modern LDS venerate Constitution; Hyde sees it as temporary/expendable

5. **Hierarchical Gatekeeping** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "If we can secure the goodwill of them that preside over us in our day, they will speak a good word for us. If you, Bishops, have a man in your Wards...the President calls upon you and asks, 'How does that man get along? First rate: he is easily governed and controlled.' Says the President, 'I am glad to hear it'"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Must satisfy bishops to satisfy presidents to satisfy Joseph to enter heaven
   - **IMPLICATIONS:** Multi-tiered judgment by church leaders

**Song Potential:**
- Title: "You Cannot Get Past Joseph"
- Hook: Joseph as sentinel/gatekeeper
- Contrast with modern Christ-centered theology
- Vengeance theme: Joseph judging his murderers

### Sermon #22: "Providence" - Brigham Young (JoD 6:143-149) ★★★★

**Category:** Recreation, Obedience, Bizarre Theology

**KEY FINDINGS:**

1. **Dancing = Serving God** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "I am willing that those who live their religion every day, hour, and minute of their lives to the glory of God shall dance all they wish to; but I have not yet given my consent for any other class to do so"
   - Quote: "If you want to dance and rest your minds, dance. But a man or woman that intends, when they go into a room prepared for music and dancing, to serve the Devil a little while, I would to God that they would go to California"
   - Quote: "If you are told to watch, watch...live your religion—that is, not YOUR religion, but the religion of Jesus Christ"
   - Quote: "You are just as much prepared for a prayer meeting after dancing as ever you were, if you are Saints. If you desire to ask God for anything, you are as well prepared to do so in the dance as in any other place, if you are Saints"
   - **BIZARRE:** Dancing = worship when done "right"
   - **TONE:** Total integration of all life into religious obedience

2. **Why God Allowed Sin** (★★★)
   - Quote: "Were you to summon the priests of the day...going from west to east, and in the south and north as far as man can penetrate...there is not knowledge enough to tell you why God suffered sin to come into the world. You have been told the reason why—that all intelligence must prove facts by their opposite"
   - Quote: "No organized beings are prepared to become associated with or crowned, heirs in the celestial kingdom, until they have passed through these ordeals and have drank of the bitter cup to the dregs, so that they know and understand good from evil"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Opposition necessary for exaltation
   - **CLAIM:** Only LDS know this principle

3. **Eve Not Blamed** (★★★)
   - Quote: "The answer that the woman was found in the transgression, and not the man, came so quickly that it hushed them up at once...Many of even these my sisters who are before me to-day have seen the wisdom that is in the Christian world...and have answered some little question that was a perfect mystery to a priest"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Eve's transgression necessary, not sinful
   - **APOLOGETIC:** Defense of Eve

**Song Potential:**
- Title: "Dance As You're Told"
- Theme: Dancing = serving God if obedient

1. **Dedicate Everything** (★★★★)

   - Quote: "When I get a new wife, I always dedicate her to God, and this is the way I have done for years. I also make a practice of dedicating my children to the Lord, that they may grow up in his wisdom and increase in his power"
   - **BIZARRE:** Dedicating wives like property/tools
   - **TONE:** Matter-of-fact about wife dedication

2. **Holy Ground in England** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "I recollect being in England, in the town of Chadburn, Lancashire; and while there I felt as if my whole system was alive; I felt quickened by some unseen power. Brother Hyde was with me, and he knows that it is true; and I felt to pull off my shoes. We pulled off our hats, for we felt such a sacred and holy feeling. I told brother Joseph about it when I came home; and said he, 'Brother Heber, that place was dedicated by one of the old Prophets, and it will always be filled with the spirit of life.'"
   - Quote: "Talk to me about my having any dubiety on my mind about these things being fulfilled!—I am just as confident of it as I am that I am called to be a saviour of men"

   - **CLAIM:** Joseph Smith confirmed this interpretation

3. **Whores in the Army** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "I understand those officers out yonder have got a good many women with them, and I do not believe there are twenty in the whole camp but what are whores, and they designed to come here to set you a pattern and to moralize this community"
   - **TONE:** Insulting to army camp followers

4. **Fashion = Whoredom** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "You may take all such dresses and new fashions, and inquire into their origin, and you will find, as a general thing, they are produced by the whores of the great cities of the world—London, New York, and from Paris, and from all the Gentile cities"

   - Quote: "Our boys are weakening their backs and their kidneys by girting themselves up as they do; they are destroying the strength of their loins and taking a course to injure their posterity"
   - **TONE:** Crude, attacking women's fashion as prostitute-originated

5. **Brigham Will Swallow Them in Riches** (★★★)
   - Quote: "Our Governor will be rich, and there is not a man on God Almighty's earth that will begin to compare with him; he will swallow them all up in riches and blessings"
   - **CLAIM:** Brigham will be richest man on earth

### Sermon #29: "Idolatry" - Brigham Young (JoD 6:193-199) ★★★★

**Category:** Idolatry, Property, Covetousness

**KEY FINDINGS:**

1. **Idolatry = Worshipping Property** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "I would as soon see a man worshipping a little god made of brass or of wood as to see him worship his property; and he would be as much justified in the sight of God"
   - Quote: "The New Testament says that covetousness is idolatry; therefore, a covetous people is an idolatrous people. Some of you are just as much idolaters as are the heathen, but you do not know it; neither do they realize their idolatry"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Covetousness = idolatry = worshipping brass idols

2. **Nothing to Sacrifice** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "When men and women talk about giving everything for the salvation which they anticipate and live for, behold, they have nothing to give; nor have they anything to do, only to do their duty"
   - Quote: "You have no wife, children, horses, houses, nor land...If God withdraws his sustaining hand, you sink"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Humans own nothing, therefore can sacrifice nothing
   - **IRONY:** Reframes "sacrifice" as doing duty with borrowed property

3. **Idolaters Should Have More Faith in Idols** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "Were I on the islands and seeing the natives bow before their images to be healed, I would say, 'Have faith.' And instead of disfellowshipping a man for worshipping an idol, I would exhort him to have exceeding great faith in his idols, upon the same principle that I exhort the brethren here to have faith in our God. 'And if your idol will not heal you, look beyond to that Being who can.'"
   - **BIZARRE:** Encouraging idol worship as stepping stone
   - **TONE:** Pragmatic about pagan practices

4. **Natives Better Than Christians** (★★★)
   - Quote: "Take the people on the Sandwich and Society Islands, and the natives of these mountains and of North and South America, or of any country where there are natives in their idolatry—those whom we call ignorant, dark, benighted, lost, possessed of little or no knowledge, and let a person understanding the Priesthood visit them, and I will venture to say that he would find more and better traits of the Priesthood of God among them than can be found among the Christians"
   - **CLAIM:** Pagans > Christians in priesthood traits

**Song Potential:**
- Title: "Worship Your Property"
- Theme: Modern idolatry = covetousness
- Ironic: Property worship = brass idol worship

### Sermon #30: "Testimony...Borne to Nations" - Orson Pratt (JoD 6:199-206) ★★★★

**Category:** Failed Prophecy, Warfare, Triumphalism

**KEY FINDINGS:**

1. **Missionaries Recalled = Judgment** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "The Lord moves upon the hearts of our First Presidency to say to the Elders of Israel abroad, 'It is enough: come home. Your testimony is sufficient. The wicked reject it; they fight against it: therefore you may now return to these mountains and valleys'"
   - Quote: "In sending forth a message of this kind to the Elders, what does it show? Why, it closes for the present the testimony of the servants of God, and shows that the warning is sufficient, and that both earth and heaven bear witness that the Gentiles are left without excuse"
   - **DOCTRINE:** Missionary recall = end of Gentile dispensation
   - **FAILED PROPHECY:** Missions resumed shortly after

2. **One Will Chase a Thousand** (★★★★)
   - Quote: "When you go to meet your enemies, they shall be prostrated before you, and you shall overcome them. And as you have overcome their priests by the strong force of argument, so shall you literally and physically put your enemies to flight, and one shall chase a thousand, and two shall put ten thousand to flight"
   - Quote: "I have the same feeling to-day—not that we have the strength to do it ourselves; but I know that God will strengthen us for the work we have to do"
   - **FAILED PROPHECY:** Supernatural military victory predicted

3. **Roll Down to Missouri** (★★★★★)
   - Quote: "When he has done this, we shall then roll down to the borders of Missouri and take possession of our inheritances, from which we have been illegally and unconstitutionally driven"
   - Quote: "Brother Kimball says we could not roll down until we rolled up. But we have been rolling up hill for the past ten years, in fulfilment of the prediction of Isaiah, which says—'O Zion, that bringest glad tidings, get thee up into the high mountain;' and having rolled up for ten years, we shall soon begin to roll down. But I do not think it will take ten years to roll down hill; for we shall be propelled by the power of God, and the work will be hastened"
   - **FAILED PROPHECY:** Saints never "rolled down" to Missouri in triumph
   - **IRONY:** Still waiting 167 years later

4. **Total Degradation of LDS** (★★★)
   - Quote: "There is not a people upon the face of the earth that were ever degraded like the Latter-day Saints. They look upon you worse than they do upon any set of pirates that travel the open seas"
   - **TONE:** Victimhood narrative

**Song Potential:**
- Title: "Roll Down to Missouri"
- Hook: Rolling up 10 years, rolling down faster
- Failed prophecy clear and dated
- Still hasn't happened
