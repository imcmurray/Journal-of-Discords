## Volume 26: Discovery Findings

### Sermon #38: "Remarks by President John Taylor" (JoD 26:346-363)

**Metadata:**
- **Speaker:** John Taylor (Church President)
- **Date:** February 20, 1884
- **Setting:** High Council trial - Deseret Hospital case (Ellen B. Ferguson)
- **Type:** Church court proceeding with extensive jurisprudential discourse
- **Length:** 18 pages

**Priority Assessment:** ★★★ MEDIUM (Church governance, not core doctrine)

**Content Summary:**

This is NOT a doctrinal sermon but an extended church trial proceeding with Taylor's remarks on justice, authority, and church governance. The case involved Dr. Ellen B. Ferguson (hospital resident surgeon) accused of morphine use, theft, and being "false."

**Key Themes:**
1. **Church Governance Principles**
   - Priesthood authority vs. administrative power
   - "No power or influence can or ought to be maintained by virtue of the Priesthood, only by persuasion, by long suffering, by gentleness and meekness"
   - Extensive discussion of constitutional vs. celestial law

2. **Legal/Political Philosophy**
   - Comparison of government types (monarchies, republics, oligarchies)
   - Martial law vs. civil law examples (General Jackson, Fitz John Porter)
   - Natural law vs. human law distinctions

3. **Celestial Law Definition**
   - "What is this celestial law? The celestial law above referred to is absolute submission and obedience to the law of God"
   - "Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven" - this is celestial law

4. **Crime Statistics (Defense)**
   - Utah crime rates: 20-80 violators among Gentiles to 1 among Mormons
   - Springville: 45% of outsiders violating law
   - Used as defense against "corrupt reformers" charge

5. **Trial Outcome**
   - Charges against Ferguson NOT sustained
   - Board acted "wisely" in asking her to resign
   - Call for reconciliation and unity

**Notable Quotes:**

**On Authority:**
> "In all the universe there is no space where there is no kingdom, and where there is no kingdom, there is no law; and all things that are governed by law are preserved by law." (JoD 26:350)

**On Gospel vs. World Law:**
> "He that keepeth the laws of God, hath no need to break the laws of the land." (JoD 26:352)

**On Constitutional Violation:**
> "If they are violative of the constitution, then the compact between the rulers and the ruled is broken and the obligation ceases to be binding." (JoD 26:352)

**On High Council Duty:**
> "This High Council has no right to condone sin. This is an error that people fall into. If men transgress and violate the laws of God, they have no right but to deal with them according to the law of God." (JoD 26:360)

**Discovery Assessment:**

**Song Potential:** ★ LOW
- Primarily administrative/judicial
- Not core doctrine or prophecy
- Limited broader application

**Historical Interest:** ★★★★ HIGH
- Unique window into church courts
- Taylor's jurisprudential philosophy
- Gender dynamics (female doctor case)
- Shows church governance in practice

**Categories:**

## Volume 25: Discovery Findings

### Sermon #1: "Design of God in Relation to the Earth and Its Inhabitants" (JoD 25:303-317)

**Metadata:**
- **Speaker:** John Taylor (Church President)
- **Date:** October 6-7, 1884 (Semi-Annual Conference)
- **Setting:** Tabernacle, Salt Lake City
- **Length:** 14 pages

**Priority Assessment:** ★★★★★ EXTREME

**Content Summary:**

This is THE definitive plural marriage defense sermon. Church President John Taylor, speaking from the prophetic office 6 years before the Manifesto, makes absolute, unequivocal statements that plural marriage will NEVER be abandoned.

**Key Themes:**

1. **FAILED PROPHECY - Core Material (★★★★★)**

**The "Never Tone Down" Prophecy:**
> "God has given us a revelation in regard to celestial marriage. I did not make it. He has told us certain things pertaining to this matter, and **they would like us to tone that principle down and change it and make it applicable to the views of the day. This we cannot do; nor can we interfere with any of the commands of God** to meet the persuasions or behests of men. I cannot do it, and will not do it." (JoD 25:309)

> "**If God has introduced something for our glory and exaltation, we are not going to have that kicked over by any improper influence, either inside or outside of the Church of the living God. We will stand by the principles of eternal truth; living we will proclaim them, and dying we will be true to them**, and after death will live again in their enjoyment in the eternal worlds." (JoD 25:309-310)

> "Some folks think that we polygamists are very much indebted to our brethren who are monogamists to help to steady the ark... Well, I won't tell you all I think about some of these things, but I do think we are all of us dependent upon God our Heavenly Father, and if He don't take care of us we shall not be taken care of." (JoD 25:310)

**Context of Prophecy:**
- October 1884 - Height of federal prosecution
- Edmunds Act actively enforced
- Taylor speaking as Church President
- 6 years before 1890 Manifesto
- COMPLETE REVERSAL occurred

2. **Polygamy vs. Prostitution Defense**

**The Test Oath Perversion:**
> "The Governor turned legislator, added to this law, and inserted in a test oath to officials, the following words regarding cohabitation, 'in the marriage relation;' **thus plainly and definitely sanctioning prostitution**, without any law of the United States, or any authority." (JoD 25:311)

**Kate Flint Incident:**
> "A son of Mayor Little was one of the election registrars... because some years before any law of this kind was in operation... he had practiced plural marriage, his son was obliged to tell his father that he could not register. Shortly afterwards **a notorious courtesan known as Kate Flint, with some of the inmates of her bagnio, drove up and requested to be registered. 'Why, of course.'**" (JoD 25:312)

**The Mistress vs. Wives:**
> "Another non-Mormon came along to one of the other registration officers, and on partly reading the oath... said, 'I am afraid I can't take that?' 'Why can't you take it?' Well, he was an honest man among the Gentiles; he did not like to foreswear himself; so he said, **'I have a wife, and then I keep a mistress.' 'Oh, well,' says the man, 'read on a little further.' He read on until he came to the words, 'in the marriage relation.' 'Oh, well, yes, I can take that,' he said, and registered.**" (JoD 25:312)

3. **Crime Statistics Defense**

**Utah Penitentiary (1883):**
- Total: 46 prisoners
- Non-Mormons: 33 (from 30,000 population = 1 in 909)
- Mormons: 13 (from 130,000 population = 1 in 10,000)
- **Ratio: 10x more crime among non-Mormons**

**Salt Lake City Arrests (1883):**
- Mormons: 150 arrests (from 19,000 = 1 in 126)
- Non-Mormons: 1,559 arrests (from 6,000 = 1 in 4)
- **Ratio: 25%+ arrest rate for non-Mormons**

**Territory-wide (1882):**
- Mormon population: 78%
- Non-Mormon population: 22%
- Mormon arrests: 300 (1/8 of total)
- Non-Mormon arrests: 1,898 (7/8 of total)

> "**They have twenty criminals right among us to our one, and more than that.**" (JoD 25:315)

4. **Abortion/Infanticide Polemic**

Taylor reads extensive quotes from non-Mormon sources:

**Dr. Nathan Allen (Lowell):**
> "Nowhere in the history of the world was the practice of abortion so common as in this country; and... in New England alone, many thousands of abortions are procured annually." (JoD 25:316)

**Dr. Reamy (Ohio State Medical Society):**
> "We have become **a nation of murderers**." (JoD 25:316)

**Bishop Coxe (Episcopal Church of NY):**
> "**The blood guiltiness of ante-natal infanticide**... they who do such things cannot inherit eternal life. If there be a special damnation for those who shed innocent blood, what must be the portion of those who have no mercy upon their own flesh." (JoD 25:316)

**Taylor's Response:**
> "These are the people that are coming here to reform us, and are so disgusted with our corruptions... I am sorry to know that these things are as they are; but these are facts, and **we do not feel very much honored with the association of such people**." (JoD 25:317)

5. **Temple Worthiness Standards**

**Command to Bishops:**
> "What shall the Presidents of Stakes do? Look after our Stakes, and **if you find adulterers or adulteresses among you, don't permit them to go into the temples of God**; for we won't have such people; they cannot be sanctioned by us, nor have our fellowship." (JoD 25:313)

> "I speak of it that the Presidents of Stakes and the Bishops may be apprised of these things. And some of these people would try to pass by the Bishops, and then by the Presidents of Stakes, and then by the President of the Church, and **crawl with all their slime and damnable hypocrisy into the Temples of the living God**." (JoD 25:318)

### Sermon #2: "A Peculiar Work" (JoD 25:268-275)

**Metadata:**
- **Speaker:** George Q. Cannon (First Presidency)
- **Date:** August 31, 1884
- **Setting:** Tabernacle, Salt Lake City (Sunday afternoon)
- **Length:** 8 pages

**Priority Assessment:** ★★★★★ EXTREME

**Content Summary:**

George Q. Cannon, speaking as member of the First Presidency, makes the single most important failed prophecy statement in all 26 volumes of the Journal of Discourses.

**THE CORE PROPHECY:**

> "God has given unto us the assurance that **this Church, this work that He has established, shall never be given into the hands of another people**, but that it shall stand forever, and it shall go forward accomplishing His designs, until it shall fill the whole earth." (JoD 25:271, emphasis added)

**Full Context:**
> "There is this consolation, however connected with the work with which we are identified—that God has made promises unto us that it shall never be given into the hands of another people. The Apostles looked forward to the time when there would be a great falling away, and the man of sin be revealed, and they warned the church in their day of that falling away. But **God has given unto us the assurance that this Church, this work that He has established, shall never be given into the hands of another people, but that it shall stand forever**, and it shall go forward accomplishing His designs, until it shall fill the whole earth. This is a glorious promise given unto us, and to our children, and we can rely upon it." (JoD 25:271)

**The Irony:**
- Spoken August 31, 1884
- **1890 Manifesto** abandoned polygamy (6 years later)
- **1904 Second Manifesto** enforced prohibition
- **Modern LDS Church** excommunicates polygamists
- **Fundamentalist Mormons** who practice Vol 25 teachings are "another people"

**Additional Key Themes:**

1. **Persecution Narrative:**
> "In espousing the Gospel of Jesus Christ the Latter-day Saints were taught... that its espousal might cost us our lives, that it might cost us everything we held dear upon the earth, and thus far in the progress of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, we have not been disappointed." (JoD 25:268)

2. **Peace Doctrine (Revelation cited):**

Cannon reads extensively from D&C 98:

> "If that enemy shall escape my vengeance, that he be not brought into judgment before me, then ye shall see to it that ye warn him in my name, that he come no more upon you, neither upon your family, even your children's children unto the third and fourth generation; And then if he shall come upon you, or your children, or your children's children until the third and fourth generation; I have delivered thine enemy into thine hands." (JoD 25:270)

**Emphasis on non-revenge:**
> "There is no room for revenge in the heart of a true Latter-day Saint. God designs that we shall be a peaceful people, a people who shall love and cultivate peace." (JoD 25:272)

3. **Constitutional Collapse Prophecy:**

> "The time will come when **constitutional government will be overthrown upon this land**, and that it will be the province of the Latter-day Saints to uphold those principles which God inspired the founders of this government to embody in the Constitution." (JoD 25:276)

> "When assassination can be justified, assassination of men peaceably worshipping their God, offending no one, committing no violation of law or of good order; when they can be shot down cruelly and inhumanly, and their murderers be justified for the deed, **it seems as though the time when constitutional principles would fail, is near at hand**." (JoD 25:276)

4. **Civil Strife Coming:**

> "For the day will come, and it is not far distant, when in our own nation, **there will be civil strife, there will be domestic broils, there will be a withdrawal of peace**, and men will yet have to come to the Latter-day Saints for that peace and that freedom from civil strife that cannot be found elsewhere." (JoD 25:276)

**Song Candidate:** "Never Given to Another People"
- **Hook:** "This Church shall never be given into the hands of another people"
- **Timeline:** August 1884 → September 1890 = 6 years to failure
- **Speaker:** First Presidency member (highest authority)

- **Angle:** "Six years later..." - undeniable timeline

**Historical Significance:** ★★★★★ CRITICAL
- Single most important failed prophecy in JoD
- First Presidency authority
- Explicit, unambiguous language
- Short timeframe to failure
- Modern church prosecutes those who believe this prophecy

**Categories:**
- Failed Prophecy (PRIMARY - TIER 1)

**Apologetic Defenses to Counter:**

1. **"Church organization never given to others, just specific doctrines"**
   - COUNTER: Polygamy was THE test of loyalty in 1884

2. **"Church still stands"**

### Sermon #3: "Object of Assembling" (JoD 25:84-96)

**Metadata:**
- **Speaker:** John Taylor (Church President)
- **Date:** February 10, 1884
- **Setting:** Assembly Hall, Salt Lake City (Sunday afternoon)
- **Length:** 13 pages

**Priority Assessment:** ★★★★ HIGH

**Content Summary:**

An expansive defense of gathering, with extensive discussion of the "twin relics" (slavery and polygamy), historical persecution, and church principles.

**Key Themes:**

1. **"Twin Relics of Barbarism"**

> "The republicans long ago put into their platform that there were two twin relics that had to be moved out of the way—**the one was slavery, and the other polygamy**. They have removed slavery out of the way, but polygamy seems to be rather a hard nut for them to crack." (JoD 25:94)

**Irony:** Both defended with same Biblical arguments
- Abraham had slaves and multiple wives
- Biblical patriarchs practiced both
- Both eventually abandoned by church

2. **Persecution History - Personal Testimony**

**Carthage Jail:**
> "I was in a worse scrape in Carthage jail, when Joseph and Hyrum were killed—penned up in a room and attacked by a blackened mob. **I had to stand at the door and ward off the guns** while they were trying to shoot us, and we without arms, and under the protection of the Governor of the State." (JoD 25:91)

> "That pledge was violated by the murder of Joseph and Hyrum Smith in Carthage jail, and **I myself received five balls in my person**; but then I am here yet." (JoD 25:91)

**Missouri:**
> "I was driven from Missouri years ago, together with the whole people. We were robbed and pillaged... the legislature actually made an appropriation for us... of $2,000... They stole a lot of hogs from them, and then turned in those hogs to make up the appropriation... **They stole the hogs from one portion of our people, and then gave them to another**." (JoD 25:90-91)

**Nauvoo:**
> "When I left Nauvoo, I left a very good house, very well furnished. I left carpets on the floors, stoves in the rooms, crockery ware in the cupboards, and I got into my carriage, with my family, and left it **to seek that protection among the Red Indians, that we could not find among the people** who lived in this boasted land of the free and home of the brave." (JoD 25:95)

3. **Abraham's Bosom Argument**

> "Men assume to judge the acts of others... They would like to get into Abraham's bosom—that is most of the Christians of the present day would like to have a place in Abraham's bosom. Would you? Would you really? Are there any of that class here that would like to go unto Abraham's bosom? Why, should you have your wish, **when you woke up you would find you were in the bosom of a polygamist, and would not that be very horrible?** But that would be the fact." (JoD 25:89)

4. **Crime Statistics (Repeated from earlier sermon)**

**Salt Lake City Criminal Record (1882):**
- Population: 25,000 (19,000 Mormon, 6,000 non-Mormon)
- Arrests: 1,561 total
  - Mormon: 188

**Retail establishments:**
- 66 beer/liquor retailers: 60 non-Mormon, 6 "nominal" Mormon
- 15 billiard/bowling: ALL non-Mormon
- 7 gambling houses: ALL non-Mormon
- 6 brothels: ALL non-Mormon (31 inmates)

> "Mr. Barclay, a member of the British Parliament, who lately visited us, writes... 'These figures conclusively prove that **the Mormons are a sober, law-abiding people, and singularly free from the grosser forms of vice**; whatever may be alleged by ignorant or prejudiced enemies.'" (JoD 25:87)

5. **Kingdom Will Never Be Given**

> "**This Kingdom will never be given into the hands of another people. It will grow and spread and increase, and no man living can stop its progress.** Hence I feel quite easy, as I said before, for the Lord reigns, and let the people rejoice." (JoD 25:96)
