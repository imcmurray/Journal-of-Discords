## Known Priority Target: CONFIRMED ★★★★★

**JoD 10:110** — Interracial marriage = death penalty teaching
- **Sermon:** "The Persecutions of the Saints, etc." by Brigham Young (pages 104-111)
- **Date:** March 8, 1863
- **CONFIRMED:** Contains explicit death penalty declaration for interracial marriage
- **Priority:** HIGH ★★★★★
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★★ (Extreme racial teaching, indefensible)

### Key Quote (THE PRIMARY TARGET):

**JoD 10:110 (line 74 of HTML file):**
> "Shall I tell you the law of God in regard to the African race? If the white man who belongs to the chosen seed mixes his blood with the seed of Cain, the penalty, under the law of God, is death on the spot. This will always be so."

**Context:**
- Brigham is discussing slavery, abolitionists, and the Civil War
- He positions himself as "neither abolitionist nor pro-slavery"
- Then delivers this teaching as "the law of God"
- States "This will always be so" — claiming permanence
- Followed by general condemnation of nations for transgressing God's laws

**Additional racial content in same sermon:**
> "The nations of the earth have transgressed every law that God has given, they have changed the ordinances and broken every covenant made with the fathers" (JoD 10:110)

> "If the Government of the United States, in Congress assembled, had the right to pass an anti-polygamy bill, they had also the right to pass a law that slaves should not be abused as they have been; they had also a right to make a law that negroes should be used like human beings, and not worse than dumb brutes. For their abuse of that race, the whites will be cursed, unless they repent." (JoD 10:110)

**Apologetic challenges:**
- The death penalty statement is unambiguous
- Presented as "the law of God" not personal opinion
- No wiggle room for context — it's stated as absolute divine law
- "This will always be so" claims eternal permanence
- Directly contradicts modern LDS teaching allowing interracial marriage
