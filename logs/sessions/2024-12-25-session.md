### Analysis Complete: Adam-God Sermon
**Agent:** Analysis (direct)
**Input:** `sources/html/vol-01/self_government.html` (JoD 1:46-53)
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** April 9, 1852

**Key Findings:**

1. **Adam is God doctrine stated explicitly:**
   > "He is MICHAEL, the Archangel, the ANCIENT OF DAYS!...HE is our FATHER and our GOD, and the only God with whom WE have to do."

2. **Adam brought Eve as "one of his wives":**
   > "When our father Adam came into the garden of Eden, he came into it with a celestial body, and brought Eve, one of his wives, with him."

3. **Jesus NOT begotten by Holy Ghost — begotten by Adam (God):**
   > "When the Virgin Mary conceived the child Jesus, the Father had begotten him in his own likeness. He was not begotten by the Holy Ghost."
   > "Jesus, our elder brother, was begotten in the flesh by the same character that was in the garden of Eden"

4. **Emphatic universal claim:**
   > "Every man upon the earth, professing Christians or non-professing, must hear it, and will know it sooner or later."

5. **Claims this is biblical but hidden:**
   > "In the Bible, you have read the things I have told you to-night; but you have not known what you did read."

**Category:** DISAVOWED
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★
**Notes:** This is the foundational Adam-God sermon. The Church has since disavowed this doctrine. Bruce R. McConkie called it "deadly heresies." Perfect for song development.
