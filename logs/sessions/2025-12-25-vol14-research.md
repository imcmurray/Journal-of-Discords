## Key Findings

### PRIMARY THEME: Polygamy Defense & Federal Opposition

Volume 14 is dominated by polygamy apologetics during the period of increasing federal pressure (1869-1872). This is a MAJOR finding.

#### Finding #1: "Saving Women" Narrative
**Sermon:** Character and Condition (JoD 14:37-45)
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** May 8, 1870

**Quote:**
> "Our doctrine and practice make and preserve life; theirs destroy it. Which is the best, saying nothing about revelation, which is the best in a moral point of view, to preserve or to destroy the life which God designs to bring upon the earth."

**Context:** Brigham argues polygamy saves women from "destruction of pre-natal life" (abortion/infanticide), while monogamy leads to death.

**Significance:**
- Moral superiority claim
- Attacks monogamy as murderous
- Ignores actual harm to polygamous wives
- Song potential: ★★★★★

---

#### Finding #2: Legal Defiance
**Sermon:** Obedience, etc. (JoD 14:119-122)
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** May 21, 1871

**Quote:**
> "The inquiry among many, and especially among our political friends, is, 'What are you going to do? Are you going to observe the law against plurality of wives, or are you going to obey the revelation?' We have obeyed the revelation thus far, and still live; that I can say, and perhaps that is enough."

**Context:** Direct challenge to anti-polygamy legislation. Brigham refuses to commit to obeying federal law.

**Quote 2:**
> "Again, a gentleman said to me, the other day, 'What are you going to do with the anti-polygamy law?' I replied, 'Nothing at all, we mind our own business, and I hope everybody else will.'"

**Significance:**
- Open defiance of federal law
- Claims revelation supersedes legislation
- "Still live" - threat or boast?
- Song potential: ★★★★

---

#### Finding #3: "We Will Kill You"
**Sermon:** The Gospel of Jesus Christ (JoD 14:45-58)
**Speaker:** George Q. Cannon
**Date:** August 15, 1869

**Quote:**
> "We are solving the problem that is before the world to-day, over which they are pretending to rack their brains. I mean the 'Social Problem.' We close the door on one side, and say that whoredoms, seductions and adulteries must not be committed amongst us, and we say to those who are determined to carry on such things we will kill you; at the same time we open the door in the other direction and make plural marriage honorable."

**Context:** Cannon argues polygamy solves the "social problem" by providing marriage option and threatening death for adultery.

**Significance:**
- Explicit death threat for adultery
- Blood atonement implication
- "Pure and virtuous community" claim immediately after death threat
- Song potential: ★★★★★

**Quote 2:**
> "Why go to the trouble and expense and incur the odium of sustaining wives and children merely to gratify licentiousness, when we could do it to the fullest extent, on the popular plan, without incurring odium or assuming responsibility and care? Read the records of New York, Washington, Chicago... men can gratify their lustful desire without incurring odium. They can even destroy females by the thousands in the gratification of their sensual appetites, but because the Latter-day Saints choose to marry them, to make women and their children respected and honorable, all hell is moved against them."

**Significance:**
- Moral superiority rhetoric
- Contrasts polygamy with prostitution/mistresses
- Ignores coercion in polygamous marriages
- Song potential: ★★★★

---

#### Finding #4: Misdirection on Persecution
**Sermon:** Political Parties and Christian Sects (JoD 14:156-163)
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** June 4, 1871

**Quote:**
> "When we left the confines of what is called civilization the doctrine of plurality of wives was not known by the world, and was not taught by us, and was known only to a very few members of our Church; but since we have declared this revelation we have dwelt in peace and safety, so we were not persecuted for that, sure. We did not leave Ohio, Missouri, Illinois, or any other State or neighborhood within the confines of civilization for believing in the doctrine of a plurality of wives... I want all our young, and all who believe the Gospel and all who do not believe it, to know that we were driven from our homes in consequence of our belief in celestial marriage. I want all to know that we were driven for believing in the Old and New Testament; not for believing in the Book of Mormon, but in the Bible, and then practising it in our lives."

**Significance:**
- Historical misdirection (polygamy was practiced secretly before exodus)
- Claims persecution for "Bible belief," not polygamy
- Sets up false victim narrative
- Song potential: ★★★★

**Quote 2:**
> "My conscience! what a generation of gentlemen and ladies we have! Their thoughts and reflections are continually about wives and husbands... If it is necessary to take a wife, take one; if it is necessary to have a husband, have one. If it is necessary to have two wives, take them. If it is right, reasonable and proper and the Lord permits a man to take half a dozen wives, take them; but if the Lord says let them alone, let them alone."

**Significance:**
- Deflection: accuses critics of obsession
- Minimizes polygamy as "if necessary"
- Defensive tone reveals sensitivity
- Song potential: ★★★

---

#### Finding #5: God Progression Doctrine
**Sermon:** Progression, etc. (JoD 14:300-309)
**Speaker:** Lorenzo Snow
**Date:** January 14, 1872

**Quote:**
> "Let this same mind be in you which was in Christ Jesus, who, finding himself in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God."

**Quote 2:**
> "We were born in the image of God our Father; he begot us like unto himself. There is the nature of deity in the composition of our spiritual organization; in our spiritual birth our Father transmitted to us the capabilities, powers and faculties which he himself possessed, as much so as the child on its mother's bosom possesses, although in an undeveloped state, the faculties, powers and susceptibilities of its parent."

**Context:** Lorenzo Snow expounding the doctrine that humans can become gods (deification theology), later formulated as "As man is, God once was; as God is, man may become."

**Significance:**
- Core doctrine now disavowed by modern LDS church
- Contradicts current LDS teaching on nature of God
- Shows evolution of doctrine
- Song potential: ★★★★
