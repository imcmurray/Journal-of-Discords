**Source:** JoD 24:227-235, John Taylor, "The Work of God"

**The Claim:**

- Taylor became church president (1880-1887), died in hiding from federal authorities
- Creates massive doctrinal contradiction with modern church

### FINDING 2: Calling Joseph Smith III "Ignorant or a Liar" (★★★★★)

**Source:** JoD 24:158-166, Erastus Snow, "Leaders of the Church Inspired"

**The Claim:**
> "Now, in regard to those who are seeking for an excuse to reject plural marriage and are inclined to receive the statement of young Joseph Smith, I wish to say that **I know that Joseph Smith is entirely ignorant of what he says, or he is a liar; for I know that he does not speak the truth.**"

**Context:**

- Led Reorganized Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints (RLDS, now Community of Christ)

**Additional Claims:**
> "I know that he [Joseph Smith] gave to me my second wife and assigned his brother, Hyrum, to seal her to me"

> "there is more real happiness in serving God and abiding in His law, and submitting to all its conditions and requirements than there can be in taking an opposite course"

> "there is treble the genuine comfort and happiness in those families who enter into this order and abide in it, than is to be found in the same number of families in monogamy"

**Why It Matters:**
- Direct personal attack on prophet's son
- RLDS/Community of Christ position eventually vindicated by historical evidence showing Joseph Smith's polygamy involvement was complex and contested
- Shows how divisive polygamy was even within Smith family
- Apostle publicly calling prophet's son a liar

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ - "Ignorant or a Liar"

### FINDING 3: Failed Prophecy - Return to Jackson County (★★★★★)

**Source:** JoD 24:20-32, Orson Pratt, "Exhortation From Isaiah"

**The Prophecy:**
> "We expect to go there [Jackson County, Missouri] just as much as we expect the sun will rise and set. We have no other expectation. We expect to return there just as much as the Jews expect to return to old Jerusalem in the latter days."

**Specific Details:**

**Temple Architecture:**
> "We expect to build a temple different from all other temples in some respects...There will be **24 different compartments in the Temple** that will be built in Jackson County. The names of these compartments were given to us some 45 or 46 years ago; the names we still have, and when we build these 24 rooms, **in a circular form and arched over the centre**, we shall give the names to all these different compartments just as the Lord specified through Joseph Smith."

**Eternal Materials:**
> "I mean to say that not only the Temple, but all the buildings that shall be built round about that Temple, and the city that will be built round about it, which will be called the New Jerusalem, **will be built of materials that never will decay.**"

> "God will be there. So He will be in the temple of Zion on this continent, and by His power, by His laws—which are superior to all those grosser laws of nature—**He will preserve both of these cities**, one on the western hemisphere, and one on the eastern hemisphere, **from any decay whatever.**"

**Divine Light:**
> "The light will shine so conspicuously from that city, extending to the very heavens, that it will in reality be like unto a city set upon a hill that cannot be hid"

> "when they [kings and nobles] see the light shining forth like the northern lights in the arctic regions, illuminating the whole face of the heavens—when they see this light shining forth long before they reach the city, fear will take hold of them"

**Timeline:**
> "We may give them a chance to see" [speaking of returning to Missouri]

> "Have you ever seen any other feeling on the part of the Latter-day Saints, only to promote peace wherever they may settle?"

**Why It Matters:**
- Sermon delivered October 26, 1879 (145+ years ago)
- Pratt claims Saints will return to Jackson County "just as much as we expect the sun will rise and set"
- Detailed architectural specifications for temple that was never built
- Church **sold** Jackson County temple lot in 2023 to Community of Christ
- Church headquarters remain in Salt Lake City with no plans to relocate
- Clear, specific, testable prophecy that failed

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ - "We Shall Return to Jackson County"

## Historical Context: The Edmunds Act Era

### Background

**March 22, 1882:** Congress passes Edmunds Act
- Made "unlawful cohabitation" a misdemeanor
- Punishable by $300 fine and 6 months imprisonment
- Barred polygamists from voting, holding office, or jury service
- Much more aggressive than previous Morrill Act (1862)

**Volume 24 Timeline:**
- Sermons delivered 1879-1883
- Most are from 1883 (peak enforcement)
- Leaders are responding to real, immediate threat
- Many leaders went "underground" to avoid arrest
- John Taylor died in hiding (1887)

### Why This Volume Is So Valuable

1. **Unfiltered defiance:** Leaders not yet willing to compromise
2. **Theological ultimatums:** Polygamy presented as essential, non-negotiable
3. **Personal testimonies:** Direct claims of practicing polygamy, receiving revelation
4. **Failed predictions:** Claims God would destroy persecutors, Saints would prevail
5. **7-year window:** Only 7 years before total reversal (1890 Manifesto)

### Speakers' Fates

**John Taylor (President 1880-1887):**
- Preached absolute defiance in this volume
- Went into hiding 1885
- Died in hiding July 1887
- Never compromised on polygamy
- Succeeded by Wilford Woodruff

**Wilford Woodruff (President 1889-1898):**
- Testified polygamy essential in this volume (1883)
- Became president 1889
- Issued Manifesto ending polygamy September 1890
- Claimed God told him to do it
- Cognitive dissonance: "essential" doctrine abandoned

**George Q. Cannon (1st Counselor):**
- 9 sermons in this volume defending polygamy
- Went to prison for unlawful cohabitation 1888
- Served 5 months in Utah Penitentiary
- Remained in First Presidency through Manifesto

## Apologetic Responses to Anticipate

### For Polygamy Claims

**Apologetic:** "Polygamy was commanded for a time, then ended when no longer needed"

**Counter:**
- Taylor, Snow, Woodruff all claimed it was **essential for exaltation**
- "Kingdom could not go one step further" without it
- "If ye abide not that covenant, then are ye damned"
- These aren't "for a time" statements - they're eternal requirements
- If it was essential in 1883, why not in 1893?

**Apologetic:** "Leaders were speaking as men, not prophets"

**Counter:**
- These are official conference sermons, published in official record
- Speakers explicitly claim revelation and divine authority
- John Taylor was **President of the Church** giving official doctrine
- If president of church can be wrong about essential doctrine, what else?

**Apologetic:** "God can change commandments (like law of Moses)"

**Counter:**
- Law of Moses explicitly temporary, preparatory (stated in scripture)
- Polygamy presented as "new and **everlasting** covenant"
- D&C 132: "everlasting" covenant, for "fullness of my glory"
- "Everlasting" doesn't mean "for 60 years"

### For Jackson County Prophecy

**Apologetic:** "We don't know God's timeline, prophecy could still happen"

**Counter:**
- Pratt said "just as much as we expect the sun will rise and set"
- This isn't ambiguous or open-ended
- Church **sold the temple lot** in 2023
- If prophecy was real, why sell the land?
- 145+ years is plenty of time to admit it failed

**Apologetic:** "Prophecies are conditional on faithfulness"

**Counter:**
- Pratt said "we expect to go there" with certainty
- No conditions mentioned in sermon
- Implies LDS unfaithful for 145+ years?
- Temple details (24 rooms, sapphires) suggest divine blueprint, not conditional promise

### For "Ignorant or a Liar" Attack

**Apologetic:** "Joseph Smith III was wrong about his father"

**Counter:**
- Maybe, but Snow's attack is still inappropriate and harsh
- Calling prophet's son a liar in official sermon shows character
- Joseph III was 11 when father died - Snow admits this
- Historical record on Joseph Smith's polygamy is complex
- Snow's certainty doesn't match historical complexity
