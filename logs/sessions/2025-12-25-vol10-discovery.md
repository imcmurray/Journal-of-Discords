## Discovery Log

### SERMON #25: "The Persecutions of the Saints...The Laws of God Relative to the African Race"
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** March 8, 1863
**Pages:** JoD 10:104-111
**Reporter:** G. D. Watt
**Status:** Complete - MULTIPLE HIGH-VALUE FINDINGS

#### Context
Civil War era sermon. Defensive tone about loyalty/secession accusations. Mormon Battalion history. Mountain Meadows Massacre defense. Transitions into racial theology.

#### PRIMARY FINDING: Death Penalty for Interracial Marriage (★★★★★)

**Quote (JoD 10:110):**
> "Shall I tell you the law of God in regard to the African race? If the white man who belongs to the chosen seed mixes his blood with the seed of Cain, the penalty, under the law of God, is death on the spot. This will always be so."

**Analysis:**
- Claims divine authority ("the law of God")
- Prescribes DEATH PENALTY for interracial marriage
- Claims eternal permanence ("This will always be so")
- Completely contradicts modern Church teaching
- Zero apologetic defense possible
- Ready for immediate song development (Song title: "Death on the Spot")

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

---

#### ADDITIONAL FINDING: Mountain Meadows Massacre Defense (★★★★)

**Quote (JoD 10:108-109):**
> "In 1857...a company of emigrants were traveling on the south route to California. Nearly all of that company were destroyed by the Indians. That unfortunate affair has been laid to the charge of the whites...I told Governor Cumming that if he would take an unprejudiced judge into the district where that horrid affair occurred, I would pledge myself that every man in the regions round about should be forthcoming when called for...but to this day they have not touched the matter, for fear the Mormons would be acquitted from the charge of having any hand in it."

**Analysis:**
- Blames Indians exclusively
- Offers to produce suspects (knowing investigation won't happen)
- Claims enemies want to maintain false narrative
- Historical record shows Mormon involvement
- Extremely defensive tone suggests awareness of guilt
- IRONIC: Claims persecution while defending massacre

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ (Very strong for irony/hypocrisy theme)

---

#### ADDITIONAL FINDING: "Black-Hearted Republicans" + Abolitionist Attack (★★★★)

**Quote (JoD 10:109-110):**
> "The rank, rabid abolitionists, whom I call black-hearted Republicans, have set the whole national fabric on fire...I am no abolitionist, neither am I a proslavery man; I hate some of their principles and especially some of their conduct, as I do the gates of hell. The Southerners make the negroes, and the Northerners worship them; this is all the difference between slaveholders and abolitionists."

**Analysis:**
- "Black-hearted Republicans" - vivid attack on Lincoln's party
- Equates abolitionists with slaveholders
- Claims both sides equally wrong on slavery
- Dismisses moral urgency of ending slavery
- Then IMMEDIATELY transitions to death penalty quote above
- TIMING: March 1863, midst of Civil War

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ (Strong political/racial content)

---

#### ADDITIONAL FINDING: Cleansing the Land / Leper Quarantine (★★★)

**Quote (JoD 10:110):**
> "Suppose the land should be cleansed from its filthiness and the law of God should predominate, if a man or woman should be found who had corrupted themselves and thereby become diseased, that man or woman would be placed by themselves, as the lepers were anciently, never more to commune with the human family."

**Analysis:**
- Advocates permanent quarantine for "corrupted" people
- Sexual "corruption" = permanent banishment

- Would apply to STD victims under "law of God"

**Song Potential:** ★★★ (Good for theocratic control theme)

### SERMON #40: "Knowledge, Correctly Applied...Slavery...True Charity"
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** May 31, 1863
**Pages:** JoD 10:187-195
**Reporter:** G. D. Watt
**Status:** Complete - MULTIPLE FINDINGS

#### PRIMARY FINDING: Servitude Justification (★★★★)

**Quote (JoD 10:192):**
> "Do we oppose the principle of servitude? I oppose it not in my judgment. If I have a man-servant or a maid-servant, they are flesh of my flesh and bone of my bone—they are the children of God as much as I am. In the providences of God their ability is such that they cannot rise above the position of a servant, and they are willing to serve me and have me dictate their labor. Then let them do service to me, and it is my duty to treat them kindly and reward them accordingly."

**Analysis:**
- Explicitly endorses servitude/slavery
- Claims divine providence makes some people inherently inferior
- "Cannot rise above the position of a servant" - permanent inferiority
- Paternalistic justification ("treat them kindly")
- Civil War context makes this especially striking
- Contradicts modern Church teaching on human dignity

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ (Strong slavery apologetics)

### SERMON #60: "Difference of Ideas Respecting God...Man Made in the Image of God"
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** July 31, 1864
**Pages:** JoD 10:318-327
**Reporter:** G. D. Watt
**Status:** Complete - MODERATE FINDINGS

#### PRIMARY FINDING: God as Literal Physical Being (★★★)

**Quote (JoD 10:320):**
> "We believe in a God who has eyes to see, ears to hear, indeed every member and sense of his body well developed as a perfect man. Is there any harm in believing this? If we do not take this view of the Great Author of our existence, what view shall we take of him? Shall we try to view Him as a shapeless, passionless, measureless entity?"

**Analysis:**
- God has literal physical body with organs
- "Every member and sense...developed as a perfect man"
- Implies God has ALL human anatomy
- Still official LDS doctrine (not problematic to modern Church)
- Strikes mainstream Christians as heretical but not scandalous

**Song Potential:** ★★★ (Moderate - theological oddity)

---

#### ADDITIONAL FINDING: Brigham's Influence Explained (★★★)

Brigham Young does nothing more than preach the truth, the people believe and love it...no man can have influence over this people, unless he is a righteous man."

**Analysis:**

### SERMON #61: "Love for the Things of God...Temporal Nature of the Kingdom"
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** June 22-29, 1864 (multi-city tour)
**Pages:** JoD 10:328-338
**Reporter:** E. L. Sloan
**Status:** Complete - HIGH PRIORITY FINDING

#### PRIMARY FINDING: Total Temporal Control Doctrine (★★★★★)

**Quote (JoD 10:336):**
> "In spiritual things you are my leader; I take you for my counsel in spiritual affairs; but if you dictate me in my temporal concerns, you touch a string that does not belong to you, to brother Heber, brother George A. Smith, nor anybody else.' If this is the case, ye Elders of Israel, we have been mistaken all the day long...it is our duty to lead this people in every act of their lives, as much in their temporal as in their spiritual affairs."

**Analysis:**
- Explicitly claims right to control ALL aspects of members' lives
- Not just spiritual - TEMPORAL control too
- "Every act of their lives" - totalitarian claim
- Directly rejects member objections to temporal control
- Grain price controls, economic dictation
- Modern parallel: Corporate church control of business/education

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ (EXTREMELY HIGH - theocratic control)

#### ADDITIONAL FINDING: Cursing Merchants (★★★★)

**Quote (JoD 10:337):**
> "I want to say a word or two with regard to brethren here taking goods from merchants to sell. Watch and learn the spirit of the man who does this, and in nine cases out of ten his faith, feelings, and affections are wholly to benefit his employer...Such a man will, sooner or later, apostatize. Those who will do this, and will shave the Saints to do a good business for the merchant who employs them, I curse in the name of Jesus Christ, and they shall be cursed."

**Analysis:**
- Brigham literally CURSES merchants in Jesus' name
- Working for Gentile merchants = apostasy
- Economic control through religious threat
- "They shall be cursed" - prophetic curse
- Theocratic economics enforced by divine threat

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ (Very strong - abuse of prophetic authority)
