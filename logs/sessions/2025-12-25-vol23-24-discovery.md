## SERMON 1: "Leaders of the Church Inspired" (JoD 24:158-166)

**Speaker:** Erastus Snow (Apostle)
**Date:** June 24, 1883
**Location:** Parowan Quarterly Conference
**Pages:** 158-166
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★

### Major Findings

#### FINDING 1: Direct Attack on Joseph Smith III
**Category:** Authority Claims, Polygamy
**Quote:**
> "Well, now, in regard to those who are seeking for an excuse to reject plural marriage and are inclined to receive the statement of young Joseph Smith, I wish to say that I know that Joseph Smith is entirely ignorant of what he says, or he is a liar; for I know that he does not speak the truth."

**Citation:** JoD 24:164
**Analysis:** Snow directly calls Joseph Smith III (RLDS prophet, Joseph Smith's son) either "ignorant or a liar" regarding polygamy history. This is an extraordinary personal attack on the prophet's own son.

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ — Direct confrontation with Joseph's son, creates powerful narrative tension.

---

#### FINDING 2: Emma Smith Gave Joseph Wives
**Category:** Polygamy
**Quote:**
> "Joseph—I refer now to the young man that is alive and who was a mere boy at his father's death, and who with his mother and her children remained behind, though his mother did know for herself that her husband did teach and practice this order of marriage, yet she was not willing to own or acknowledge it to her children"

**Citation:** JoD 24:164
**Analysis:** Claims Emma Smith knew about and participated in polygamy but concealed it from her children. Directly contradicts RLDS historical narrative and Emma's later denials.

**Cross-reference:** JoD 23:124-133 (Wilford Woodruff: "Emma Smith gave her husband in marriage several women")

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ — Emma's role in polygamy is controversial and well-documented issue.

---

#### FINDING 3: Personal Testimony of Polygamy Revelation (1842)
**Category:** Polygamy, Authority
**Quote:**
> "I know that he taught to me as early as in the spring of 1842 what God had revealed to him on the subject; I know that he gave to me my second wife and assigned his brother, Hyrum, to seal her to me"

**Citation:** JoD 24:164
**Analysis:** Direct firsthand testimony placing polygamy revelation to 1842, full year before official 1843 dating. Names Hyrum Smith as sealer.

**Historical Note:** Pre-dates official revelation (D&C 132, dated July 12, 1843) by over a year.

---

#### FINDING 4: Treble Happiness in Plural Marriage
**Category:** Polygamy
**Quote:**
> "I say there is treble the genuine comfort and happiness in those families who enter into this order and abide in it, than is to be found in the same number of families in monogamy in this Church, to say nothing of the Gentile world."

**Citation:** JoD 24:163
**Analysis:** Claims "treble" (triple) the happiness in polygamous families. Empirical claim without evidence.

**Song Potential:** ★★★ — Quantified happiness claim, easily contrasted with modern church position.

---

#### FINDING 5: Population Statistics as Divine Sign
**Category:** Polygamy, Demographics
**Quote:**
> "When we visit the settlements of the Saints and attend the Quarterly Conferences throughout the land, what do we hear in the reports? We hear that an average of about thirty per cent. of the entire population are children under eight years of age, and another one-third between that and twenty"

**Citation:** JoD 24:165
**Analysis:** Uses high birth rates (66% of population under 20) as evidence of divine approval of polygamy. Presents demographic growth as threatening to enemies.

**Tone:** Triumphalist — "This is the cause of the opposition waged against us."

---

#### FINDING 6: Youth Birth Rate as Weapon
**Category:** Polygamy, Political
**Quote:**
> "Think you, you can stay the purposes of the Great Jehovah? No; these spirits will come and our streets will be full of children, sons and daughters, and they will say, as they crowd up: 'The place is too strait, Give place to me that I may dwell'"

**Citation:** JoD 24:165
**Analysis:** Describes demographic growth as unstoppable divine plan. Uses language of conquest and expansion.

**Cross-reference:** Isaiah 49:20, 54:2-3 (prophetic template for population expansion)

---

### Sermon Summary
**Primary Theme:** Aggressive defense of polygamy against RLDS church
**Tone:** Combative, dismissive of Joseph Smith III
**Key Strategy:** Personal testimony, attack on opponents' credibility
**Theological Claim:** Polygamy required for exaltation

**Weaknesses for Apologetics:**
- Direct attack on Joseph's son creates family division narrative
- Emma's knowledge claim contradicts later church historical narrative
- "Treble happiness" claim easily falsifiable

## SERMON 2: "The Work of God" (JoD 24:227-235)

**Speaker:** John Taylor (LDS President)
**Date:** 1883 (Bear Lake trip)
**Pages:** 227-235
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★

### Major Findings

#### FINDING 1: Kingdom Could Not Go Forward Without Polygamy
**Category:** Polygamy — Doctrinal Ultimatum
**Quote:**
> "He presented this principle to the Twelve, and called upon them to obey it, and said if they did not, the kingdom of God could not go one step further. Why could it not go one step further? Because we had a religion to live by, but none that placed our associations upon eternal principles or gave us a claim upon each other in the family relations in the eternal worlds."

**Citation:** JoD 24:229
**Analysis:** Strongest possible statement linking polygamy to church progression. Without it, "the kingdom of God could not go one step further."

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ — Perfect contrast with 1890 Manifesto and current church position.

---

#### FINDING 2: Joseph Smith's Confrontation with Taylor
**Category:** Polygamy, Authority
**Quote:**
> "I was riding out of Nauvoo on horseback, and met Joseph Smith coming in, he too, being on horseback... he looked at me very intently. 'Look here,' said he, 'those things that have been spoken of must be fulfilled, and if they are not entered into right away, the keys will be turned.'"

**Citation:** JoD 24:230
**Analysis:** Dramatic confrontation narrative. Joseph threatens that "the keys will be turned" if polygamy not entered into immediately. Creates sense of urgency and divine ultimatum.

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ — Vivid scene, high drama, direct threat.

---

#### FINDING 3: Revelation Reading (D&C 132)
**Category:** Polygamy
**Quotes:**
> "For behold! I reveal unto you a new and everlasting covenant; and if ye abide not that covenant, then are ye damned."

> "no one can reject this covenant, and be permitted to enter into my glory."

> "he that receiveth a fullness thereof, must and shall abide the law, or he shall be damned, saith the Lord God."

**Citation:** JoD 24:230 (quoting D&C 132:4, 6)
**Analysis:** Taylor reads the most extreme passages of D&C 132, emphasizing damnation for non-compliance. No hedging, no softening.

**Current Status:** D&C 132 still in LDS canon, but interpreted very differently today.

---

#### FINDING 4: Adultery Death Penalty Case
**Category:** Polygamy, Violence/Punishment
**Quote:**
> "A certain Bishop wrote to me to know what should be done in the following case: A man had been away from home on a mission, and during his absence his wife had committed adultery... That revelation states that, 'If a man receiveth a wife in the new and everlasting covenant, and if she be with another man, and I have not appointed unto her by the holy anointing, she hath committed adultery and shall be destroyed.'"

**Citation:** JoD 24:231
**Analysis:** Taylor recounts case where he ordered woman "destroyed in the flesh" for adultery during husband's mission absence. Brought "three beautiful little boys" to meeting. Heart-wrenching scene used to demonstrate law severity.

**Further quote:**
> "they shall be destroyed in the flesh, and shall be delivered unto the buffetings of Satan unto the day of redemption, saith the Lord God."

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ — Brutal application of revealed law, human cost visible.

**Apologetic Challenge:** How to reconcile "destroyed in the flesh" with modern church mercy teachings?

---

#### FINDING 5: Taylor's Personal Struggle with Polygamy
**Category:** Polygamy, Personal
**Quote:**
> "I had always entertained strict ideas of virtue, and I felt as a married man that this was to me, outside of this principle, an appalling thing to do. The idea of my going and asking a young lady to be married to me, when I had already a wife! It was a thing calculated to stir up feelings from the innermost depth of the human soul."

**Citation:** JoD 24:230
**Analysis:** Rare moment of vulnerability. Taylor admits polygamy was "appalling" to him initially, required "knowledge of God" to overcome natural repugnance.

**Song Potential:** ★★★ — Humanizes the struggle, shows internal conflict.

---

#### FINDING 6: No Sympathy in Judgment
**Category:** Priesthood Authority
**Quote:**
> "My sympathies in the case that I related were very strong; but I must not be governed by sympathies—I must be governed by the law of God."

**Citation:** JoD 24:232
**Analysis:** After describing woman's destruction, Taylor emphasizes that authority must override compassion. Law above mercy.

---

### Sermon Summary
**Primary Theme:** Polygamy as absolute requirement, enforcement of strict punishment
**Tone:** Authoritarian, uncompromising
**Key Strategy:** Divine ultimatum + dramatic narratives + scriptural authority
**Theological Claim:** Polygamy required for exaltation, rejection = damnation

**Weaknesses for Apologetics:**
- "Kingdom could not go forward" directly contradicts 1890 Manifesto continuation
- Destruction of adulteress creates moral discomfort
- "Keys will be turned" threat creates urgency that didn't materialize

## SERMON 3: "Exhortation From Isaiah" (JoD 24:20-32)

**Speaker:** Orson Pratt (Apostle)
**Date:** October 26, 1879
**Pages:** 20-32
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★

### Major Findings

#### FINDING 1: Jackson County Return Prophecy
**Category:** Failed Prophecy
**Quote:**
> "We expect to return there just as much as we expect the sun will rise and set. We have no other expectation. We expect to return there just as much as the Jews expect to return to old Jerusalem in the latter days."

**Citation:** JoD 24:22
**Analysis:** Absolute certainty of return to Jackson County, Missouri. Compares it to Jewish return to Jerusalem (which occurred in 1948). LDS return: never happened.

**Timeline:** Sermon delivered 1879. 146 years later (2025): Still in Utah.

---

#### FINDING 2: Detailed Temple Architecture
**Category:** Failed Prophecy
**Quote:**
> "We expect to build a temple different from all other temples in some respects... there will be 24 different compartments in the Temple that will be built in Jackson County. The names of these compartments were given to us some 45 or 46 years ago; the names we still have, and when we build these 24 rooms, in a circular form and arched over the centre, we shall give the names to all these different compartments just as the Lord specified through Joseph Smith."

**Citation:** JoD 24:23
**Analysis:** Extremely specific architectural details:
- 24 compartments (names given by revelation)
- Circular form
- Arched center
- Different from all other temples

**Status 2025:** Never built. Church sold Jackson County properties.

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ — Specificity makes failure undeniable.

---

#### FINDING 3: Materials That Never Decay
**Category:** Failed Prophecy, Cosmology
**Quote:**
> "O though afflicted, tossed with tempest, and not comforted, behold, I will lay thy stones with fair colors, and lay thy foundation with sapphires. And I will make thy windows of agates, and thy gates of carbuncles, and all thy borders of pleasant stones... the Lord will bring or create, or form, as the case may be, or tell His children how to form those precious stones in great abundance"

**Citation:** JoD 24:24 (quoting Isaiah 54:11-12)
**Analysis:** Temple to be built of sapphires, agates, carbuncles. God will "create, or form" these materials in abundance. Buildings will never decay.

**Further quote:**
> "I mean to say that not only the Temple, but all the buildings that shall be built round about that Temple, and the city that will be built round about it, which will be called the New Jerusalem, will be built of materials that never will decay."

---

#### FINDING 4: God's Presence Will Preserve Buildings
**Category:** Failed Prophecy, Miracle Claims
**Quote:**
> "Why? Because God will be there. So He will be in the temple of Zion on this continent, and by His power, by His laws—which are superior to all those grosser laws of nature—He will preserve both of these cities, one on the western hemisphere, and one on the eastern hemisphere, from any decay whatever."

**Citation:** JoD 24:24
**Analysis:** Claims God's physical presence will supernaturally preserve buildings forever. Compares to manna preservation miracle.

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ — Grandiose miracle claim, easy to verify never happened.

---

#### FINDING 5: Light Visible for Miles
**Category:** Failed Prophecy
**Quote:**
> "It will never go down, it will be a standing miracle by day and by night, from one week to another, month after month, year after year, until the one thousand years shall have rolled away... when they get within a day or two's journey of the city they will be alarmed. Some of these kings and nobles, when they see the light shining forth like the northern lights in the arctic regions, illuminating the whole face of the heavens... fear will take hold of them"

**Citation:** JoD 24:25
**Analysis:** New Jerusalem will emit visible light like aurora borealis, visible from days away. Kings will flee in terror.

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ — Theatrical, testable, never happened.

---

#### FINDING 6: Timeline: "Not Very Far Distant"
**Category:** Failed Prophecy
**Quote:**
> "Now, we have it recorded here in this book, in the 31st chapter of Jeremiah, that this city on the eastern continent shall not be thrown down any more forever. It seems, therefore, to be an eternal city, never to be destroyed."

**Citation:** JoD 24:24
**Analysis:** Implies fulfillment soon. 1879 + 146 years = 2025. Still waiting.

---

#### FINDING 7: Latter-day Saints Will Inherit Gentile Cities
**Category:** Failed Prophecy, Theocracy
**Quote:**
> "Now, there are a great many cities in the United States that will not be totally destroyed when the inhabitants are swept off the surface of the earth. Their houses, their desolate cities will still remain unoccupied until Zion in her glory and strength shall enlarge the place of her tents, and stretch forth the curtains of her habitations. That is the destiny of this nation, and the destiny of the Latter-day Saints."

**Citation:** JoD 24:27
**Analysis:** Prophecy that US inhabitants will be "swept off," cities left empty for LDS inheritance.

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ — Apocalyptic takeover prophecy.

---

### Sermon Summary
**Primary Theme:** Jackson County return and New Jerusalem construction
**Tone:** Prophetic certainty, grandiose vision
**Key Strategy:** Detailed specificity to demonstrate revelation authenticity
**Status:** Complete failure. Church sold Jackson County land.

**Weaknesses for Apologetics:**
- Extreme specificity makes failure undeniable
- Timeline ("not far distant") creates falsifiability
- Architectural details (24 rooms, names given) never materialized
- Light miracle never occurred
- No return after 146 years

## SERMON 5: "The Age in Which We Live" (JoD 24:347-356)

**Speaker:** John Taylor (LDS President)
**Date:** December 9, 1883
**Location:** Kaysville, Davis County
**Pages:** 347-356
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★

### Major Findings

#### FINDING 1: No Cowards in Our Ranks
**Category:** Polygamy Defiance
**Quote:**
> "We want no cowards in our ranks,
Who will our colors fly;
We call for valiant-hearted men,
Who are not afraid to die.

No yielding up of principles that God has revealed. What, turn our backs on Jehovah! and place ourselves in the hands of men who would deprive us of the last vestige of liberty, and take our lives if they had the power! What! shall we forsake God our Heavenly Father? No, never!"

**Citation:** JoD 24:351
**Analysis:** Explicit call for no compromise on polygamy. Framed as loyalty to God vs. apostasy.

**Irony:** Taylor himself went into hiding (Underground) rather than comply with law. Never formally renounced polygamy before death (1887).

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ — Poetic form, defiant tone, perfect contrast with 1890 Manifesto (3 years later).

---

#### FINDING 2: Legislature Should NOT Abolish Polygamy
**Category:** Polygamy, Politics
**Quote:**
> "I have heard some people say, 'Don't you think that we are in very great danger now?' We should be if the Lord did not rule... 'But,' say some, 'don't you think that when our Legislature meet they had better go to work and pass a law doing away with polygamy?' No; no such thought ever enters my mind"

**Citation:** JoD 24:351
**Analysis:** Taylor explicitly rejects any legislative compromise. "No such thought ever enters my mind."

**Timeline:** December 1883. Manifesto: October 1890 (6.8 years later).

---

#### FINDING 3: Cain as "Religious Cuss"
**Category:** Rhetoric, Biblical Interpretation
**Quote:**
> "Cain was called the great Master Mahan. Still he was a religious 'cuss.' Excuse the expression; but we have a great many such to-day... He was going to be, as I have said, a religious 'cuss,' a religious hypocrite"

**Citation:** JoD 24:349
**Analysis:** Uses vulgar slang ("cuss") in pulpit to describe Cain. Applies to modern critics.

**Tone:** Crude, dismissive of opponents.

---

#### FINDING 4: Christian Civilizers Bring Corruption
**Category:** Anti-Christian Rhetoric
**Quote:**

Who maintains prostitution here? Our Christian civilizers... Who introduced billiard halls, and gambling hells in our midst? Our Christian civilizers... Who sustain drunkenness and saloons in our midst? Our Christian civilizers."

**Citation:** JoD 24:352
**Analysis:** Systematic indictment of "Christian civilizers" for every vice in Salt Lake City. "Christian" becomes insult.

**Statistics cited:**
- 45 saloons in Salt Lake City

#### FINDING 5: Wicked Shall Slay the Wicked

And the time is not very far distant when another Scripture will be fulfilled, namely, 'that every man that will not take up his sword against his neighbor, must needs flee unto Zion for safety.' In Zion there will be safety."

**Citation:** JoD 24:352
**Analysis:** Prophecy of widespread violence and slaughter. Only Zion (LDS territory) will be safe.
