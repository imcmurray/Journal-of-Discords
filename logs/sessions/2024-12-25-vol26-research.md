## Executive Summary

Volume 26 (1882-1885) represents the final volume of the Journal of Discourses and contains **55 sermons** delivered during a critical period of federal persecution under the Edmunds Act (1882). This volume is EXCEPTIONALLY rich in polygamy defense content, with extensive theological justifications and direct responses to federal prosecution.

**Key Finding:** This volume contains some of the most explicit and doctrinally comprehensive defenses of plural marriage in the entire JoD corpus, delivered at the height of the anti-polygamy campaign.

## HIGH Priority Sermons (Read in Full)

### 1. "The Creation, etc." - Erastus Snow (JoD 26:213-227)
**Date:** May 31, 1885, Provo Tabernacle
**Priority:** HIGH
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★

**Key Findings:**
- **Heavenly Mother theology**: "In the heavens are parents single? / No; the thought makes reason stare! / Truth is reason; truth eternal / Tells me, I've a Mother there."
- **Plural marriage as divine commandment**: "God commanded that they should take of the daughters of Eve for wives and multiply and increase in the land... from and after Abraham it was enjoined upon Israel"
- **Anti-birth control polemic**: Extended attack on New England for limiting families, "foeticide, infanticide and child-murder"
- **Edmunds Act unmasked**: Claims prosecutors admitted the law targets Mormon marriage status, not sexual immorality
- **Quote from President Cleveland**: "I wish you people out there could be like the rest of us" (interpreted as: conform to monogamy)
- **Judge Zane's ruling**: Argument that cohabitation prosecution requires no proof of sexual intercourse, only "habit and repute of marriage"

**Notable Quotes:**
- "Modern Christendom—in these United States especially, and in staid New England more than perhaps any other portion of this American continent—is this commandment of God to multiply and replenish the earth nullified?"
- "The chief warfare against the Latter-day Saints at the present time is an endeavor to compel us to conform to their new state of things"
- "I wish you out there could be like the rest of us... if you only disown your wives, then do what you will you are secure"

### 2. "Communities are Made up of Family Organizations, etc." - Henry W. Naisbitt (JoD 26:114-127)
**Date:** March 8, 1885, Salt Lake Tabernacle
**Priority:** HIGH
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Key Findings:**
- **Eternal marriage theology**: Comprehensive argument for polygamy based on eternal marriage principle
- **Abraham's two wives tradition**: References Rabbinical tradition that Adam had two wives (Lilith and Eve), claims Joseph Smith taught this
- **Biblical polygamy cataloged**: Lists Abraham, Jacob, Moses, Gideon, Jehoida, Abdon, Rehoboam, Abijah, Esau, Lamech, Jerubbaal, Jair
- **David and Solomon defended**: "In none of these things did he violate the commandments, save in the case of the wife of Uriah" (1 Kings 15:5)
- **Monogamy as inherited tradition**: "From whence did you derive your monogamy?... it is the outgrowth of tradition"
- **Personal testimony**: "I have lived in that relationship... to me and to mine it was productive of good... when I had three wives given me of God, and occupying but one habitation"
- **Eternal Fathers theology**: "Every Father becomes, by virtue of his position, an everlasting father"

**Notable Quotes:**
- "What of the whoredoms, the adultery, the fornication, the prostitution of women in monogamic nations? What of sexual diseases, of blighted lives, of martyred women, of little graves dotting every hillside?"
- "Dr. Newman also made another remark... that polygamy was not intended for the poor man, that it was intended for the kings of the earth... God decreed that he would gather His Israel from the poor of all nations"

### 3. "The Lord is Teaching us Valuable Lessons" - Moses Thatcher (JoD 26:327-335)
**Date:** October 8, 1885, Logan Tabernacle
**Priority:** HIGH
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Key Findings:**
- **Judge not a Christian**: "I don't think him to be a Christian; if he is, he does not understand the Scriptures"
- **Concubines defended**: "All concubines, anciently, were considered wives... A concubine, then, was a maid servant married to a free man... made her a legal wife in the sight of God"
- **Abraham vindicated**: Extended defense of Abraham's relationships with handmaids against judge's characterization
- **Lazarus in Abraham's bosom**: Uses parable to defend Abraham's character and eternal position
- **Prophecy of government collapse**: Detailed predictions of labor/capital conflict, Knights of Labor power, Rock Springs massacre
- **Utah as refuge**: "When war and desolation and bloodshed... come upon the nation... Come to Utah and we will divide our morsel of food with you"
- **Secret societies warning**: References Pittsburg riots, Knights of Labor, weakness of government

**Notable Quotes:**
- "Abraham not only lived with his wives, but also with his wife's handmaids; in other words, said the judge, the same as though you were to live with your hired girls. Now, while that might do for Abraham, said he, it will not do for this enlightened age."
- "If the Judge of the Supreme Court of the Territory of Utah is a Christian, how will he feel when he comes into the presence of Father Abraham, whom he has sought to cast reflections upon?"

### 4. "Hostility of the World to the Gospel, etc." - John Taylor (JoD 26:87-97)
**Date:** February 12, 1882, Salt Lake Assembly Hall
**Priority:** HIGH
**Song Potential:** ★★★★

**Key Findings:**
- **Polygamy vs. bigamy distinction**: "Our plural marriage is no more their bigamy than white is black or than light is darkness"
- **Bigamy is fraud, polygamy is honorable**: "Their bigamy is deception and fraud and a breach of the marriage covenant; while our polygamy... is the fulfilling of the marriage covenant"
- **Eternal vs. temporal covenants**: "The Christian world make their covenants for time only; we for time and for eternity"
- **Seth replaced Abel**: Theological argument about Seth taking Abel's place after Cain's murder
- **Enoch's translation**: Extended narrative about Enoch's city being caught up to heaven
- **Not preaching polygamy to the world**: "We do not preach to them this very obnoxious doctrine of polygamy for them to practice"
- **Plural marriage for the pure only**: "It belongs to the people who have obeyed the Gospel... who are in possession of the principles of life, and who are keeping the commandments of God"

**Notable Quotes:**
- "With them a man may have his wife and also be mixed up with other women... his poor, unfortunate offspring, the product of his vice and corruption, may be sweeping the crossings of the streets of our large cities"
- "God save us from such 'Christianity,' from this time, henceforth and forever" [congregation responds "Amen"]

## Historical Context Notes

**Volume 26 Time Period:** 1882-1885

### Critical Events:
- **Edmunds Act (1882)**: Made polygamy a felony, created "unlawful cohabitation" charge
- **Utah Commission**: Disenfranchised polygamists
- **John Taylor in hiding**: President Taylor went underground in 1885, died in exile 1887
- **Mass prosecutions**: Hundreds of Mormon men imprisoned
- **Test oath**: Registration oath excluding polygamists from voting

### This Volume's Unique Contribution:
Volume 26 represents the LDS Church's most sustained, theologically sophisticated defense of plural marriage at the moment of its greatest legal jeopardy. The sermons show:

1. **Theological deepening**: More elaborate biblical/theological arguments than earlier volumes
2. **Direct legal engagement**: Explicit discussion of court cases, judges, prosecutors
3. **Persecution theology**: Framing federal action as religious persecution
4. **Prophecy intensification**: Increased predictions of government collapse
5. **No retreat**: Absolute refusal to compromise despite imprisonment
