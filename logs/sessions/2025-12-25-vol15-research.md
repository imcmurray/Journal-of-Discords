## HIGH Priority Findings

### 1. Pre-existence of Our Spirits (JoD 15:241-253)
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** December 15, 1872
- **Pages:** 241-253
- **Flag Reason:** Comprehensive pre-existence doctrine with testable claims
- **Topics:** Cosmology, Theology, Authority
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★☆

**Key Claims:**
1. All humans existed as spirits before mortal birth, "thousands of years ago"
2. Spirits have literal parents in heaven who begat them sexually
3. "We all have a literal father and a literal mother in the heavens by whom their spirits were brought into existence"
4. Memory of pre-existence is intentionally withheld
5. Those married for eternity will beget spirit children as gods do
6. Unmarried Saints become "angels" serving those with eternal families
7. Michael = Adam, "our first great progenitor"

**Problematic Doctrinal Statements:**
- "We as Latter-day Saints believe that the spirits that occupy these tabernacles have form and likeness similar to the human tabernacle"
- Pre-existence is "legitimately his sons and daughters independent of adoption"
- Marriage is "essentially necessary to qualify them to propagate their species throughout all eternity"

**Analysis:** This is now-disavowed Mormon doctrine. Modern LDS Church does not teach that God the Father has a physical wife or that humans will beget spirit children as gods. Highly detailed theological speculation presented as revealed truth.

### 2. Different Degrees of Reward and Punishment (JoD 15:312-324)
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** January 19, 1873
- **Pages:** 312-324
- **Flag Reason:** Blood atonement reference, eternal marriage requirements, three degrees of glory doctrine
- **Topics:** Blood Atonement, Polygamy, Theology
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★☆

**Key Claims:**
1. Saints who shed innocent blood after receiving Holy Ghost cannot be forgiven
2. "Would it be our duty, if such a person were judged, and condemned by the laws of our country to be hanged by the neck until dead, to visit him and pray that God would forgive his sins? No, we would be sinning against God in doing so."
3. Murderers who never received Holy Ghost may be forgiven in world to come
4. "Old bachelors" who reject eternal marriage forfeit exaltation
5. Unmarried persons become "angels" or "servants" in celestial kingdom
6. Those who reject eternal marriage covenant "shall not have forgiveness of sins in this world nor in the world to come"

**Problematic Statements:**
- Clear blood atonement reference (LDS members who murder are beyond prayer)
- Eternal marriage presented as absolute requirement for exaltation
- Non-married persons relegated to servant status eternally
- David "had to suffer the penalty of that crime...a thousand years at least"

**Analysis:** Contains blood atonement doctrine and extreme marriage requirements now distanced from by modern LDS Church. Presents damnation for those who reject polygamy/eternal marriage.

- **Date:** May 19, 1872
- **Pages:** 27-34
- **Flag Reason:** Comprehensive polygamy defense, authority claims
- **Topics:** Polygamy, Authority, History
- **Priority:** HIGH

### 4. True Christmas and New Year (JoD 15:253-265)
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** December 29, 1872
- **Pages:** 253-265
- **Flag Reason:** Chronological claims, Book of Mormon "proof," cosmology, creation timeline
- **Topics:** Authority, Cosmology, Book of Mormon
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★★

**Key Claims:**
1. Christ born April 11 (Thursday), not December 25
2. Church organized exactly 1800 years after crucifixion "to the very day"
3. "This is something very marvelous...God commanded that boy to organize the Church on that day"
4. Days of creation were 1,000-year periods, not 24 hours
5. Earth created in 6,000-year periods
6. Book of Mormon proves Christ's exact age at crucifixion through longitude calculations
7. "Joseph Smith, being an unlearned man, never saw this to the day of his death; that is, he never understood it"
8. Earth will exist temporally for 7,000 years total
9. Spiritual creation preceded temporal creation

**Problematic Statements:**
- Specific chronological claims (April 11 birth) presented as revelation
- Claims Joseph didn't understand his own translation
- Earth age claims (7,000 years temporal existence)
- "God made the world in six days...six thousand years"

**Analysis:** Multiple testable claims that are scientifically falsified (earth age). Also admits Joseph Smith didn't understand his own translation, undermining prophetic authority. The 1800-year "miracle" is based on incorrect chronology assumptions.
