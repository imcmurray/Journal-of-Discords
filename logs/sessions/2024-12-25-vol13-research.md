## High-Priority Findings

### 1. Celestial Marriage (Orson Pratt) - JoD 13:183-196 ★★★★★

**Date:** October 7, 1869
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Location:** New Tabernacle, Salt Lake City

**Key Claims:**

1. **Statistical Manipulation:**
   - "Pennsylvania in 1860: 17,588 more females than males between ages 20-30"
   - "New York in 1860: 45,104 more females than males between ages 20-30"
   - "Massachusetts in 1865: 33,452 more females than males between ages 20-30"
   - Conclusion: Monogamy makes women "old maids or prostitutes"

2. **Historical Distortions:**
   - Claims polygamy practiced by Abraham was God-commanded
   - Claims David received 8 wives from God, plus Saul's wives
   - Solomon's polygamy was fine, only his foreign wives were wrong
   - Book of Mormon exception proves polygamy is God's general rule

3. **Physiological Claims:**
   - "Procreative powers of man endure much longer than those of woman"
   - Polygamy necessary for male health
   - Monogamy forces men to "do himself violence" or commit adultery

4. **Social Claims:**
   - Roman monogamy caused prostitution
   - "Mother of Harlots" (Catholic Church) introduced monogamy
   - Polygamous nations (China, Japan) have no prostitution
   - "Oriental nations" have "entire absence of many evils which exist in our own nation"

5. **Doctrinal Claims:**
   - Polygamy required for highest heaven
   - Angels are those who rejected plural marriage
   - "No thrones and kingdoms" for non-polygamists

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ - Census statistics can be fact-checked, physiological claims falsified, historical claims debunked, doctrine disavowed.

**Apologetic Vulnerabilities:**
- Census data verifiable
- Polygamy officially discontinued 1890
- Manifesto admits it was NOT required for exaltation
- Asian nations claim easily disproven

### 2. Celestial Marriage (George Q. Cannon) - JoD 13:197-209 ★★★★★

**Date:** October 9, 1869
**Speaker:** George Q. Cannon
**Location:** New Tabernacle, Salt Lake City

**Key Claims:**

1. **Exaltation Requirements:**
   - "Highest order and class of beings...should exist in the capacity of husbands and wives"
   - Only polygamists will have "privilege of propagating their species"
   - "This privilege of multiplying spirits" denied to non-polygamists
   - Angels are those who "never received the everlasting covenant of marriage for eternity"

2. **Women's "Curse" Claims:**
   - Polygamy will "redeem woman from the effects of the Fall"
   - Will "redeem" women from "Thy desire shall be to thy husband, and he shall rule over thee"
   - Women "will do anything to gratify that instinct" including "be dishonored"
   - Women "are not held accountable to the same extent as men are"

3. **Roman Empire Claims:**
   - Julius Caesar had 4 wives, "divorced one after another"
   - Augustus Caesar had 4 wives
   - Rome fell because of monogamy and courtesans
   - Polygamic hordes from north were more "pure" than monogamic Rome

4. **Health/Physiological Claims:**
   - "Children born here under this system" are healthier and smarter
   - Professor Park testifies Utah children are brightest in the world
   - "Travel throughout our entire Territory, and virtue prevails"
   - Medical professor in New York: can't find 25-year-old male "free from a certain disease"

5. **Statistical Claims:**
   - Massachusetts: 63,011 more females than males
   - These women made "either old maids or prostitutes" by law

6. **Authority Claims:**
   - "God and the people" will defend polygamy
   - "God will sustain us; we cannot bear it off"
   - "This same woman" (Emma Smith) "destroyed the revelation"
   - Emma "brought up her children to believe...no such thing as plurality"

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ - Exaltation requirements explicitly disavowed, statistics verifiable, health claims falsifiable, Roman history distorted, Emma Smith attacked.

**Apologetic Vulnerabilities:**
- Current church explicitly denies polygamy required for exaltation
- Census claims can be fact-checked
- Disease statistics likely fabricated
- Roman history easily verified
- Emma Smith character assassination

### 3. The Holy Ghost—Patriarchal Marriage (Wilford Woodruff) - JoD 13:156-169 ★★★★

**Date:** December 12, 1869
**Speaker:** Wilford Woodruff
**Location:** Tabernacle, Salt Lake City

**Key Claims:**

1. **Sealing Ordinance Claims:**
   - "There is not a man who has lived since the Church went into the wilderness...until Moroni...who can claim a wife in the resurrection"
   - "Not one of them has been married for eternity, but only until death"
   - Sealing ordinances "revealed" through Joseph
   - Before Joseph, ALL marriages ended at death

2. **Exaltation Claims:**
   - "I would just as lief they would take away any other principle of the Gospel as this"
   - Patriarchal marriage = "partake of eternal lives"
   - "Have wives and posterity in the world to come and throughout the endless ages of eternity"
   - Without it, no "fullness of celestial glory"

3. **Abraham's Seed Promise:**
   - "This promise has not been fulfilled" yet
   - "Take one square yard of sand...more numerous than all inhabitants that ever lived"
   - "In the resurrection there will be no end to the increase of Abraham"
   - Impossible if "marriage relation ceases with...this life"

4. **Prophecy/Kingdom Claims:**
   - Kingdom "will continue to grow...never be overthrown"
   - "Little stone Daniel saw will become a mountain and fill the earth"
   - "Kingdoms of this world becoming the kingdom of our God"

5. **Blood Atonement Reference:**
   - "Shedding innocent blood has cost the Jews eighteen hundred years of suffering"
   - "Cost this nation already four years of war, with two millions of men laid in the dust"
   - "Woe be to that nation...that sheds the blood of the Saints"
   - "Somebody has got to pay the bill for the shedding of that innocent blood"

6. **Apostate Psychology:**
   - "Two things which have always followed apostates"
   - "Fear their fellow men...fancy their lives were in danger"
   - "Desired to kill those who had been their benefactors"
   - Higbees, Laws sought Joseph's life

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ - Sealing doctrine claims, exaltation requirements, blood atonement overtones, failed kingdom prophecies.

**Apologetic Vulnerabilities:**
- Church now admits marriage ordinances were available in ancient times
- Polygamy not required for exaltation
- Kingdom prophecy timeline failed
- Blood atonement language

### 4. The Order of Enoch (George Q. Cannon) - JoD 13:95-103 ★★★★

**Date:** April 6, 1869
**Speaker:** George Q. Cannon
**Location:** New Tabernacle, Salt Lake City

**Key Claims:**

1. **Sermon = Scripture:**
   - "When any man speaks as he is moved upon by the Holy Ghost, that is the word of God to the people"
   - "Though the heavens and the earth pass away, not one jot or tittle of the word of God will fall unfulfilled"
   - "Whether it be by His own voice...by the ministration of angels...or by the inspiration of the Holy Ghost through His servants, it is the word of God to the people"
   - "It is truth and it will have its effect and fulfilment"

2. **United Order Theology:**
   - "If ye are not equal in earthly things, ye cannot be in obtaining heavenly things"
   - Order of Enoch required to build Center Stake of Zion
   - "Upon the reception and proper carrying out of this Order hinges...triumph of the kingdom of God"
   - Cannot redeem Zion without United Order

3. **Social Revolution:**
   - God will solve "social problem" through United Order
   - Will eliminate prostitution
   - Will eliminate theft, fraud, dishonesty
   - "Love of money is the root of all evil" - United Order cures this

4. **Wealth and Power Claims:**
   - "God designs to make of the Latter-day Saints the head"
   - "Intends to place in their hands...the wealth of the world"
   - "When our wealth shall be controlled by the President...we shall have greater power"
   - "Power will be used for...amelioration of the condition of the human family"

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ - "Sermons = scripture" claim directly contradicts current teaching, United Order failed, wealth prophecy unfulfilled.

**Apologetic Vulnerabilities:**
- Official church statement: prophets can be wrong
- United Order abandoned
- "Sermons = word of God" no longer taught
- Wealth of world not in LDS hands

### 5. The Right and Authority of President Brigham Young (George Q. Cannon) - JoD 13:43-55 ★★★★

**Date:** December 5, 1869
**Speaker:** George Q. Cannon
**Location:** Tabernacle, Salt Lake City

**Key Claims:**

1. **Hatred = Proof of Authority:**
   - "If the Saints had wanted evidence...who had the authority, the very fact that the world hated, reviled and persecuted Brigham should have been sufficient evidence"
   - Joseph was target of hatred, then Brigham = same authority
   - False prophets (Rigdon, Strang, etc.) were "hailed as brethren by the wicked"
   - This is a "peculiarity" proving who has true authority

2. **Absolute Obedience:**
   - "As well might we rebel against Jehovah Himself, or against Jesus" as rebel against Brigham
   - "During twenty-five long years...God has blessed him in everything he has told us to do"
   - "When we have prayed...to obey the counsels of His servant, He has given unto us His Holy Spirit"
   - Obedience to Brigham = obedience to God

3. **Succession Claims:**
   - Keys of priesthood given to Joseph by Peter, James, and John
   - Joseph gave keys to Twelve in Nauvoo temple
   - Brigham holds same authority Joseph held
   - Authority will never be taken from earth again

4. **Foreordination:**
   - "Long before he was born...Joseph Smith and Brigham Young were chosen"
   - "Before the earth was organized"
   - Like Jeremiah: "Before I formed thee in the belly, I knew thee"

**Song Potential:** ★★★★ - Absolute authority claims, "hatred = truth" logic, obedience theology contradicts modern teaching.

**Apologetic Vulnerabilities:**
- Current church denies prophetic infallibility
- "Obedience = agency" argument problematic
- Hatred test would validate many false prophets
- Foreordination to specific callings not emphasized

### 6. The Latter-day Kingdom of God (Orson Pratt) - JoD 13:124-138 ★★★

**Date:** April 10, 1870
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Location:** Tabernacle, Salt Lake City

**Key Claims:**

1. **Book of Mormon "Proofs":**
   - Newark, Ohio mound contained stone with Ten Commandments in Hebrew
   - "May the Lord have mercy on me a Nephite" found in another mound
   - Both found 30-35 years after Book of Mormon published
   - "I have seen that sacred stone" at Ethnological Society in New York
   - Hebrew scholars confirmed translation

2. **Chronology Claims:**
   - April 6, 1830 was "precisely to a day, 1800 years after the crucifixion"
   - April 11 is anniversary of Jesus's birth
   - April 6 is anniversary of crucifixion
   - Based on Book of Mormon Nephite calendar

3. **Time Zone "Proof":**
   - Book of Mormon says crucifixion was "in the morning" in America
   - Bible says afternoon (12-3pm) in Jerusalem
   - 7.5 hours longitude difference proves Book of Mormon true
   - Joseph "unaware...of any difference in the time of day"

4. **Civil War Prophecy:**
   - "I went forth...when I was a youth of nineteen, now I am fifty-eight"
   - Declared revelation "twenty-eight years before the war commenced"
   - "Pointed out the place...South Carolina"
   - People "laughed me to scorn"

5. **Kingdom Prophecy:**
   - "Kingdom of God...has been organized preparatory to...the coming of our Lord"
   - "Shall become universal and shall cover the whole earth"
   - "This kingdom...organized on the earth has been organized...to be thrown down no more for ever"

6. **Martyrdom and Judgment:**
   - "Blood of hundreds...almost thousands, will be required at the hands of this nation"
   - "Shedding innocent blood has cost the Jews eighteen hundred years of suffering"
   - "Cost this nation already four years of war"
   - "Woe be to that nation...that sheds the blood of the Saints"

**Song Potential:** ★★★ - Archaeological claims questionable, chronology claims debatable, Civil War prophecy implications, failed kingdom timeline.

**Apologetic Vulnerabilities:**
- Newark Holy Stones now considered fraudulent by archaeologists
- "Nephite" inscription likely hoax
- Chronology math debatable
- Civil War prophecy post-diction elements
- Kingdom hasn't filled earth in 150+ years
