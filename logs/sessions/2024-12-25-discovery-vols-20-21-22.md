## Findings Summary

### Top-Tier Findings (★★★★★)

1. **Polygamy Required for Exaltation** (JoD 22:120-132, 21:9-13, 21:286-296)
   - Multiple church leaders (Joseph F. Smith, Orson Pratt, George Q. Cannon)
   - "Without it we never could be exalted to associate with and become gods"
   - "Your faith is all vain" if polygamy not true
   - DISAVOWED by modern church

2. **Curse of Cain as Devil's Representation** (JoD 22:297-312)
   - John Taylor: "it was necessary that the devil should have a representation upon the earth"
   - Most extreme racist theology in entire JoD
   - DISAVOWED by 2013 Gospel Topics Essay

3. **Christ Coming in This Generation** (JoD 21:121-128)
   - Wilford Woodruff (future prophet): "We live in the generation itself when Jesus Christ will come"
   - Failed prophecy from 1880
   - Specified "this generation"

4. **Man First Flesh, Contradicts Geology** (JoD 21:197-206)
   - Orson Pratt explicitly: "contradicting the theories of geologists"
   - No death before fall
   - Falsified by paleontology, evolution

5. **Immortal Adam and Eve Procreating 1900 Years** (JoD 21:286-296)
   - Orson Pratt: Adam and Eve "as immortal beings have brought forth immortal sons and daughters since their resurrection"
   - Bizarre, specific, connects to Adam-God theology

6. **Excommunication for Civil Litigation** (JoD 22:297-312)
   - John Taylor: "if you take this matter to law before the courts of the ungodly, you shall be cut off from the Church"
   - Extreme authority claim
   - Congregation voted approval
