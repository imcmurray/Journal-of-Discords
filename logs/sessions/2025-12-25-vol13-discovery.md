### SERMON #22: Celestial Marriage — Orson Pratt (JoD 13:183-196)
**Date:** October 7, 1869
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ EXTRAORDINARY

**Category:** Polygamy — Scientific falsification, statistical manipulation, degrading claims about women

**Major Findings:**

1. **CENSUS STATISTICS MANIPULATION** (★★★★★)
   - **Claim:** Pennsylvania had 17,588 more females than males (ages 20-30) in 1860
   - **Claim:** New York had 45,104 more females than males (ages 20-30) in 1860
   - **Claim:** Massachusetts had 33,452 more females than males (ages 20-30) in 1865
   - **Quote:** "What is to be done with them?...They say, virtually, 'We will pass a law so strict, that if these females undertake to marry a man who has another wife, both they and the men they marry shall be subject to a term of imprisonment in the penitentiary.' Indeed! Then what are you going to do with these hundreds of thousands of females of a marriageable age? 'We are going to make them either old maids or prostitutes, and we would a little rather have them prostitutes, then we men would have no need to marry.'" (JoD 13:184)
   - **Falsifiable:** These census statistics can be fact-checked against actual 1860/1865 census data
   - **Song potential:** EXTREMELY HIGH — specific, shocking, verifiable

2. **PHYSIOLOGICAL JUSTIFICATIONS FOR POLYGAMY** (★★★★★)
   - **Claim:** God implanted feelings in males and females "the gratification of which is necessary to health and happiness"
   - **Claim:** Monogamic system leads to prostitution because it deprives women of "legitimate" gratification
   - **Quote:** "This law of monogamy, or the monogamic system, laid the foundation for prostitution and the evils and diseases of the most revolting nature and character under which modern Christendom groans, for as God has implanted, for a wise purpose, certain feelings in the breasts of females as well as males, the gratification of which is necessary to health and happiness, and which can only be accomplished legitimately in the married state, myriads of those who have been deprived of the privilege of entering that state, rather than be deprived of the gratification of those feelings altogether, have, in despair, given way to wickedness and licentiousness" (JoD 13:184)

3. **DEGRADING CLAIMS ABOUT MONOGAMY** (★★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "When the religious Reformers came out, some two or three centuries ago, they neglected to reform the marriage system...Rome...was a monogamic nation...committed numerous adulteries.    - **Claim:** Adam and Eve married as immortals in Garden of Eden, before the Fall
   - **Claim:** Only celestial beings can procreate in eternity
   - **Claim:** Angels have no wives and "are servants to those that sit upon thrones"
   - **Quote:** "There is not a woman under the sound of my voice to-day, but can bear witness of this, if she has tried it. You, sisters, whose husbands have taken other wives, can you not bear testimony that the principle has purified your hearts?" (JoD 13:185)

5. **EMMA SMITH CONDEMNATION** (★★★★★)
   - **Claim:** Emma Smith destroyed the original polygamy revelation
   - **Quote:** "What became of the original? An apostate destroyed it; you have heard her name. That same woman, in destroying the original, thought she had destroyed the revelation from the face of the earth. She was embittered against Joseph, her husband, and at times fought against him with all her heart; and then again she would break down in her feelings, and humble herself before God and call upon His holy name, and would then lead forth ladies and place their hands in the hands of Joseph, and they were married to him according to the law of God." ### SERMON #23: Celestial Marriage — George Q. Cannon (JoD 13:197-209)
**Date:** October 9, 1869
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ EXTRAORDINARY

**Category:** Polygamy — Exaltation requirement, degrading women, physiological claims

**Major Findings:**

1. **POLYGAMY REQUIRED FOR EXALTATION** (★★★★★)
   - **Strongest claim in entire volume**
   - **Quote:** "The Lord has said that 'if ye are not equal in earthly things, ye cannot be in obtaining heavenly things'...Strait is the gate and narrow the way that leadeth unto the exaltation and continuation of the lives, and few there be that find it" (JoD 13:198)
   - **Quote:** "God promised to Abraham that his seed should be as numerous as the stars of heaven or as the sands on the sea-shore...this very passage goes to prove that plurality was a principle God did approve under circumstances when it was authorized by Him." (JoD 13:200)
   - **Later disavowal:** Church now explicitly denies polygamy is required for exaltation

2. **JULIUS CAESAR MARRIAGES ARGUMENT** (★★★★★)
   - **Bizarre historical argument**
   - **Quote:** "Julius Caesar was First Consul at Rome...He had four wives during his life, and committed numerous adulteries. His first wife he married early; but, becoming ambitious, the alliance did not suit him, and, as the Roman law did not permit him to retain her and to marry another, he put her away. He then married the daughter of a consul...The third was divorced, and he married a fourth...His grandnephew, the Emperor Augustus Caesar, reigned at the time of the birth of Christ...he also had four wives. He divorced one after another" (JoD 13:201)
   - **Argument:** Romans had divorce + mistresses (bad), Mormons have polygamy without divorce (good)
   - **Irony:** Using pagan Rome's immorality to justify polygamy

3. **WOMEN DEGRADED BY MONOGAMY** (★★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "In the monogamic system...woman prostituted, debased and degraded as she is through Christendom. She may be treated coldly and degraded, but among them, except where the Christian element prevails to a large extent, she is not debased and polluted, as she is among the so-called Christian nations." (JoD 13:201)
   - **Quote:** "This is the result of the revelations of the Gospel unto us...every woman who chooses can be an honored wife and move in society in the enjoyment of every right which woman should enjoy to make her the equal of man as far as she can be his equal." (JoD 13:202)

4. **PHYSIOLOGICAL ARGUMENTS** (★★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "The procreative powers of man endure much longer than those of woman. Granting, as some assert, that an equal number of the sexes exist, what would this lead to? Man must practice that which is vile and low or submit to a system of repression; because if he be married to a woman who is physically incapable, he must either do himself violence or what is far worse, he must have recourse to the dreadful and damning practice of having illegal connection with women" (JoD 13:204)
   - **Implication:** Women's reproductive capabilities decline before men's, justifying additional wives

5. **CURSE ON WOMEN WILL BE REMOVED** (★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "I believe the correct practice of this principle will redeem woman from the effects of that curse—namely, 'Thy desire shall be to thy husband, and he shall rule over thee.' All the evils connected with jealousy have their origin in this. It is natural for woman to cleave to man; it was pronounced upon her in the beginning, seemingly as a punishment. I believe the time will come when, by the practice of the virtuous principles which God has revealed, woman will be emancipated from that punishment and that feeling." (JoD 13:204-205)

6. **MASSACHUSETTS STATISTICS** (★★★★★)
   - Repeats Pratt's census manipulation
   - **Quote:** "In the single State of Massachusetts, at the last census, there were 63,011 females more than males" (JoD 13:204)
   - **Falsifiable claim**

7. **HEALTH CLAIMS ABOUT UTAH CHILDREN** (★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "The testimony of Professor Park, the principal of the University of Deseret, and of other leading teachers of the young here, is that they never saw children with greater aptitude for the acquisition of knowledge than the children raised in this Territory. There are no brighter children to be found in the world than those born in this Territory. Under the system of Patriarchal Marriage, the offspring, besides being equally as bright and brighter intellectually, are much more healthy and strong." (JoD 13:205)
   - **Contradicts modern genetics**

8. **DISEASE CLAIMS** (★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "An eminent medical professor in New York, recently declared, while delivering a lecture to his class in one of the colleges there, that if he wanted a man twenty-five years of age, free from a certain disease, he would not know where to find him. What a terrible statement to make! In this community no such thing exists. Our boys grow up in purity" (JoD 13:205)

### SERMON #16: The Latter-day Kingdom of God — Orson Pratt (JoD 13:124-138)
**Date:** April 10, 1870
**Song Potential:** ★★★ HIGH

**Category:** Book of Mormon "proofs," Failed prophecy, Archaeological claims

**Major Findings:**

1. **NEWARK OHIO HEBREW STONES** (★★★★★)
   - **Archaeological claim**
   - **Quote:** "Thirty years after the Book of Mormon was put in print, giving the history of the settlement of this country, one of the great mounds south of the great lakes near Newark, in Ohio, was opened...they found a coffin in the midst of a hard kind of fire clay. Underneath this they found a large stone that appeared to be hollow...On the stone taken from the inside was carved the figure of a man with a priestly robe flowing from his shoulders; and over the head of this man were the Hebrew characters for Moshe, the ancient name of Moses; while on each side of this likeness, and on different sides of the stone, above, beneath, and around about were the Ten Commandments that were received on Mount Sinai, written in the ancient Hebrew characters." (JoD 13:134)
   - **Later scholarship:** Newark Holy Stones are considered fraudulent (1860s hoax)

2. **"MAY THE LORD HAVE MERCY ON ME A NEPHITE"** (★★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "Five years after the discovery of this remarkable memento of the ancient Israelites on the American continent, and thirty-five years after the Book of Mormon was in print, several other mounds in the same vicinity of Newark were opened, in several of which Hebrew characters were found. Among them was this beautiful expression, buried with one of their ancient dead, 'May the Lord have mercy on me a Nephite.'" (JoD 13:135)
   - **Later scholarship:** Also considered fraudulent

3. **CRUCIFIXION TIMING "PROOF"** (★★★★)
   - **Argument for Book of Mormon authenticity**
   - **Quote:** "This book, the Book of Mormon, informs us that the time of day at which Jesus was crucified, I mean the time of day here in America, was in the morning; the New Testament tells us that Jesus was crucified in Asia in the afternoon...Simply by the difference in longitude...Seven and a half hours difference in longitude would account for this apparent discrepancy; and if the Book of Mormon had said the crucifixion took place in the afternoon we should have known at once that it could not be true." (JoD 13:131-132)
   - **Pratt's claim:** Joseph couldn't have known about time zones

4. **CIVIL WAR PROPHECY** (★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "Well, it seems as if the Lord our God is giving the nation a pretty thorough warning. He told this nation by revelation, twenty-eight years before it commenced, of the great American war...I went forth before my beard was gray, before my hair began to turn white, when I was a youth of nineteen, now I am fifty-eight, and from that time on I published these tidings among the inhabitants of the earth. I carried forth the written revelation, foretelling this great contest, some twenty-eight years before the war commenced. This prophecy has been printed and circulated extensively" (JoD 13:136)
   - **Evaluation:** Partial fulfillment, but predicted worldwide war did not occur

5. **BLOOD OF SAINTS CRIES FOR VENGEANCE** (★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "This same book says, 'In that day the blood of the Saints shall cry from the ground for vengeance on the heads of the wicked.'...The blood of hundreds, and I might almost say thousands, will be required at the hands of this nation unless the people repent." (JoD 13:136-137)

6. **JACKSON COUNTY PROPHECY** (★★★★)
   - **Quote:** "Whatever the final result may be...there is one thing sure—as sure as the sun shines forth in yonder heavens, so sure will the Lord fulfil one thing with regard to this people. What is that? He will return them to Jackson county, and in the western part of the State of Missouri they will build up a city which shall be called Zion, which will he the head-quarters of this Latter-day Saint Church" (JoD 13:138)
   - **Falsifiable:** Has not occurred in 155 years

**Cross-References:**
- Newark Stones: Now considered fraudulent
- Jackson County: Still unfulfilled prophecy
- Civil War: D&C 87, partial fulfillment
