## Key Findings

### 1. Failed Prophecy: Times of Gentiles in One Generation

**Sermon:** "Prophecies of Joseph Smith and Their Literal Fulfillment"
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Date:** August 26, 1876
**Pages:** JoD 18:222-229

**Key Quote:**
> "How long will it be before the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled? This is a question I cannot fully answer. It will be in the generation that the angel comes. Forty-six years have already transpired, since the Lord sent his Gospel message to the Gentile nations..."

**Analysis:**
- Pratt explicitly ties "times of Gentiles" to the generation beginning in 1830
- Speaking in 1876 (46 years later), suggests fulfillment is imminent within that generation
- Claims precedent: Civil War prophecy took 28 years to fulfill
- 148+ years later (2024), "times of Gentiles" still not fulfilled by LDS standards
- **Song Potential: ★★★★★** - Clear generational timeline claim, easily falsifiable

---

### 2. Failed Prophecy: Divine Command on Slavery

**Sermon:** "The Blessing of Life for Evermore"
**Speaker:** Daniel H. Wells
**Date:** October 7, 1875
**Pages:** JoD 18:95-102

**Key Quote:**
> "It is not a great while since the word of the Lord came through Joseph Smith, the Prophet of the Lord, to this nation, to free their slaves, and for the Government to pay for them out of the treasury of the United States. Would the people receive the word of the Lord through his servant? No, they would not. What was the result? Why a fratricidal civil war in which thousands of millions of dollars were spent, devastation was spread over the land and rivers of blood were shed, and all this might have been avoided and the slaves liberated by peaceful means at not more than one-tenth of the expense, if they would have hearkened to the word of the Lord."

**Analysis:**
- Claims God commanded US Government to purchase slave freedom through Joseph Smith
- Government "rejected" this divine command
- Civil War presented as punishment for disobedience
- No historical record of such revelation being delivered to government
- D&C 87 (Civil War prophecy) says nothing about purchasing slaves
- **Song Potential: ★★★★★** - Clear claim of divine command with no evidence

---

### 3. Racial Doctrine: Lamanite Curse of Dark Skin

**Primary Sermon:** "Gathering of Israel"
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Date:** (Date not in intro, ~1875-1876)
**Pages:** JoD 18:16-29

**Key Quote:**
> "Why do they suppose any such thing? The ruins of their ancient cities, palaces and temples, proclaim in the ears of all living that once there dwelt on this hemisphere a great and powerful people, who were civilized and understood the art of constructing beautiful and substantial buildings. But now, O! how degraded, fallen and sunk into the very depths of darkness are the descendants of that once great, powerful and exalted people! 'They shall dwindle in unbelief because of iniquity;' because they rejected the Gospel. In the fourth century of the Christian era they apostatized from the religion of their fathers; they were cursed by the Almighty, a skin of darkness came upon them; they were cursed in all that they set their hands to do, and the withering curse of the Almighty has been upon them from generation to generation, until the present day."

**Supporting Quote (Wilford Woodruff, JoD 18:109-122):**
> "Some of the outside world are finding a good deal of fault with the Indians. Who are the Indians? Read the Book of Mormon, and you will learn that they are the literal descendants of Israel; they have been cursed through the transgressions of their fathers, and a skin of darkness has come upon them."

**Analysis:**
- Explicit claim that dark skin is divine punishment
- "Cursed by the Almighty" for apostasy
- Connects skin color directly to spiritual worthiness
- Now officially disavowed by LDS Church (2013 statement)
- **Song Potential: ★★★★★** - Multiple sources, clear statements, official disavowal

### 4. Unusual Doctrine: Three Nephites Appearing in Dreams

**Primary Sermon:** "Gathering of Israel"
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Pages:** JoD 18:16-29

**Key Quotes:**
> "What do they want with the Elders? They want to be baptized. Who told them to come and be baptized? They say that men came to them in their dreams, and spoke to them in their own language, and told them that away yonder was a people who had authority from God to baptize them; but that they must repent of their sins, cease their evil habits and lay aside the traditions of their fathers, for they were false; that they must cease to roam over the face of the land, robbing and plundering, and learn to live as the white people."

> "Who are these men who have been to the Indians and told them to repent of their sins, and be baptized by the 'Mormons?' They are men who obtained the promise of the Lord, upwards of eighteen centuries ago, that they should be instruments in his hands of bringing about the redemption of their descendants."

**Analysis:**
- Claims Three Nephites are actively appearing to Native Americans in 1870s
- Appearing in dreams, teaching Gospel, directing to LDS Church
- Specific claim: "thousands testify" to these experiences
- Ties to racial narrative (civilizing "degraded" descendants)
- **Song Potential: ★★★** - Interesting but secondary to racial/prophetic claims

### 5. Orson Pratt's Bold Claim on JSJ Prophecies

**Sermon:** "Prophecies of Joseph Smith and Their Literal Fulfillment"
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Pages:** JoD 18:222-229

**Key Quote:**

## Apologetic Responses to Anticipate

### "Times of Gentiles" Defense
- "Generation" can mean indefinite period
- "About to be fulfilled" is future tense, not claiming already fulfilled
- Pratt was speaking his opinion, not prophesying
- **Counter:** Pratt explicitly connects to 1830, uses past tense for 46 years

### Slavery Revelation Defense
- Revelation may have been private/informal
- Government never gave JS chance to deliver it
- D&C 87 implies peaceful solution was possible
- **Counter:** No evidence in D&C, JSJ papers, or contemporary records

### Skin of Darkness Defense
- "Spiritual darkness" metaphor
- Cultural understanding of the time
- No longer taught/officially disavowed
- **Counter:** Explicitly states "skin of darkness," describes physical degradation, contrasts with "white and delightsome"
