#### Sermon #9: "Rebuking Iniquity" - Jedediah M. Grant
**JoD 4:49-51 | September 21, 1856**

##### Blood Atonement Discoveries

1. **The Committee Proposal - STRIKING ★★★★★**
   > "I say, that there are men and women that I would advise to go to the President immediately, and ask him to appoint a committee to attend to their case; and then let a place be selected, and let that committee shed their blood."

   - **Category:** SPECIFIC - Institutional blood atonement proposal
   - **Why it stands out:** Not metaphorical. Grant proposes an actual administrative committee, a designated place, and a formal process for shedding blood.
   - **Song potential:** ★★★★★ - The bureaucratic specificity is chilling

2. **"Let the smoke ascend" - STRIKING ★★★★★**
   > "And you who have committed sins that cannot be forgiven through baptism, let your blood be shed, and let the smoke ascend, that the incense thereof may come up before God as an atonement for your sins, and that the sinners in Zion may be afraid."

   - **Category:** STRIKING - Vivid imagery of blood sacrifice
   - **Why it stands out:** Old Testament sacrificial language applied to killing church members. "That the sinners in Zion may be afraid" - openly terroristic purpose.
   - **Song potential:** ★★★★★ - The "smoke ascend" imagery is unforgettable

3. **"Water will not do" - STRIKING ★★★★★**
   > "We have those amongst us that are full of all manner of abominations, those who need to have their blood shed, for water will not do, their sins are of too deep a dye."

   - **Category:** STRIKING - Baptism insufficient, execution required
   - **Why it stands out:** Explicitly states that standard Christian atonement (baptism) is inadequate
   - **Song potential:** ★★★★★

##### Tone Discoveries

4. **Prayer for Curses - TONE ★★★★**
   > "They are the old hardened sinners, and are almost—if not altogether—past improvement, and are full of hell, and my prayer is that God's indignation may rest upon them, and that He will curse them from the crown of their heads to the soles of their feet."

   - **Category:** TONE - Vicious hostility toward members
   - **Why it stands out:** A church president publicly praying for God to curse church members
   - **Song potential:** ★★★★

5. **"I mean you" - TONE ★★★★★**
   > "If I hurt your feelings let them be hurt. And if any of you ask, do I mean you? I answer, yes. If any woman asks, do I mean her? I answer, yes."

   - **Category:** TONE - Deliberate cruelty to congregation
   - **Why it stands out:** Aggressive, confrontational, designed to terrorize
   - **Song potential:** ★★★★★ - Shows character/attitude

6. **Polygamy Coercion Context - IRONIC ★★★★**
   > "And we have women here who like any thing but the celestial law of God; and if they could break asunder the cable of the Church of Christ, there is scarcely a mother in Israel but would do it this day."

   - **Category:** IRONIC - Admits women hate polygamy
   - **Why it stands out:** Acknowledges universal female opposition to polygamy while demanding compliance
   - **Song potential:** ★★★★

##### Summary
The most explicitly violent blood atonement sermon in the entire JoD. Grant doesn't just teach the doctrine - he proposes an actual implementation with committee, place, and procedure. Delivered same day as Brigham Young's companion sermon (#10). The combination of institutional specificity and Old Testament sacrificial imagery makes this uniquely horrifying.

**Discovery Rating:** ★★★★★ CRITICAL
**Next Step:** Full Analysis Agent treatment + immediate song development consideration
