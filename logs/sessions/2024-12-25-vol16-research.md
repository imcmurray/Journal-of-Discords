- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★★
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "All the marriages of our forefathers, for many long generations past, have been illegal in the sight of God... and the children illegitimate." (JoD 16:46)
  - "In the sight of heaven, their children are illegitimate." (JoD 16:46, about non-LDS marriages)
  - Jacob defense: "Here were four women married to Jacob... God so honored the twelve sons of Jacob's four polygamic wives, that he made them the heads, the patriarchs of the whole twelve tribes of Israel." (JoD 16:86-87)
  - On entering New Jerusalem: "I don't want to go in at that gate, the people are polygamists... Do you want to associate with them? Well, I think their society will be a little more pleasant than that of those old polygamists?" (JoD 16:90, satirizing anti-polygamists)

  - "We have the privilege of believing in the Old Testament as well as the New." (JoD 16:96)

#### 2. Temples to Be Built to the Name of the Lord
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** Oct 7, 1873
- **Pages:** JoD 16:251-262
- **Flag Reason:** Children born outside eternal covenant are "illegitimate in sight of heaven," extensive temple marriage doctrine
- **Topics:** Temple ordinances, Eternal marriage, Legitimacy, Polygamy implications
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "All you young people who have been married in this Territory or abroad, by justices of the peace, or even by Elders of the Church only for time... your children are illegitimate in the sight of heaven." (JoD 16:46)
  - "In the sight of heaven, their children are illegitimate. Of course they are legitimate according to the laws of the country... But when I say illegitimate I mean in the sight of heaven." (JoD 16:46)
  - "There must be a Temple. God has ordained a building of a different pattern wherein laws, statutes, judgments, and ordinances are to be revealed." (JoD 16:38)
  - "All children born out of the covenant... have to be made legitimate heirs... according to the ordinance and law of adoption." (JoD 16:49-50)
  - On Urim and Thummim: "When that instrument is restored to the house of God... our ancestry... will be made manifest... by the manifestation of holy angels in his house, and by the Urim and Thummim." (JoD 16:64)

#### 3. The Increased Powers and Capacities of Man in His Future State
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** Jan 27, 1874
- **Pages:** JoD 16:353-368
- **Flag Reason:** Cosmological claims about immortal bodies' powers, vision, hearing across vast distances
- **Topics:** Cosmology, Resurrection, Future state, Divine capacities
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Song Potential:** ★★★
- **Key Quotes:**
  - Vision claim: "Moses... beheld every particle of the earth, and there was not a particle of it which he did not behold, discerning it by the Spirit of God." (JoD 16:60)
  - "If a man in a state of mortality could have his vision so enlarged that he could see all these particles at once, what may be expected when we are immortal?" (JoD 16:62)
  - On God's vision: "The Lord's eye can pierce all these creations, and he can hold them, as it were, in his hand... his eye can pierce them." (JoD 16:65)
  - "We shall be like him" - extensive discourse on humans becoming gods: "Man is a God in embryo... They shall be Gods, even the Sons of God" (JoD 16:82)
  - Hearing claims: "Sounds, voices or noises in one world might be transferred through that medium to the immortal beings in another world." (JoD 16:57)
  - "When the first angel among the seven shall sound his trump, all nations and kindreds and tongues of the earth shall hear it... Every ear shall hear the sound of that trump." (JoD 16:58)
  - Sleep unnecessary: "Will man, after the resurrection, require sleep? I think not... it looks inconsistent... like an imperfection in the great work of the Creator, to suppose that for about one-third part of all future eternity intelligent beings are to forget even their own existence in slumber." (JoD 16:42)
  - Eating unnecessary: "Immortality was stamped upon their very systems, and they would have been this day alive had they not transgressed... whether they had eaten food or not." (JoD 16:38)
  - Heat resistance: "I do not believe that heat will have any tendency to dissolve, destroy, injure or to produce any unpleasant effects upon them." (JoD 16:44)
  - Gravity control: "He had a superior power given to him, by which he could control the law of universal gravitation just as he pleased, as in the case of his ascension from the Mount of Olives." (JoD 16:46)
  - Multiple creations: References to "numberless creations" - "Were it possible that man could number all the particles of this earth and millions of earths like this, it would not be a beginning to the number of thy creations." (JoD 16:64)
  - Future comprehension: "Man will possess in a future state... being able to comprehend more than one thing at a time... he can grasp in his vision myriads of things at once." (JoD 16:88)
  - Seeing the future: "The faculty of seeing and comprehending that which will take place in the future ages of eternity, for millions of years to come." (JoD 16:90)
