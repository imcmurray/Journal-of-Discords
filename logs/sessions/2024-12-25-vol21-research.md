## Key Findings

### HIGH Priority Sermons (5)

#### 1. Visions of Moses (JoD 21:286-296)
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Date:** July 18, 1880
**Topics:** Adam-God, Pre-existence, Polygamy doctrine, Cosmology
**Flag Reasons:**
- **Immortal Adam before the fall**: "When he made these two intelligent beings and placed them upon this creation, he made them after his own likeness and his own image. He did not make them mortal, but he made them immortal, like unto himself."
- **Fall brought mortality**: Contradicts modern understanding that Adam was always mortal
- **Command to multiply given to immortals**: "Was there any commandment given to those two immortal beings before the fall? There was one commandment, namely: 'Be fruitful and multiply, and replenish the earth.' What! Did the Lord command two immortal beings to multiply their species! He did."
- **Post-resurrection procreation**: "I cannot believe that Adam and Eve, during these nineteen centuries, have been in idleness...but I believe that during this time they have been fulfilling literally the commandment that was given to them in the morn of creation and as immortal beings have brought forth immortal sons and daughters since their resurrection."
- **Polygamy argument from eternal marriage**: "All these principles that I have treated upon, pertaining to eternal marriage, the very moment that they are admitted to be true, it brings in plurality of marriage, and if plurality of marriage is not true...then marriage for eternity is not true: and your faith is all vain."

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★
**Priority:** HIGH - Multiple falsifiable claims about biology and marriage theology

#### 2. Pre-existence of Man, Animals, and Earth (JoD 21:197-206)
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Date:** November 12, 1879
**Topics:** Cosmology, Creation doctrine, Pre-existence
**Flag Reasons:**
- **Spiritual creation of animals**: "We learn...that not only all the children of men, of all generations, and of all ages, were created spiritually in heaven, but that fish and fowls, and beast, and all animated things, having life, were first made spiritual in heaven...before bodies of flesh were prepared for them on the earth"
- **First flesh on earth**: "Among all the animated creatures of flesh, man was the first that was ever placed upon the earth in this temporal condition, contradicting the theories of geologists"
- **No death before fall**: "Was there any death in this creation after the temporal was formed, before the fall? No. Were any birds of the air subject to death? No."
- **Literal earth transformation**: Describes baptism of earth by water (flood), baptism by fire (millennium), and final glorification
- **Earth has intelligence/agency**: "The earth abideth a law of a celestial kingdom, for it filleth the measure of its creation, and transgresseth not the law"

**Song Potential:** ★★★★
**Priority:** HIGH - Directly contradicts geology and evolutionary biology

#### 3. Law of Celestial Marriage (JoD 21:9-13)
**Speaker:** Joseph F. Smith
**Date:** December 7, 1879 (funeral sermon)
**Topics:** Polygamy doctrine, Authority claims
**Flag Reasons:**
- **Polygamy as essential doctrine**: "This doctrine of eternal union of husband and wife, and of plural marriage, is one of the most important doctrines ever revealed to man in any age of the world. Without it man would come to a full stop, without it we never could be exalted to associate with and become gods"
- **William Clayton wrote revelation**: "It was his pen that wrote for the first time the revelation in relation to the eternity of the marriage covenant and of a plurality of wives"
- **Polygamy required for godhood**: "Neither could we attain to the power of eternal increase, or the blessings pronounced upon Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, the fathers of the faithful"
- **Failed predictions**: This was 1879 - polygamy was abandoned 11 years later (1890)

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★
**Priority:** HIGH - Core doctrine later disavowed

## Topic Analysis

### Cosmology
Volume 21 is **extraordinarily rich** in cosmological material, primarily from Orson Pratt:
- Pre-existence of humans AND animals in spirit form
- Man created before animals (contradicts geology/evolution)
- No death before fall for any creature
- Adam and Eve immortal before fall
- Earth has agency/intelligence
- Earth baptized by water (flood), will be baptized by fire
- Laws of physics as divine laws
- Multiple sermons on world-making and transformation

**Assessment:** This is one of the strongest volumes for cosmology content. Orson Pratt's sermons are systematic, detailed, and highly falsifiable.

### Polygamy
Extremely strong material:
- Joseph F. Smith's funeral sermon declaring polygamy essential for godhood
- Orson Pratt's detailed argument that eternal marriage requires polygamy
- Both sermons delivered 1879-1880, just 10-11 years before Official Declaration 1 (1890)

**Assessment:** Critical for demonstrating the centrality of polygamy doctrine just before abandonment.

### Prophecy/This Generation Claims
Multiple failed prophecy claims:
- Wilford Woodruff's "Christ coming in this generation" (1880)
- Destruction of American nation imminent
- Missouri temple with cloud of glory
- Ten tribes returning with highway cast up
- Temple in Logan with specific predictions

**Assessment:** Strong material for failed prophecy category.

### Adam-God/Creation
Not explicit Adam-God doctrine, but related material:
- Adam created immortal
- Adam commanded to multiply as immortal
- Eve not deceived, Adam chose to fall
- Post-resurrection procreation by Adam and Eve

**Assessment:** Supports Adam-God implications without explicit statement.

### Blood Atonement
**None found** in this volume.

### Racial Content
Limited direct racial material:
- References to "seed of Cain" in multiple sermons
- "Lamanites will fulfill all that God has said about them" (Woodruff)
- Israel/Gentile breeding program (Penrose)

**Assessment:** Present but not as strong as other volumes.

## Research Notes

### Volume Character
Volume 21 is **theologically dense** and **systematically argued**. Orson Pratt dominates the volume with what amounts to systematic theology lectures on:
- Cosmology and creation
- Marriage and polygamy
- Laws of nature as divine laws
- Pre-existence and resurrection

This is NOT a volume of casual remarks or passing comments. These are formal, structured theological arguments presented as authoritative doctrine.

### Historical Context (1879-1880)
- **10 years before Manifesto** - polygamy still taught as essential
- **Reynolds v. United States** (1879) - Supreme Court upheld anti-polygamy laws
- **Edmunds Act** (1882) - Just 2 years away when these sermons given
- Church under legal pressure but doubling down on doctrine

### Apologetic Challenges
The material in this volume will be difficult to defend because:
1. **Systematic not casual** - Can't dismiss as off-the-cuff remarks
2. **Multiple speakers** - Not just one man's opinion
3. **Presented as doctrine** - Explicitly taught as revealed truth
4. **Timing** - Close enough to abandonment to show discontinuity
5. **Specificity** - Details make claims testable and falsifiable

### Song Development Challenges
- **Length** - Orson Pratt's arguments are detailed; hard to reduce to lyrics
- **Technicality** - Cosmology material requires scientific literacy
- **Multiple claims** - Each sermon has several falsifiable claims; pick the strongest

**Solution:** Focus on single strongest claim per song, use others as verses/bridges.
