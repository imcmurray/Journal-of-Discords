## Major Findings

### Finding 1: Celestial Marriage REQUIRED for Exaltation
**Sermon:** JoD 22:120-132
**Speaker:** George Q. Cannon (First Counselor, First Presidency)
**Date:** October 31, 1880, Tooele City
**Priority:** ★★★★★ HIGHEST

**Key Quotes:**
> "Some men think they can slip around—I have heard such men talk—they think they are going to get into the celestial kingdom without obeying the law of celestial marriage. I do not have any such ideas about exaltation"

> "If we live in the flesh, you may depend upon it we shall be tried in all things. If I have an appetite, if I have a passion, if I have an inclination which is in conflict with the law of God, if I do not subdue it and bring it into complete subjection to His law, I do not see how I can enter into celestial glory."

> "No woman can enter into the celestial kingdom any more than a man whose will is in opposition to the will of God."

**Analysis:**
- Explicit requirement: Celestial marriage (polygamy) necessary for celestial kingdom
- Conditional exceptions: Men who never married/only had one wife can ONLY be saved if they prove "willingness" and "perfection"
- Women must submit to plural marriage or forfeit exaltation
- Direct contradiction of current LDS teaching (polygamy not required, was "for a time")

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★
**Apologetic Responses to Anticipate:**
1. "That was Cannon's opinion, not official doctrine" — COUNTER: He was First Counselor speaking from pulpit
2. "Polygamy was for that dispensation only" — COUNTER: Cannon speaks of eternal law, not temporary
3. "He's talking about eternal principle, not earthly practice" — COUNTER: "If we live in the flesh" makes clear it's about mortal practice

### Finding 2: Curse of Cain Continued Through Ham's Wife
**Sermon:** JoD 22:297-312
**Speaker:** John Taylor (President of the Church)
**Date:** August 28, 1881, Provo
**Priority:** ★★★★★ HIGHEST

**Key Quotes:**
> "After the flood we are told that the curse that had been pronounced upon Cain was continued through Ham's wife, as he had married a wife of that seed."

> "And why did it pass through the flood? Because it was necessary that the devil should have a representation upon the earth as well as God; and that man should be a free agent to act for himself"

**Analysis:**
- Explicit racial theology: Curse of Cain (dark skin, no priesthood) continued through Noah's flood
- Theological purpose: Black people are "devil's representation on earth"
- Pre-existence tie-in: Righteous spirits in heaven demanded God destroy wicked to avoid corrupt bodies
- Most extreme racial teaching in Volume 22
- Direct quotation from PRESIDENT of the church (not subordinate)

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★
**Topics:** Race (Curse of Cain), Pre-existence, Theodicy
**Status:** Officially DISAVOWED by LDS Church in 2013 Gospel Topics Essay "Race and the Priesthood"

**Apologetic Responses to Anticipate:**
1. "Taylor was speaking as a man, not prophet" — COUNTER: He was President, speaking from pulpit in official capacity
2. "This was common 19th century view" — COUNTER: Doesn't excuse prophetic claim, and theology is unique
3. "Church has disavowed this" — AGREE, but this proves prophets taught false doctrine
4. "Ham's wife isn't in Bible" — CORRECT: This is extra-biblical Mormon tradition

### Finding 3: Word of Wisdom Violations and Jackson County Return
**Sermon:** JoD 22:98-109
**Speaker:** George Q. Cannon
**Date:** July 25, 1880, Salt Lake City
**Priority:** ★★★★ HIGH

**Key Quotes on Word of Wisdom:**
> "When I think about it, when I see our people, after what God has said upon this subject, after the plain manner in which he has spoken to us and told us what would be the result of the observance of certain laws, deliberately day after day flying in the face of the counsel which God has given unto us in that Word of Wisdom, I get exceedingly amazed and I wonder how it is that God bears with us."

> "We see young men learning to drink liquor, to smoke and chew tobacco, and acquiring this habit and the other habit which is expressly forbidden"

> "Supposing here are a good many young men that belong to this Church, some of whom are very eager for knowledge—reading books, studying, going to the University, imagining that is the most direct and easy way to obtain it, and at the same time these same young men, members of the Church, drinking their tea and coffee and smoking their cigarettes."

**Key Quotes on Jackson County:**
> "I will tell you what I have sometimes thought: that the Lord is going to deal with us as he did with the Israelites. They hardened their hearts against the Lord, became careless and disobedient, and finally the Lord, in His wrath, decreed that none of them, with the exception of Caleb and Joshua, should enter the promised land. The words that are used are very expressive. Their carcasses were to fall in the wilderness, all over a certain age. But the Lord spared the little ones. He raised up a new generation and led them to the promised land. We have the same promise that some will be left to go back to the promised land, and I feel satisfied it will be fulfilled."

**Analysis:**
- In 1880, Word of Wisdom widely violated by members (including young men at University)
- Cannon "exceedingly amazed" God tolerates violations
- Jackson County return still expected, but Cannon hints older generation may not make it (like Israelites in wilderness)
- Prophecy timing issue: 47 years after Missouri expulsion (1833), still expecting return

**Song Potential:** ★★★ MEDIUM
**Topics:** Word of Wisdom (Evolution), Jackson County (Failed Prophecy)

**Apologetic Responses to Anticipate:**
1. "Word of Wisdom wasn't strictly enforced until 20th century" — AGREE, but Cannon clearly expected it to be obeyed
2. "Jackson County return is still future" — COUNTER: 192 years later, seems unlikely; original prophecies indicated near-term
3. "New generation" interpretation leaves it open-ended — FAIR, but moves goalposts significantly

### Finding 4: Miracles NOT Designed to Convert
**Sermon:** JoD 22:357-368
**Speaker:** George Q. Cannon
**Date:** July 24, 1881, Salt Lake City
**Priority:** ★★★★ HIGH

**Key Quotes:**
> "Jesus himself gave no signs to convince unbelievers. When applied to himself to give a sign, he said it was a wicked and an adulterous generation that sought for a sign, and no sign should be given them."

> "In fact we are told in the Scriptures, that the day would come when miracles should be wrought by false prophets, and men would be deceived by false evidence of this character."

> "If men were to work miracles before me, to convince me of the truth of any system, I could not be convinced by any such evidence."

> "It has been a matter of remark among those who have had experience in this Church, that where men have been brought into the Church by such manifestations, it has required a constant succession of them to keep them in the Church"

**Analysis:**
- Miracles are NOT evidence of divine truth (can be worked by false prophets)
- Gathering of Saints from many nations claimed as greater evidence than miracles
- Spirit-driven unity and love among diverse peoples seen as true evidence
- Contradicts earlier Mormon emphasis on miraculous gifts as evidence

**Song Potential:** ★★★ MEDIUM
**Topics:** Miracles (Inadequate Evidence), Authority Claims

**Apologetic Responses to Anticipate:**
1. "He's being modest/humble" — POSSIBLE, but seems to be making theological argument
2. "He's distinguishing true vs false miracles" — PARTIALLY, but also minimizing miracles generally
3. "Spirit witness is superior to miracles" — AGREE, but this is shift from earlier Mormon emphasis

### 1880-1881 Political Situation
- Utah Territory under federal pressure over polygamy

- Church leadership increasingly defensive

### George Q. Cannon's Role
- First Counselor to John Taylor (1880-1901)
- Previously Utah Territory delegate to Congress (1872-1882)
- Politically savvy, legally embattled
- Defending polygamy while knowing federal prosecution coming

### John Taylor's Presidency
- Succeeded Brigham Young in 1880
- Would die in exile (1887) hiding from federal authorities
- Never renounced polygamy
- These sermons reflect defiant stance early in presidency
