### Sermon #15: The Priesthood to Dictate in Temporal As Well As Spiritual Things
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** June 16, 1867
**Pages:** JoD 12:56-64
**Status:** ✅ Complete

#### Categories
- **Theocracy** (primary)
- **Economic control**
- **Apostates**

#### Key Findings

**1. Three Classes of Poor (★★★★★ - Song Potential: HIGH)**
- **Quote:** "Hence we have the Lord's poor, the devil's poor, and poor devils." (JoD 12:56)
- **Context:** Brigham dividing the poor into categories while arguing against economic equality
- **TONE:** Shockingly callous and elitist for someone claiming to represent Christ
- **Song Potential:** "Three Kinds of Poor" - ironic contrast with Jesus's teachings on the poor

**2. Joseph's Vision Against Trading with Gentiles (★★★★★ - Song Potential: HIGHEST)**
- **Quote:** "You may think that I saw him in vision, and it was a vision, given right in broad daylight. Said he—'Never spend another day to build up a Gentile city, but spend your days, dollars, and dimes for the upbuilding of the Zion of God upon the earth, to promote peace and righteousness and to prepare for the coming of the Son of Man, and he who does not abide this law will suffer loss.' That is a saying of one of the apostles of the Lord Jesus Christ. He said it to me. Do you want to know his name? It is not recorded in the New Testament among the apostles, but it was an apostle whom the Lord called and ordained in this my day, and in the day of a good portion of this congregation, and his name was Joseph Smith, junior. These words were delivered to me in July, 1833, in the town of Kirtland, Geauga County, State of Ohio." (JoD 12:59)
- **Context:** Brigham claiming Joseph told him in a vision to boycott non-Mormon businesses
- **SPECIFICITY:** Exact date, location provided - but no documented revelation exists
- **Problem:** This "revelation" is not in D&C or any historical record. Brigham appears to be inventing revelations and attributing them to Joseph.
- **Song Potential:** "Joseph Told Me (In My Mind)" - about leaders claiming private revelations to control economics

**3. Eternal Damnation Threat for Trading with Apostates (★★★★★)**
- **Quote:** "I will promise those who feel thus that they will never enter the celestial Kingdom of our Father and God. That is my business. It is my business to preach the truth to the people, and it will be my business by and by to testify for the just and to bear witness against the ungodly." (JoD 12:59)
- **Context:** Threatening eternal damnation for trading with ex-Mormons
- **TONE:** Tyrannical, cult-like control
- **Category:** Economic coercion, shunning

**4. Apostates as "Poor, Miserable Devils" (★★★★)**
- **Quote:** "Never, from this time henceforth and forever, sustain a man, men, a people, a community, or anybody that operates against or forsakes the Kingdom of God. Do you know what I call them, or have you forgotten what I said about the poor of this world? The Lord has chosen them, it is true, but He has not chosen the devil's poor nor the poor devils. They who forsake or operate against the Kingdom of God are what I call poor, miserable devils. That is a harsh expression, especially to come from the pulpit, but I built this stand to say just what I pleased in it." (JoD 12:61)
- **IRONY:** "I built this stand to say just what I pleased in it" - admitting it's his personal opinion, not revelation
- **Song Potential:** "I Built This Stand"

**5. Sons of Perdition Prediction (★★★)**
- **Quote:** "Every apostate who ever received this gospel in faith, and had the Spirit of it, will have to repent in sackcloth and ashes, and sacrifice all he possesses, or be a son of perdition, go down to hell, and there dwell with the damned" (JoD 12:62)
- **Category:** Extreme threats against apostates
- **IRONIC:** Modern LDS church doesn't teach this

### Sermon #20: Condition of Apostates
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** June 30, 1867
**Pages:** JoD 12:93-98
**Status:** ✅ Complete

#### Categories
- **Apostates** (primary)
- **Polygamy** (secondary)

#### Key Findings

**1. Devil's Opinion of Apostates (★★★★★ - Song Potential: HIGHEST)**
- **Quote:** "If there is a despicable character on the face of the earth, it is an apostate from this Church. He is a traitor who has deceived his best friends, betrayed his trust, and forfeited every principle of honor that God placed within him. They may think they are respected, but they are not. They are disgraced in their own eyes. There is not much honesty within them; they have forfeited their heaven, sold their birthright, and betrayed their friends. What will the devil do with such characters? Will he have them in his kingdom? Yes, he will be obliged to, because he is an apostate himself. He apostatized from the Celestial Kingdom, and was thrust down to hell. Yet, when apostates get to his kingdom, he will say—'I do not like you, for you are just as mean as I am. I was a traitor and a liar, and I am yet. I despise myself and every character that betrays his trust.'" (JoD 12:93-94)
- **BIZARRE:** Brigham claims to know what the devil will say to apostates in hell
- **Song Potential:** "Even the Devil Hates You" - darkly comedic song from devil's perspective

**2. No Bible Christians Anywhere (★★★)**
- **Quote:** "My brother Joseph once said to me (and we were both Methodists at the time), 'Brother Brigham, there is not a Bible Christian in the world; what will become of the people?' For many years no person saw a smile on his countenance, in consequence of the burden of the Lord being upon him, and realizing that the inhabitants of the earth had all gone out of the way and had turned every man to his own views." (JoD 12:94-95)
- **Context:** Pre-conversion story about Joseph's pessimism
- **Interesting:** Joseph as Methodist was apparently never happy

**3. Polygamy Threats to Women (★★★★★ - Song Potential: HIGH)**
- **Quote:** "Woe to you who do it; you will lose your crowns as sure as God lives. Be careful! 'Well,' but say you, 'these men, these elders of Israel, have it all their own way.' That is not so, and we are not going to have it all our own way, unless our way is to do just right. And the man and woman who set up their will against the providence of God, will be found wanting when accounts are squared. They will have to say, 'the summer is past, the harvest is ended, and we have not received our crowns.' Will you think of this, sisters, you who are not married as well as you who are? I have a good many daughters, but it would be better for every one of my daughters, and for every female in this Church, to marry men who have proved themselves to be men of God, no matter how many wives they have, than to take these miserable characters who are running around here." (JoD 12:97)
- **COERCION:** Explicit threat to women who refuse polygamy - "you will lose your crowns"
- **IRONY:** Calling young single men "miserable characters" while promoting old polygamists
- **Song Potential:** "Better to Be Wife #7" - about pressure on young women

### Sermon #39: Forbearance to Each Other
**Speaker:** Heber C. Kimball
**Date:** April 12, 1868
**Pages:** JoD 12:188-191
**Status:** ✅ Complete

#### Categories
- **Adam-God** (confirmed!)
- **Polygamy threats**
- **Personal revelation claims**

#### Key Findings

**1. ADAM'S ALTAR - Location Claim (★★★★★ - Song Potential: HIGHEST)**
- **Quote:** "I have been to the altar where Adam offered sacrifices and blessed his son and then left them and went to heaven." (JoD 12:189)
- **BIZARRE:** Heber claiming to have physically visited Adam's altar
- **Later:** "I have been, as I have already told you, to where Adam offered sacrifices and blessed his sons, and I felt as though there were hundreds of angels there, and there were angels there like unto the three Nephites. I have also been over the hill Cumorah, and I understand all about it." (JoD 12:190)
- **SPECIFICITY:** Claims personal visitation to Adam's altar with angels present
- **Problem:** Where is this altar? Modern church denies these specific location claims
- **Song Potential:** "I've Been to Adam's Altar" - about false specificity in visions

**2. Polygamy Removal Threat (★★★★★)**
- **Quote:** "There are hundreds and thousands of men in this Church today who have a plurality of wives which will be taken from them and they cannot help themselves, because they do not keep the celestial law." (JoD 12:189)
- **IRONY:** Threat that God will remove plural wives from unworthy men
- **Context:** 1868 - at height of polygamy practice
- **Modern irony:** God DID take them away - via federal law, not unworthiness

**3. Infantile Saints Comparison (★★★)**
- **Quote:** "They have to be watched like an infant running around the house, that knows no better than to take the carving knife or fork and fall upon it and put out its eyes." (JoD 12:56)
- **Category:** Condescending view of membership

**4. Never Made to Die Spiritually (★★★)**
- **Quote:** "And now let me tell you that I was never made to die, that is spiritually; but that I am an inhabitant of this earth and will never destroy my right to it. It is my Father's and I know it, and His angels administer to men." (JoD 12:190)
- **Category:** Personal exaltation claim

### Sermon #51: The Gospel a Perfect System
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** Aug 9, 1868
**Pages:** JoD 12:255-263
**Status:** ✅ Complete

#### Categories
- **Polygamy** (primary)
- **Historical revisionism**

#### Key Findings

**1. Monogamy Invented by Roman Thieves (★★★★★ - Song Potential: HIGHEST)**
- **Quote:** "Just ask yourselves, historians, when was monogamy introduced on to the face of the earth? When those buccaneers, who settled on the peninsula where Rome now stands, could not steal women enough to have two or three apiece, they passed a law that a man should have but one woman. And this started monogamy and the downfall of the plurality system. In the days of Jesus, Rome, having dominion over Jerusalem, they carried out the doctrine more or less. This was the rise, start and foundation of the doctrine of monogamy; and never till then was there a law passed, that we have any knowledge of, that a man should have but one wife." (JoD 12:262)
- **HISTORICALLY FALSE:** Complete fabrication - monogamy existed in many cultures before Rome
- **IRONY:** Bible explicitly allows monogamy; Jesus never taught polygamy
- **Song Potential:** "When the Buccaneers Ran Out of Women" - absurd history lesson

**2. Polygamy Will Save the World Morally (★★★★★)**
- **Quote:** "This doctrine so hateful and annoying to the feelings of many, was revealed from heaven to Joseph Smith, and obedience is required to it by the Latter-day Saints—this very principle will work out the moral salvation of the world." (JoD 12:260)
- **Argument:** Women should force men to marry them, then monogamy will work
- **BIZARRE logic:** Polygamy exists to force men to treat women better
- **Modern irony:** Abandoned as immoral 20 years later

**3. Brigham's Personal Preference for Monogamy (★★★★)**
- **Quote:** "I should have been in the beginning; the one wife system would not have disagreed with me at all. If the prophet had said to me, 'Brother Brigham, you can never have but one wife at a time.' I should have said, 'glory, hallelujah, that is just what I like.' But he said, 'you will have to take more than one wife, and this order has to spread and increase until the inhabitants of the earth repent of their evils and men will do what is right towards the females." (JoD 12:261)
- **IRONY:** Brigham admitting he'd prefer one wife, but claiming Joseph commanded polygamy
- **Song Potential:** "Glory Hallelujah (Just One Wife)" - Brigham's reluctant polygamy

**4. Willing to Give Up Wives (★★★)**
- **Quote:** "If you will produce this to-day, I'll tell you what I would be willing to do, I would be willing to give up half or two-thirds of my wives, or to let the whole of them go, if it was necessary" (JoD 12:262)
- **TONE:** Cavalier attitude toward wives as property
- **Callousness:** Treating wives as negotiable commodities

**5. New York City Will Become Ruins (★★★ - failed prophecy)**
- **Quote:** Referenced in context of America's need to repent
- **Category:** Apocalyptic prophecy

### Sermon #64: America a Choice Land—Its Aborigines
**Speaker:** Orson Pratt
**Date:** Dec 27, 1868
**Pages:** JoD 12:338-346
**Status:** ✅ Complete

#### Categories
- **Adam-God geography**
- **Book of Mormon geography**
- **Failed prophecy**
- **Native Americans**

#### Key Findings

**1. Adam Lived in America (★★★★★ - Song Potential: HIGHEST)**
- **Quote:** "The very first man who had dominion on the face of the earth, under the direction of the Heavens, once dwelt on this Continent. His name was Adam. Whether his first residence was on this land, whether the garden that was planted for his occupation was on this Continent, or some other, is not revealed in any written or printed revelation. But he certainly did, in the course of his lifetime, either from this being his native land, or by emigration, actually come in possession of this part of the globe" (JoD 12:338)
- **SPECIFICITY:** "It was here where Adam, Seth, Enos, Cainan, Mahalaleel, Jared, Enoch, Methusaleh and Noah dwelt. It was on this land where Noah built his ark, which was blown by the winds of Heaven away to the east, and landed on Ararat." (JoD 12:338)
- **BIZARRE:** Complete rewrite of Genesis geography
- **Modern disavowal:** LDS church now says this is speculation, not doctrine
- **Song Potential:** "Noah Built His Ark in Missouri" - absurd geography claims

**2. Enoch's City in America (★★★★)**
- **Quote:** "It was here where Enoch preached and published glad tidings of great joy; it was on this land—the American Continent—where he gathered the people from many nations, and built up a city and called it Zion...and when he spake the word of the Lord the earth upon which he stood trembled and shook by the power of the Almighty, and the mountains fled from before his presence, and the great rivers of this Continent were turned out of their courses" (JoD 12:338)
- **Category:** American exceptionalism, Book of Mormon cosmology

**3. Jaredite Vessels with Holes (★★★★ - Apologetic Defense)**
- **Quote:** Detailed defense of Book of Mormon's submarine-like vessels: "These vessels were built, not in the form of a tea saucer as has been represented by some 'anti-Mormons' in their discussions; but the Book of Mormon informs us that they were peaked at the ends, and enlarged as they came to the middle, and they were tight like a dish on the water, and were very light, like to the lightness of a fowl." (JoD 12:339-340)
- **IRONY:** Defending patently absurd Book of Mormon story
- **Note:** Modern apologetics still struggles with this

**4. New York City in Ruins - Failed Prophecy (★★★★★ - Song Potential: HIGHEST)**
- **Quote:** "For instance the great, powerful and populous city of New York, that may be considered one of the greatest cities of the world, will in a few years become a mass of ruins. The people will wonder while gazing on the ruins that cost hundreds of millions to build, what has become of its inhabitants. Their houses will be there, but they will be left desolate. So saith the Lord God. That will be only a sample of numerous other towns and cities on the face of this continent." (JoD 12:342)
- **SPECIFICITY:** "in a few years" - spoken in 1868
- **FAILURE:** New York City is now the most populous city in America, 157 years later
- **Song Potential:** "New York in Ruins (Any Day Now)" - failed prophecy song

**5. Civil War as "Just the Beginning" (★★★★)**
- **Quote:** "This great war is only a small degree of chastisement, just the beginning; nothing compared to that which God has spoken concerning this nation, if they will not repent...He will send a desolating scourge on the land; that He will leave their cities desolate, without inhabitants." (JoD 12:342)
- **Context:** Orson claiming Civil War was only the start of God's judgment
- **FAILURE:** No such desolation occurred

**6. Some Latter-day Saints Will Live to See It (★★★)**
- **Quote:** "But there are some in this congregation who will live, to behold the fulfillment of these other things, and will visit the ruins of mighty towns and cities scattered over the face of this land destitute and desolate of inhabitants." (JoD 12:342)
- **FAILURE:** Everyone in that 1868 congregation is now dead; prophecy failed
