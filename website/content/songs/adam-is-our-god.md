## The Quote

> "Now hear it, O inhabitants of the earth, Jew and Gentile, Saint and sinner! When our father Adam came into the garden of Eden, he came into it with a celestial body, and brought Eve, one of his wives, with him. He helped to make and organize this world. He is MICHAEL, the Archangel, the ANCIENT OF DAYS! about whom holy men have written and spoken—HE is our FATHER and our GOD, and the only God with whom WE have to do. Every man upon the earth, professing Christians or non-professing, must hear it, and will know it sooner or later."
> — Brigham Young, JoD 1:50 (April 9, 1852)

## Historical Context

**The Setting:** April 9, 1852 — Salt Lake Tabernacle. This sermon, titled "Self-Government—Mysteries—Recreation and Amusements, not in Themselves Sinful—Tithing—Adam, Our Father and Our God," was delivered during General Conference.

**The Speaker:** Brigham Young — Second President of the LDS Church (1847-1877), Prophet, Seer, and Revelator. Young taught this doctrine repeatedly throughout his presidency.

**The Doctrine:** Young taught that Adam was not merely the first man, but was actually God the Father in a mortal body, having come to earth with Eve (one of his celestial wives) to begin the human race.

**The Aftermath:**
- The "Adam-God doctrine" was taught in LDS temples during Brigham Young's lifetime
- Later church presidents distanced themselves from this teaching
- **1976:** Church President Spencer W. Kimball called it a "theory" that "we denounce"
- Modern LDS Church considers it false doctrine incompatible with scripture

---

## The Modern Disavowal

> "We denounce that theory and hope that everyone will be cautious against this and other kinds of false doctrine."
> — Spencer W. Kimball, Church News, October 9, 1976

The LDS Church's official Gospel Topics essay on "Becoming Like God" makes no mention of the Adam-God doctrine, effectively treating it as an aberration rather than prophetic teaching.

<summary><strong>Addressing Apologetic Responses</strong></summary>

### "Speaking as a man, not as prophet"
He said "Now hear it, O inhabitants of the earth" and "Every man... must hear it, and will know it sooner or later." This is prophetic declaration language, not personal speculation. It was taught in General Conference and in temple ceremonies.

### "Just his personal theory"
Young taught this repeatedly over 25 years, including in the temple endowment ceremony. Apostle Orson Pratt publicly disagreed and was rebuked. This was not presented as opinion.

### "Church has since clarified it"
If a prophet's solemn declaration from the pulpit that "every man... will know it sooner or later" can be completely false, what prophetic statements can be trusted? The disavowal proves prophets can teach false doctrine as divine truth.

### "He was speaking of something we don't fully understand"
The words are not ambiguous: "HE is our FATHER and our GOD, and the only God with whom WE have to do." Either Adam is God the Father or he isn't. Modern church says he isn't. Brigham said he was.

### "Context matters"
The full context makes it stronger, not weaker. Young elaborates that Adam "helped to make and organize this world," that Jesus was "begotten in the flesh by the same character that was in the garden of Eden," and that denying this doctrine will prove "their salvation or damnation."
