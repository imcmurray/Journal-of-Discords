## The Quote

> "The only men who become Gods, even the Sons of God, are those who enter into polygamy. Others attain unto a glory and may even be permitted to come into the presence of the Father and the Son; but they cannot reign as kings in glory, because they had blessings offered unto them, and they refused to accept them."
> — Brigham Young, JoD 11:269 (August 19, 1866)

---

## The Failed Prophecy

> "If we do not embrace that principle soon, the keys will be turned against us. If we do not keep the same law that our Heavenly Father has kept, we cannot go with him... I wish here to say to the Elders of Israel, and to all the members of this Church and kingdom, that it is in the hearts of many of them to wish that the doctrine of polygamy was not taught and practiced by us... It is the word of the Lord, and I wish to say to you, and all the world, that if you desire with all your hearts to obtain the blessings which Abraham obtained, you will be polygamists at least in your faith, or you will come short of enjoying the salvation and the glory which Abraham has obtained."
> — Brigham Young, JoD 11:269

## Historical Context

**The Setting:** August 19, 1866 — The Bowery, Salt Lake City. Federal pressure on polygamy was intensifying.

**The Speaker:** Brigham Young declared with absolute prophetic authority that polygamy was required for exaltation AND prophesied they would never abandon it for statehood.

**Both Claims Failed:**
- **1890:** Church abandons polygamy practice
- **1896:** Utah achieves statehood
- **Today:** Church promises exaltation to monogamous couples

---

## The Contradiction

**D&C 132** (still canonized scripture) commands polygamy for exaltation.

**Modern Church** promises exaltation to monogamous couples and excommunicates those who practice polygamy.

If Brigham spoke truth, modern Mormons cannot become Gods.
If the modern church is correct, Brigham spoke false prophecy.

---

*Either the founding prophet was catastrophically wrong, or the modern church is selling a false promise.*
