## Key Findings Summary

### Failed Prophecies
1. **Times of Gentiles in one generation** (Pratt, 222-229) - Claims 46 years (1830-1876) is "the generation that the angel comes"
2. **Slavery revelation rejected** (Wells, 95-102) - Lord commanded US Gov to purchase slave freedom, rejected, causing Civil War
3. **Wheat storage famine** (Woodruff, 109-122) - "The day will come when...you will want your wheat for bread"
4. **Jackson County temple** (Woodruff, 122-130) - References laying cornerstone

### Racial Doctrine
1. **Lamanites cursed with dark skin** (Pratt, 16-29) - "Cursed by the Almighty, a skin of darkness came upon them"
2. **Degraded descendants** (Pratt, 16-29) - "How degraded, fallen and sunk into the very depths of darkness"
3. **Skin of darkness for rejection** (Woodruff, 109-122) - "They were cursed...a skin of darkness came upon them"

### Unusual Doctrines
1. **Three Nephites active in 1870s** (Pratt, 16-29; Woodruff, 109-122) - Appearing to Indians in dreams, teaching them Gospel
2. **All JSJ prophecies fulfilled** (Pratt, 222-229) - "In no instance has that man uttered a single prophecy that...has not already been fulfilled, to the very letter, or will not have its fulfillment"

### Polygamy/Celestial Marriage
- Multiple sermons reference celestial marriage, plural marriage, sealing ordinances
- Standard doctrine for the period, no exceptional claims beyond what's already documented

## Song Potential

### Highest Potential (★★★★★)
1. **"The Generation Shall Not Pass"** - Orson Pratt's claim that times of Gentiles fulfilled in 46 years (1830-1876)
   - Direct quote: "How long will it be before the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled? This is a question I cannot fully answer. It will be in the generation that the angel comes. Forty-six years have already transpired..."
   - Can contrast with 2024 reality (148+ years later, still waiting)

2. **"The Lord Commanded"** - Daniel Wells' slavery prophecy
   - Direct quote: "The word of the Lord came through Joseph Smith...to this nation, to free their slaves, and for the Government to pay for them out of the treasury of the United States. Would the people receive the word of the Lord through his servant? No, they would not."
   - Failed divine command claim with historical context

3. **"A Skin of Darkness"** - Multiple speakers on Lamanite curse
   - Multiple direct quotes across sermons
   - Clear racial doctrine, now disavowed
   - Can include three Nephites appearing in dreams to teach Indians

### Medium Potential (★★★)
4. **"Three Men Who Never Die"** - Three Nephites doctrine
   - Appearing to Indians in 1870s, teaching them Gospel
   - Specific claims of dreams, visions, and conversions
   - Ties to broader Lamanite narrative
