## Song Development Potential

### Tier 1 - Immediate Song Candidates (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)

1. **"God Was Once a Man"** - JoD 6:1-11 (King Follett Discourse)
   - Quote: "God himself was once as we are now, and is an exalted Man"
   - Quote: "you have got to learn how to be Gods yourselves"
   - Quote: "the head God brought forth the Gods in the grand council"
   - **Song Potential:** Massive - one of the most controversial LDS doctrines, later disavowed
   - **Apologetic Challenges:** Official LDS sources now distance from this teaching

2. **"Polygamy Is God's Law"** - JoD 6:349-364 (Orson Pratt)
   - Quote: "If polygamy be a crime, the inhabitants of this Territory are in an awful condition"
   - Quote: "The Lord approbated polygamy...gave laws regulating the descent of property in polygamic families"
   - Extended Biblical defense of plural marriage
   - **Song Potential:** High - direct defense of now-abandoned practice
   - **Apologetic Challenges:** Official Declaration ended polygamy in 1890

3. **"Endless Punishment"** - JoD 6:283-298 (Brigham Young)
   - Quote: "Endless is my name. Wherefore, eternal punishment is God's punishment. Endless punishment is God's punishment"
   - Degrees of glory, universal salvation (except sons of perdition)
   - **Song Potential:** Medium-High - challenges traditional hell doctrine, includes interesting temple work descriptions

4. **"The Only God You'll See"** - JoD 6:Unknown (Orson Hyde - Heirship)
   - Quote: "If that same Elder who washed his feet against you in this should be the only God you should ever see or find in the eternal world"
   - Priesthood holders as gods in the next life
   - Saints judging the world NOW (not future judgment)
   - **Song Potential:** High - shocking claim about priesthood authority
   - **Apologetic Challenges:** Contradicts mainstream Christian soteriology

5. **"Nature Is Not Sin"** - JoD 6:Unknown (Brigham Young - Spirit/Will)
   - Quote: "It is nature for the child to be influenced by the Spirit of God"
   - Quote: "They are not 'conceived in sin and brought forth in iniquity,' pertaining to their spirits"
   - Rejection of original sin doctrine
   - Indomitable will implanted by God cannot be broken
   - **Song Potential:** High - contradicts orthodox Christianity and modern LDS emphasis on "natural man"
   - **Apologetic Challenges:** Conflicts with King Benjamin's "natural man is enemy to God"

### Tier 2 - Potential Candidates (⭐⭐⭐)

4. **"God's Provision Through Blood"** - JoD 6:50-52 (Heber C. Kimball)
   - Mild blood atonement language
   - **Song Potential:** Low-Medium - not explicit enough, mostly about self-reliance
