## Historical Significance

Volume 26 represents the LDS Church's final, most comprehensive defense of plural marriage before the Manifesto (1890). These sermons were delivered with:

- President John Taylor in hiding (died in exile 1887)
- Hundreds of Mormon men in prison
- Federal marshals hunting polygamists
- Church assets being seized
- Statehood denied
- Members disenfranchised

The sermons show **absolute refusal to compromise** despite extraordinary pressure. This makes them invaluable primary sources for understanding the depth of 19th-century LDS commitment to plural marriage.
