## Notes

Volume 12 covers 1867-1868, a period of consolidation in Utah. The sermons primarily focus on temporal affairs, cooperative economics, and defending polygamy. Less Adam-God and blood atonement content than earlier volumes, but significant polygamy defense and prophecies about America's destruction.

### Discovery Session Highlights (2025-12-25)

**Best Findings:**
1. **Brigham's undocumented "Joseph vision"** (JoD 12:59) - Claims Joseph appeared to him in 1833 commanding economic boycott of non-Mormons, but no such revelation exists in D&C
2. **Monogamy invented by Roman thieves** (JoD 12:262) - Complete historical fabrication about origins of monogamy
3. **Devil will reject apostates** (JoD 12:93-94) - Brigham claiming to know what Satan will say to apostates in hell
4. **Heber at Adam's altar** (JoD 12:189-190) - Claims physical visitation to Adam's altar with angels present
5. **New York City in ruins "in a few years"** (JoD 12:342) - Failed prophecy from 1868 by Orson Pratt
6. **Three classes of poor** (JoD 12:56) - "Lord's poor, devil's poor, and poor devils"
7. **Noah's ark built in America** (JoD 12:338) - Orson Pratt rewriting Genesis geography
