## High-Priority Sermons (Detailed Analysis Required)

### 1. JoD 22:120-132 — "Difference Between True Church and Churches of the World" (George Q. Cannon)
**Priority:** ★★★★★ HIGH
**Date:** October 31, 1880, Tooele City

**Key Findings:**
- **Celestial Marriage REQUIRED:** "Some men think they can slip around...they think they are going to get into the celestial kingdom without obeying the law of celestial marriage. I do not have any such ideas about exaltation"
- **Conditional salvation only:** Men who never had wives or only one wife can ONLY be exalted IF they prove "willingness" and "perfection before God"
- **Explicit requirement:** "If we live in the flesh, you may depend upon it we shall be tried in all things" including celestial marriage
- **No exceptions:** Must obey celestial marriage law or cannot enter celestial kingdom
- **Wives must submit:** Women must bring their will into subjection to God's will on plural marriage

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★
**Topics:** Polygamy (Required), Exaltation (Conditional)

### 2. JoD 22:297-312 — "Duties of the Saints, etc." (John Taylor)
**Priority:** ★★★★★ HIGH
**Date:** August 28, 1881, Provo

**Key Findings:**
- **Curse of Cain doctrine:** "After the flood we are told that the curse that had been pronounced upon Cain was continued through Ham's wife, as he had married a wife of that seed"
- **Purpose of curse:** "And why did it pass through the flood? Because it was necessary that the devil should have a representation upon the earth as well as God"
- **Racial theology:** Black people are devil's representation on earth to provide opposition
- **Pre-existence teaching:** Spirits waiting for bodies had right to protest wicked parentage; God destroyed wicked to protect righteous spirits
- **Enoch's translation:** City of Enoch gathered and translated
- **Authority claims:** Threatens excommunication for taking water disputes to civil courts instead of church courts
- **Priesthood succession:** Detailed chain from Adam through Joseph Smith to current leadership

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★
**Topics:** Race (Curse of Cain), Pre-existence, Authority (Absolute), Blood Atonement (implied)

---

### 3. JoD 22:98-109 — "Office of the Holy Spirit, etc." (George Q. Cannon)
**Priority:** ★★★★ HIGH
**Date:** July 25, 1880, Salt Lake City

**Key Findings:**
- **Word of Wisdom violations:** Extensive criticism of Saints drinking tea/coffee, using tobacco despite revelation
- **Promise of knowledge:** "If we will observe it, we shall have health...and treasures of knowledge and wisdom given to us"
- **Jackson County return:** Reference to returning to promised land
- **Class distinctions condemned:** Gospel should produce equality, but inequality growing among Saints
- **Suffering required:** Must bring "broken heart and contrite spirit" as sacrifice
- **Modern challenges:** Liquor saloons, gambling, brothels in Utah seen as trials to test faith
- **Prophecy about younger generation:** Hint that older generation may not return to Missouri, similar to Israelites in wilderness

**Song Potential:** ★★★ MEDIUM
**Topics:** Word of Wisdom (Prophecy), Jackson County (Failed), Equality (Violation)

### 4. JoD 22:357-368 — "The Gathering, etc." (George Q. Cannon)
**Priority:** ★★★★ HIGH
**Date:** July 24, 1881, Salt Lake City

**Key Findings:**
- **Miracles NOT for conversion:** "Jesus himself gave no signs to convince unbelievers...a wicked and adulterous generation sought for a sign"
- **Miracles by false prophets:** Warns that miracles can be worked by false prophets and deceivers
- **No parallel in history:** Claims gathering of Mormons is "one of the most remarkable phenomena that has ever been witnessed"
- **Spirit-driven gathering:** People gathered not by human persuasion but by irresistible spiritual compulsion
- **Unity as evidence:** Love and unity among diverse peoples claimed as greater evidence than miracles
- **Gathering prophecy:** Fulfillment of Revelation 14:6-8 (angel with everlasting gospel) and Isaiah 2:2-3 (mountain of the Lord's house)

**Song Potential:** ★★★ MEDIUM
**Topics:** Miracles (Inadequate), Authority (Spiritual Compulsion), Gathering (Prophesied)

## Survey Notes

**Volume Context:** 1880-1881, Utah Territory under increasing federal pressure over polygamy. Sermons show defensive posture regarding plural marriage while also emphasizing absolute authority of church leadership.

**George Q. Cannon prominence:** As First Counselor in First Presidency (1880-1901), Cannon gave 14 of 66 sermons in this volume. His teachings on required celestial marriage and Word of Wisdom are particularly significant.

**John Taylor era:** These sermons come early in John Taylor's presidency (1880-1887). Taylor's sermon includes one of the most explicit "curse of Cain" doctrines in JoD, framing it as necessary for devil's representation on earth.

**Tone:** Generally defensive about persecution, but also confident in authority claims. Emphasis on gathering, temple work, and obedience to leadership.

**Historical significance:** Volume 22 captures church leadership's thinking during crucial transition period as federal government increased anti-polygamy enforcement.
