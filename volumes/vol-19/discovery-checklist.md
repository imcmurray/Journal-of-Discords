## HIGH Priority Findings (8)

### 1. "There is a God" - Erastus Snow (JoD 19:266-279)
**Category:** Adam-God, Cosmology, Theology
**Song Potential:** ★★★★★
**Key Quotes:**
- "What, do you mean we should understand that Deity consists of man and woman? Most certainly I do."
- "There is not in all the eternities that exist, nor ever will be, a God in any other way... a man and a woman; the male and the female."
- "If I believe anything that God has ever said about himself... I must believe that Deity consists of man and woman."
- Uses extended metaphor: "I sometimes illustrate this matter by taking up a pair of shears... they are necessarily parts, one of another... neither one of them is fitted for the accomplishment of their works alone."
- "The man is not without the woman, nor the woman without the man in the Lord."

**Why HIGH:**
- Most explicit statement in entire JoD about God being both male and female
- Directly addresses Adam-God concepts
- Utterly abandoned by modern LDS theology
- Song-worthy due to the shears metaphor and bold assertions

## Notes

Volume 19 is remarkably rich in doctrinal material now disavowed or significantly modified:

1. **Adam-God/Mother God:** Erastus Snow's "There is a God" is perhaps the most explicit statement in the entire JoD about God being both male and female united. This is not quite Adam-God doctrine, but related "God consists of man and woman" theology.

2. **Plurality of Gods:** Orson Pratt's two sermons provide the clearest articulation of the doctrine that Saints would become individual gods, with millions of gods in eternity.

3. **Polygamy:** Multiple defenses, including Joseph F. Smith's extensive argument and Erastus Snow's sarcastic attack on Congress.

4. **Cosmology:** Extensive material on the material nature of God, pre-existence, and man's divine potential.

5. **Evolution:** Clear anti-Darwin stance by Erastus Snow.

**High song potential** for at least 3-4 songs from this volume alone.
