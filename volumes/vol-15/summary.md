## Executive Summary

Volume 15 is a **HIGH VALUE** volume for the Journal of Discords project. It contains extensive doctrinal content that has been subsequently disavowed, downplayed, or contradicted by the modern LDS Church, along with scientifically falsifiable claims.

**Key Strengths:**
- **Orson Pratt's Theological Speculation:** Detailed pre-existence doctrine, cosmology, and degrees of glory teachings now distanced from by modern LDS Church
- **Eternal Marriage Requirements:** Multiple strong statements that rejection of celestial/plural marriage results in damnation
- **Chronological Claims:** Testable (and falsified) claims about earth's age and Christ's birth date
- **Blood Atonement Reference:** Clear statement about LDS murderers being beyond forgiveness or prayer
- **Polygamy Defense:** Biblical arguments for polygamy now contradicted by church practice

**Primary Topics:** Pre-existence doctrine, eternal marriage requirements, cosmology/earth age, polygamy defense, blood atonement, prophecy

#### 1. Pre-existence of Our Spirits
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** December 15, 1872
- **Pages:** JoD 15:241-253
- **File:** `Pre-existence_of_Our_Spirits.html`
- **Flag Reason:** Comprehensive pre-existence doctrine with now-disavowed claims about literal heavenly parents, spirit begetting, and eternal family structure
- **Topics:** Cosmology, Theology, Authority, Gender
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★☆
- **Status:** Read in full

**Key Claims:**
- All humans existed as spirits "thousands of years ago" before mortal birth
- Spirits have "a literal father and a literal mother in the heavens" who begat them
- Memory of pre-existence intentionally withheld as part of mortal test
- Married Saints will beget spirit children eternally as gods
- Unmarried Saints become "angels" or servants, not gods
- Michael = Adam, "our first great progenitor"
- Patriarchal order continues eternally with children subject to fathers forever

**Why This Matters:**
- Modern LDS Church soft-pedals or denies Heavenly Mother doctrine details
- Claims about spirit begetting and eternal family structure are speculative
- Presents now-uncertain theology as revealed truth
- Creates theological problems about pre-mortal existence

**Apologetic Challenges:**
- How does modern church reconcile distance from this detailed doctrine?
- If this was revelation, why is it now not taught?
- Is Orson Pratt wrong, or has doctrine changed?

- **Song Potential:** ★★★★☆
- **Status:** Read in full

**Key Claims:**

- Unmarried persons ("old bachelors") forfeit exaltation, become servants/angels
- Those who reject eternal marriage covenant "shall not have forgiveness of sins in this world nor in the world to come"
- David suffered in hell "a thousand years at least" for his sins

**Why This Matters:**

- Eternal marriage presented as absolute salvation requirement
- Unmarried = eternal servant status
- Now contradicts modern LDS acceptance of single members

**Apologetic Challenges:**
- Is Orson Pratt teaching false doctrine about blood atonement?

#### 3. Patriarchal Marriage
- **Speaker:** George A. Smith
- **Date:** May 19, 1872
- **Pages:** JoD 15:27-34
- **File:** `Patriarchal_Marriage,_etc.html`
- **Flag Reason:** Comprehensive polygamy defense with biblical arguments, authority claims
- **Topics:** Polygamy, Authority, Biblical Interpretation
- **Song Potential:** ★★★☆☆
- **Status:** Read in full

**Key Claims:**
- Polygamy commanded by God through Joseph Smith
- "The Bible is a polygamous book, and no man can believe it without believing plurality of wives"
- New Testament does not forbid polygamy; Jesus would have rebuked it if sinful
- Isaiah 4:1 (seven women to one man) refers to holy times
- Anti-polygamy petitions are "unmitigated tissue of utter and absolute falsehoods"
- "Joseph Smith was a Prophet of God...every man and every woman who rejects it, rejects the truth, and will be responsible for it"

**Why This Matters:**
- Claims Bible requires/endorses polygamy
- Absolute prophetic authority claim for Joseph Smith
- Now contradicted by LDS excommunication of polygamists
- Historical irony: church now opposes what it once claimed was biblical requirement

**Apologetic Challenges:**
- If Bible is "polygamous book," why did church abandon practice?
- Are those who believe Bible obligated to accept polygamy?
- Was George A. Smith wrong about biblical polygamy?

#### 4. True Christmas and New Year
- **Speaker:** Orson Pratt
- **Date:** December 29, 1872
- **Pages:** JoD 15:253-265
- **File:** `True_Christmas_and_New_Year.html`
- **Flag Reason:** Chronological claims, earth age, Book of Mormon "proof," admission Joseph didn't understand own translation
- **Topics:** Chronology, Cosmology, Book of Mormon, Authority
- **Song Potential:** ★★★★★ (HIGHEST)
- **Status:** Read in full

**Key Claims:**
- Christ born April 11 (Thursday), not December 25
- Church organized exactly 1,800 years after crucifixion "to the very day"
- "This is something very marvelous...God commanded that boy to organize the Church on that day"
- **"Joseph Smith, being an unlearned man, never saw this to the day of his death; that is, he never understood it"** [undermines prophetic authority]
- Days of creation were 1,000-year periods, not 24 hours
- Earth created in six 1,000-year periods (6,000 years)
- Earth will exist temporally for 7,000 years total
- Book of Mormon proves Christ's age through longitude calculations

**Why This Matters:**
- Scientifically falsified claims about earth's age (6,000-7,000 years)
- Admits Joseph Smith didn't understand his own translation
- Specific date claims (April 11) presented as revelation
- "1,800 year miracle" based on incorrect chronology assumptions

**Apologetic Challenges:**
- Why didn't prophet Joseph understand his own translation?
- Are the chronological claims revelation or speculation?
- How to reconcile 7,000-year earth with scientific evidence?
- If these details are wrong, what else might be wrong?

**Song Development Note:** This sermon is the TOP candidate for immediate song development. Multiple angles: scientific falsification, prophetic authority undermining, chronological confidence in false claims.
