## Summary of Key Findings

### Polygamy (★★★★★ Sermons: 3)

**Sermon #34: "The Twin Relics, Slavery and Polygamy" (JoD 23:294-302)** by Erastus Snow
- Joseph Smith's proposal to abolish slavery through compensated emancipation (rejected by nation)
- Monogamy originated from Roman state, not Christianity
- Christ himself was "the fruit of polygamy" in the flesh
- Detailed defense distinguishing polygamy from bigamy and concubinage
- Ten Tribes prophecy and Book of Mormon connections
- **Song Potential:** Joseph Smith as prophet of social justice (slavery), monogamy as Roman invention

**Sermon #26: "The Marriage Question" (JoD 23:224-234)** by Erastus Snow
- Abraham's concubines were "associated wives" not mistresses
- New England accused of infanticide and "child murder"
- "Imprison and be damned" — direct defiance of federal law
- Utah has lower illiteracy and idiocy rates than US average
- Children of polygamy as future "saviors of America"
- **Song Potential:** Fiery rhetoric, accusation reversals, statistical claims

**Sermon #14: "Liberty of Conscience" (JoD 23:124-133)** by Wilford Woodruff
- Fall of Adam and Eve was "ordained of God"
- Emma Smith "gave her husband in marriage several women"
- Plural marriage commanded by God, sworn testimony
- Patriarchal marriage for eternal family kingdoms
- Denying wives = denying God
- **Song Potential:** Emma Smith's role, Adam's fall as preordained

## Notes

**Volume Context:** Volume 23 was published covering sermons from 1882, during the height of federal prosecution under the Edmunds Act (1882). This context explains the extremely defensive and combative tone regarding polygamy throughout the volume.

**Dominant Theme:** Polygamy defense comprises roughly 25% of the volume's content. Multiple sermons directly address federal legislation, public opinion, and religious opposition.
