**Mormon Reformation (1856-1857):** Volume 4 documents the peak of Mormon extremism in Utah. Multiple church presidents taught blood atonement doctrine from the pulpit during this period. The rhetoric preceded:
- Mountain Meadows Massacre (September 1857)
- Parrish-Potter murders (March 1857)
- Various violence against apostates
- Atmosphere of fear in 1850s Utah

**Key Timeline:**
- September 21, 1856: Brigham Young and Jedediah M. Grant deliver blood atonement sermons

- February 8, 1857: Brigham Young delivers second major blood atonement sermon
