## Research Notes

### Polygamy Defense Patterns

Volume 24 contains **exceptionally strong polygamy material** because:

1. **Timing:** Published during peak Edmunds Act enforcement (1882-1883)
2. **Defiance:** Leaders are explicitly rejecting any compromise
3. **Theological ultimatums:** Multiple speakers claim polygamy essential for exaltation
4. **Specific details:** Names Joseph Smith III as opponent, describes punishments for adultery
5. **No hedging:** Direct statements that non-compliance means damnation

### Failed Prophecy - Jackson County Return

**Orson Pratt's sermon (JoD 24:20-32)** contains:
- Detailed prophecy of LDS return to Jackson County, Missouri
- Purchase of land and establishment of New Jerusalem
- Temple with 24 named compartments in circular form
- Materials (sapphires, carbuncles, precious stones) that will never decay
- God's presence will preserve buildings eternally
- Light from city visible for miles, terrifying the wicked
- Predicted "by and by" and "not very far distant"

**Status in 2024:** Never happened. Church sold Jackson County properties. No return plans.
