1. **Polygamy Defense** - Orson Pratt's extensive defense of plural marriage
2. **Eternal Marriage Doctrine** - Temple ordinances and children born "illegitimate" outside eternal marriage
3. **Cosmology Claims** - Orson Pratt on future state powers and capacities
