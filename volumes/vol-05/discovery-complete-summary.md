## Executive Summary

Volume 5 (1857-1858) is the **most violent, most militant, and most theocratically extreme** volume of the Journal of Discourses. All 77 sermons delivered during the Utah War period, with concentrated rhetoric around the Mountain Meadows Massacre (September 11, 1857).

### Historical Context

- **Period:** July 1857 - November 1857
- **Crisis:** Utah War (federal troops sent to suppress Mormon rebellion)
- **Massacre:** Mountain Meadows (120 emigrants killed, September 11, 1857)
- **Leadership:** Brigham Young declares martial law, rejects federal authority
- **Outcome:** Troops winter at Fort Bridger, peace negotiated 1858
