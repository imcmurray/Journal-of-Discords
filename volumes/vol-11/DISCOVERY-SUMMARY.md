## CRITICAL Findings (★★★★★)

### 1. Polygamy Required for Exaltation
**Citation:** JoD 11:269
**Speaker:** Brigham Young
**Date:** August 19, 1866

> "The only men who become Gods, even the Sons of God, are those who enter into polygamy. Others attain unto a glory and may even be permitted to come into the presence of the Father and the Son; but they cannot reign as kings in glory, because they had blessings offered unto them, and they refused to accept them."

**Why CRITICAL:**
- Categorical statement: "The ONLY men"
- Creates three-tier salvation system
- Direct modern contradiction (monogamous couples CAN achieve exaltation now)
- Impossible to defend as "out of context"
- Central doctrine (exaltation is THE core LDS promise)

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ "The Only Men Who Become Gods"

---

### 2. Human Lifespan: 1000 Years to 25 Years
**Citation:** JoD 11:203
**Speaker:** Amasa M. Lyman
**Date:** April 5, 1866

> "The Bible tells us that men used to reach a longevity that extended to near a thousand years; this was near six thousand years ago... yet the statistics of to-day relating to the average life of the human race show that it extends to a fraction over a quarter of a century."

**Why CRITICAL:**
- **SCIENTIFICALLY FALSIFIED:** Actual 1866 US life expectancy ~40 years, not 25
- Modern life expectancy ~78 years = OPPOSITE trend
- Used to justify polygamy as necessity
- Falsifiable claim with clear data

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ "The Human Race Is Dying"

---

### 3. Polygamy Prevents Human Extinction
**Citation:** JoD 11:203
**Speaker:** Amasa M. Lyman
**Date:** April 5, 1866

> "I have learned to regard it as the world's great necessity—the great necessity of the race to-day, and it is God's greatest necessity in reference to the salvation of the world... there has been, and that there is still in existence, operating and producing its deadly effects, a system of physical degeneracy that is telling fearfully upon the history of the race... how narrow a margin of time is left for the continuation of our race on the earth before it becomes entirely extinct."

**Why CRITICAL:**
- "World's great necessity" = polygamy prevents extinction
- **FAILED PROPHECY:** Humanity thrived WITHOUT polygamy
- Human population grew exponentially after 1866
- Mormon polygamy discontinued 1890; humanity didn't die out
- Goal was humans living "until he attain to the age of a tree" (failed)

**Song Potential:** ★★★★★ "The Human Race Is Dying" (combined with #2)

### 5. Polygamy for Resurrection, Not This World
**Citation:** JoD 11:269 | **Speaker:** Brigham Young

> "What is this order for? It is for the resurrection; it is not for this world. I would not go across this bowery for polygamy, if it only pertained to this world. It is for the resurrection."

**Analysis:** Admits no earthly value. Contradicts apologetic claims about caring for widows.

### 6. Mary Had Two Husbands (God and Joseph)
**Citation:** JoD 11:268 | **Speaker:** Brigham Young

> "The man Joseph, the husband of Mary, did not, that we know of, have more than one wife, but Mary the wife of Joseph had another husband... That very babe that was cradled in the manger, was begotten, not by Joseph, the husband of Mary, but by another Being. Do you inquire by whom? He was begotten by God our heavenly Father."

**Analysis:** God literally physically fathered Jesus. Polyandry defense. Modern church avoids this.

---

### 7. Seven Women Per Man Prophecy (Failed)
**Citation:** JoD 11:270 | **Speaker:** Brigham Young

> "It is written in the Bible, 'and in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, we will eat our own bread, and wear our own apparel; only let us be called by thy name, to take away our reproach.' The Government of the United States do not intend that that prophecy shall be fulfilled, and the Lord Almighty means that it shall. Do you not think that the Lord will conquer?"

**Analysis:** Isaiah 4:1 as polygamy prophecy. "The Lord will conquer" but government won.

---

### 8. God Creates As We Create Children
**Citation:** JoD 11:122 | **Speaker:** Brigham Young

> "He created man, as we create our children; for there is no other process of creation in heaven, on the earth, in the earth, or under the earth, or in all the eternities, that is, that were, or that ever will be."

**Analysis:** Physical procreation is ONLY process of creation. God procreates sexually.

---

### 9. Martin Luther Approved Polygamy
**Citation:** JoD 11:126 | **Speaker:** Brigham Young

> "Philip, Landgrave of Hesse... wrote to the great reformer Martin Luther... anxiously imploring them to grant unto him the privilege of marrying a second wife... [Reformers replied:] 'So far do we approve it... for the gospel hath neither recalled nor forbid what was permitted in the law of Moses with respect to the marriage.'"

**Analysis:** Uses Protestant reformers to legitimize polygamy. Historically accurate but selective.

---

### 10. Polygamy Extends Marriage to All Women
**Citation:** JoD 11:205 | **Speaker:** Amasa M. Lyman

> "If all men and all women in a community were honorably married, you can readily understand one thing, that there would be no prostitution of women in that community, there would be an end of the corruption of man in that community, there would be no illegitimacy there."

**Analysis:** Polygamy as solution to prostitution. Assumes demographic imbalance.

---

### 11. Salvation Only Through Marriage
**Citation:** JoD 11:206 | **Speaker:** Amasa M. Lyman

> "We are men and women... if we are ever saved in heaven, we shall be saved as men and women... Women must be saved, if the task should devolve on a man to marry two or three of them."

**Analysis:** Marriage required for salvation. Men's duty to marry multiple women to save them.

---

### 12. Prince Christian's Morganatic Wife
**Citation:** JoD 11:261 | **Speaker:** Brigham Young

> "Prince Christian of Holstein, who has recently married one of the daughters of Victoria, Queen of England, has what is termed a morganatic wife in Germany, by whom he has several children, yet the first lady in Europe... accepts him as a suitable match for her youthful daughter."

**Analysis:** Real 1866 event. Uses European royal polygamy to defend Mormon practice.

---

### 13. Illicit Sex Tolerated, Plural Wives Persecuted
**Citation:** JoD 11:261 | **Speaker:** Brigham Young

> "Men may do as they please with women, have numerous children by them, and take as many liberties with them as if they were their wives, and yet not call them wives, and modern society smiles upon them. But whenever a man applies the sacred name of wife to the mother of his children, if he happen to have more than one, then the world professes to be wonderfully shocked at the idea."

**Analysis:** Fair point about Victorian hypocrisy. Mistresses tolerated, plural wives persecuted.

---

### 14. Every God Rules Over His Own Progeny
**Citation:** JoD 11:262 | **Speaker:** Brigham Young

> "Every man who is faithful and gets a salvation and glory, and becomes a king of kings and Lord of Lords, or a father of fathers, it will be by the increase of his own progeny. Our Father and God rules over his own children. Wherever there is a God in all the eternities possessing a kingdom and glory and power it is by means of his own progeny."

**Analysis:** Gods rule literal offspring. More wives = more children = bigger kingdom.
