### Exceptional Findings (★★★★★)

1. **"Making Devils" Doctrine** (JoD 8:181-182)
   - Brigham Young explicitly states endowments are given to create devils from apostates
   - "Do you query why we give endowments? It is to make devils of those who will deny the faith, for that is also necessary, as a host of devils will be needed."
   - PRIORITY 1 for song development
   - Category: Sons of Perdition / Temple Theology

2. **"I Shall Go to Jackson County"** (JoD 8:348-350)
   - Heber C. Kimball prophesies he will return to Jackson County with thousands
   - First-person prophecy, absolute statement, never fulfilled
   - Combined with "Have your rifles ready" for militant edge
   - PRIORITY 2 for song development
   - Category: Failed Prophecy

3. **Joseph Smith Could Save the Nation** (JoD 8:319-320)
   - Brigham claims Joseph could have prevented Civil War and national collapse
   - "The nation is ruined, and will crumble to pieces... Joseph would have saved it"
   - Nation still standing 164 years later
   - Category: Failed Political Prophecy / Messianism

4. **Divorce Fees as Revenue** (JoD 8:345-346)
   - Brigham admits charging for divorces "keeps me in spending money"
   - Monetizing temple ordinances (negatively) in polygamous context
   - PRIORITY 3 for song development
   - Category: Economic Practice / Temple Ordinances

### High-Value Findings (★★★★)

5. **Delegate from Each State at Joseph's Murder** (JoD 8:320)
   - Conspiracy claim with no historical evidence
   - Shows persecution complex

6. **Have Your Rifles Ready** (JoD 8:348)
   - Militant call to arms alongside Jackson County prophecy
   - "Keep your powder dry, and have your balls and duck-shots ready"

7. **Joseph Could Steal Horses Daily** (JoD 8:15-16)
   - Hypothetical: doctrine valid even if prophet commits daily crimes
   - Shows blind obedience culture
   - Separates prophet's behavior from doctrine's validity

8. **Sell Every House for Taxes** (JoD 8:344)
   - Authoritarian tax collection
   - "I would sell every improvement, every ox, cow, mule, horse..."

### Insights

- **Brigham's Candor:** Remarkably open about temple theology, economic practices, prophetic authority
- **Cosmic Planning:** "Making devils" shows view of apostasy as necessary function
- **Political Messianism:** Joseph presented as potential political savior, not just religious prophet
- **Economic Control:** Taxation, divorce fees, missionary restrictions show tight financial control
- **Failed Prophecies:** Jackson County return, nation "crumbling to pieces" both unfulfilled
