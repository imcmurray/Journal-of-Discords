## Research Summary

### Volume 14 Overview
- **Dates:** 1869-1872 (post-railroad completion)
- **Major Theme:** Polygamy defense in the face of federal opposition
- **Speakers:** Primarily Brigham Young, with significant content from George Q. Cannon, Orson Pratt, John Taylor, Lorenzo Snow

### Key Findings

#### Polygamy Content (MAJOR THEME)
Volume 14 contains extensive polygamy apologetics as federal pressure intensified:

1. **Defensive rhetoric:** Claims persecution was for "Bible belief," not polygamy
2. **"Saving women" narrative:** Contrasts polygamy (preserving life) with monogamy (destruction of life)
3. **Legal defiance:** Direct confrontation with anti-polygamy legislation
4. **Moral superiority claims:** "Pure and virtuous community"

1. **God Progression:** Lorenzo Snow's sermon (14:300-309) contains deification theology
2. **Celestial Marriage:** Extensive theological justification
